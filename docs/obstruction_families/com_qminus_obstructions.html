COM Q-Minus Obstructions

Name: COM Q-Minus Obstructions

Symbol:
\[\mathcal Q^-\]

Description Type: Parameterized

Definition:

The family consists of $Q_n^{-*}$ and $Q_n^{--}(m)$ for $n\ge4$ and $1\le m\le n$. Here $Q_n^{-*}$ deletes one cube vertex and one neighbor of its antipode; $Q_n^{--}(m)$ deletes an antipodal pair and any $m$ neighbors of one member of the pair.

Parameters:

Integers $n\ge4$ and, for $Q_n^{--}(m)$, $1\le m\le n$; cube automorphisms make the indicated choices isomorphic.

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Recognition:

Each member contains an antipodal subgraph that is not gated. No proper pc-minor contains a member of $\mathcal Q^-$ as a pc-minor.

References:

\cite[Lemma~5.1]{KnauerMarc20}

Proof Source:

Comments:

This infinite family is the pc-minor-minimal excluded family for COM tope graphs.
