Codimension-One Staircase q-Cap Contraction Obstructions

Name: Codimension-One Staircase q-Cap Contraction Obstructions

Symbol:
\[\{\operatorname{Pend}_f(\mathcal K_d^{\mathrm{st},\mathrm{qcap}},\varnothing)\}\]

Description Type: Parameterized

Definition:

Let $X=\{a,b,c,z,q\}\mathbin{\dot\cup}W$ and $U=W\mathbin{\dot\cup}\{z\}$. Delete $\{a\}$, $\{b\}$, $\{a,b\}$, $\{a,c\}$, the anchored subcube $\{\{a,b,c\}\cup T:T\subseteq U\}$, and its codimension-one q-cap $\{\{a,b,c,q\}\cup S:S\subseteq W\}$. The base is the complement $\mathcal K_d^{\mathrm{st},\mathrm{qcap}}$ in $2^X$; attach a new pendant coordinate $f$ at the empty endpoint.

Parameters:

$d=|X|\ge5$. The pendant family has $2^d-3\cdot2^{d-5}-3$ states.

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Canonical Form:

The coordinate $z$ distinguishes the codimension-one face $W$ of the anchored support $U$, and $q$ carries the added cap.

Recognition:

A construction certificate verifies the valid root $\{b,c\}$, the exact rooted-union holes at $\varnothing$ and $X$, and their repair sets $\{a,b,c\}$ and $U\cup\{q\}$.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/survey/main.tex?#prop:learning-space-staircase-q-cap-pendant-obstructions

Comments:

Every member is contraction-minimal outside learning-space graphs. No pc-minimality or completeness claim is made because learning-space graphs are not closed under convex restrictions.
