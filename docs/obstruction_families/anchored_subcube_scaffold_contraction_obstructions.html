Anchored Subcube-Scaffold Contraction Obstructions

Name: Anchored Subcube-Scaffold Contraction Obstructions

Symbol:
\[\{\operatorname{Pend}_f(\mathcal K_d^{\mathrm{an},\delta},\varnothing)\}\]

Description Type: Parameterized

Definition:

Let $X=\{a,b,q\}\mathbin{\dot\cup}U$ with $U\ne\varnothing$. For $\delta\in\{0,1\}$, delete $\{a\}$, $\{b\}$, every $\{a,b\}\cup T$ with $T\subseteq U$, and additionally $\{a,q\}$ when $\delta=1$. The base is the complement $\mathcal K_d^{\mathrm{an},\delta}$ of this deleted family in $2^X$; attach a new pendant coordinate $f$ at the empty endpoint.

Parameters:

$d=|X|\ge4$, $U\ne\varnothing$, and $\delta\in\{0,1\}$. The pendant family has $2^d-2^{d-3}-1-\delta$ states.

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Canonical Form:

The displayed coordinates distinguish the two singleton anchors $a,b$, the cap coordinate $q$, and the nonempty subcube support $U$; $\delta$ records the optional deleted state $\{a,q\}$.

Recognition:

A construction certificate verifies that the base has valid root $\{a,b,q\}$, that its complementary endpoints are the only critical pair used in the pendant criterion, and that their repair sets are $\{a,b,q\}$ and $U\cup\{q\}$ for $\delta=0$ or $U$ for $\delta=1$.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/survey/main.tex?#prop:learning-space-subcube-scaffold-pendant-obstructions

Comments:

Every member is contraction-minimal outside learning-space graphs. No pc-minimality or completeness claim is made because learning-space graphs are not closed under convex restrictions.
