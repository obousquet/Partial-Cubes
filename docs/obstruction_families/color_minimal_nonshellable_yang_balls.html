Color-Minimal Nonshellable Yang Balls

Name: Color-Minimal Nonshellable Yang Balls

Symbol:
\[\mathfrak B^{\mathrm{col}}\]

Description Type: Predicate

Definition:

For each $d\ge1$, take the irredundant ample families $X\subseteq Q_d$ whose Yang complex is a nonshellable constructible $(d-1)$-ball, while every link at one literal vertex and every induced deletion of one complete literal pair is shellable. Equivalently, their Alexander-dual full-transversal ideals have a $d$-linear resolution but no linear-quotient order, while all $3d$ one-color restriction and contraction images have linear quotients.

Parameters:

Dimension $d\ge1$, modulo permutations of the $d$ coordinate colors and independent swaps of the two literals in each color.

Ambient Class: Ample Classes

Canonical Form:

An orbit of an ideal $J\in\mathfrak B_d^{\mathrm{col}}$ under $C_2\wr S_d$; the minimal generators uniquely recover the irredundant cube embedding up to that action.

Recognition:

Check a linear resolution and failure of linear quotients for $J$, then check linear quotients for the two literal-retaining images and the color-contraction image in every coordinate. Equivalently, test nonshellability of the Yang ball and shellability of its $3d$ elementary color minors.

Comments:

This is an exact predicate parametrization of every ample pc-minimal obstruction to dismantlable ampleness. It is not asserted to be a finite list or a classification by elementary named graph families.
