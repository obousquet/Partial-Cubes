Antipodally Punctured Hypercubes

Name: Antipodally Punctured Hypercubes

Symbol:
\[\{Q_n^{--}:n\geq3\}\]

Description Type: Parameterized

Definition:

For $n\geq3$, choose an antipodal pair $x,\overline{x}$ in $Q_n$ and take the subgraph induced by $V(Q_n)\setminus\{x,\overline{x}\}$. Its isomorphism type is denoted $Q_n^{--}$.

Parameters:

Integer $n\geq3$; all choices of the deleted antipodal pair are equivalent under cube automorphisms.

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Canonical Form:

Up to partial-cube isomorphism there is one member $Q_n^{--}$ in each dimension $n\geq3$.

Recognition:

A certificate is an irredundant $n$-cube embedding whose complement consists of two ambient-antipodal vertices.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/daisy-cubes/sections/daisy_pc_minors_body.tex?#thm:periphery-free; PartialCubes:papers/cover-recursive/main.tex?#prop:punctured-cubes; PartialCubes:papers/survey/main.tex?#prop:cover-recursive-peripheral-antipodal-obstructions

Comments:

These are exactly the nonample members of both the complete daisy basis and the complete convex-treelike basis. They are periphery-free and pc-minimal outside each target class.

Peripheral Daisy Obstructions

Name: Peripheral Daisy Obstructions

Symbol:
\[O(P,R,A;\mathcal W)\]

Description Type: Generative

Definition:

Choose disjoint finite sets $P,R$ with $P\ne\varnothing$, a down-set $\mathcal W\subseteq2^R$, and a set $A\subseteq R$ such that $A$ and $R\setminus A$ are distinct inclusion-maximal members of $\mathcal W$. Form $O(P,R,A;\mathcal W)=(2^P\times\mathcal W)\setminus\{(P,A),(\varnothing,R\setminus A)\}$.

Parameters:

The displayed tuple must satisfy the down-set, nonempty cube-factor, complementary-maximal-pair, and irredundancy conditions in the coordinate normal-form theorem.

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Canonical Form:

Every daisy obstruction with a peripheral coordinate has this form. Once a peripheral coordinate and reduced roots are fixed, the parameters are uniquely recoverable; distinct presentations may still describe isomorphic graphs.

Recognition:

A certificate gives $(P,R,A,\mathcal W)$ and verifies downward closure, complementary inclusion-maximality, and the two deleted vertices. Equivalently, delete a suitably maximal ambient-antipodal pair from a daisy cube with at least two roots.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/daisy-cubes/sections/daisy_pc_minors_body.tex?#thm:peripheral-normal-form

Comments:

The single-root factor $\mathcal W$ may vary; this is a complete generative family rather than a finite list.

Ample pc-Minimal Periphery-Free Partial Cubes

Name: Ample pc-Minimal Periphery-Free Partial Cubes

Symbol:
\[\mathcal O_{\mathrm{per}}^{\mathrm{amp}}\]

Description Type: Predicate

Definition:

The finite ample partial cubes $O$ with no peripheral coordinate for which every proper pc-minor has a peripheral coordinate in every nontrivial pc-minor, equivalently every proper pc-minor belongs to $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{per}}$.

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Canonical Form:

Members are considered up to cube isomorphism. Together with the antipodally punctured hypercubes, these are all pc-minimal obstructions to $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{per}}$.

Recognition:

For an irredundant family on $X$, inspect every feasible state $\tau:X\to\{\mathsf K,\mathsf C,0,1\}$. The original state must be periphery-free and every other nontrivial state must expose a peripheral coordinate. Equivalently, at any chosen outer coordinate, the two layers and their union belong to $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{per}}$, while the all-retained layer state is the unique state in which every displayed coordinate retains defects in both orientations. The latter condition is computable from the minimal repair antichains. A direct implementation uses at most $4^{|X|}$ states.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/cover-recursive/sections/cover_recursive_peripheral_basis.tex?#thm:coordinate-state-basis; PartialCubes:papers/cover-recursive/sections/cover_recursive_peripheral_repair.tex?#thm:complete-rper-basis; PartialCubes:papers/cover-recursive/sections/cover_recursive_peripheral_repair.tex?#thm:static-repair-basis; PartialCubes:papers/cover-recursive/main.tex?#prop:obstruction-structure

Comments:

This family is exact but not reduced to a finite list. It includes the pentagonal obstruction and several infinite explicit subfamilies retained in the source paper.

Two-Control Directed-Cycle Obstructions

Name: Two-Control Directed-Cycle Obstructions

Symbol:
\[\{D_m:m\geq4\text{ even}\}\]

Description Type: Parameterized

Definition:

For an even integer $m\geq4$, orient the cycle $G_m$ consistently and form the two-control class $D_m=C_2(G_m)$. It has one private coordinate per cycle vertex and controls $0,1$. The empty private support has the full control square; a singleton support at a vertex in the left (respectively right) bipartition has the square with $00$ (respectively $11$) deleted; an oriented edge has the singleton control word $01$ from left to right and $10$ from right to left; every other private support has empty fiber.

Parameters:

Even integer $m\geq4$; $D_m$ has $m+2$ coordinates and $4m+4$ concepts.

Ambient Class: Convex-Treelike Partial Cubes

Canonical Form:

There is one member $D_m$ for each even cycle length $m\geq4$, up to coordinate permutations and independent bit flips. Distinct lengths have distinct cardinalities.

Recognition:

A construction certificate gives a consistently directed chordless even cycle. Equivalently within a two-control class $C_2(G)$, condition all private coordinates outside a shortest directed cycle to zero.

References:

[TwoControlEvidence]

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/coefficient-topology/two_control_graphs.tex?#thm:two-control-minors

Comments:

Every member is ample, dismantlable, finite convex-treelike, union-closed-orientable, and pc-minimal outside realizable ample classes. This is an infinite subfamily of the global obstruction basis; no global completeness or minimum-cardinality claim is made.

Ambient Class: Ample Classes

Punctured-Cube Pendant Contraction Obstructions

Name: Punctured-Cube Pendant Contraction Obstructions

Symbol:
\[\{\mathcal R_{d+1}(W,A)\}\]

Description Type: Parameterized

Definition:

Let $|X|=d\ge4$, let $W\subseteq X$ be a singleton, choose $A\subseteq X$ with $2\le|A\triangle W|\le d-2$, and introduce $e\notin X$. Set $\mathcal K_d^-(W)=2^X\setminus\{W\}$ and $\mathcal R_{d+1}(W,A)=\{A\}\cup\{K\cup\{e\}:K\in\mathcal K_d^-(W)\}$.

Parameters:

$d\ge4$ and Hamming distance $2\le t=|A\triangle W|\le d-2$; the family has $d+1$ active coordinates and $2^d$ states.

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Canonical Form:

Up to cube isometries fixing the missing singleton $W$, the pointed base type is determined by $t=|A\triangle W|$.

Recognition:

A construction certificate supplies the missing singleton, the complementary critical root pair, and the pendant coordinate. Every one-coordinate contraction is then checked by the root-repair criterion.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/learning-spaces/sections/punctured_cube_family.tex?#prop:learning-space-punctured-cube-pendant-obstructions

Comments:

Every member is contraction-minimal outside learning-space graphs. This does not assert pc-minimality because learning-space graphs are not closed under convex restrictions.

Critical-Pendant Chain Contraction Obstructions

Name: Critical-Pendant Chain Contraction Obstructions

Symbol:
\[\{\mathcal J_{d+1,j}^{\epsilon}\}\]

Description Type: Parameterized

Definition:

On $X_d=\{a,b_1,\ldots,b_{d-1}\}$, combine the prefix chain $C_i=\{b_1,\ldots,b_i\}$ with the full $a=1$ fiber except $\{a,b_j\}$, optionally add $\{b_2\}$, and attach a new pendant coordinate above this base as in the source formula.

Parameters:

$d\ge4$, $2\le j\le d-1$, and $\epsilon\in\{0,1\}$, with $\epsilon=1$ permitted only for $j\ge3$. The family has $d+1$ active coordinates and $2^{d-1}+d+\epsilon$ states.

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Canonical Form:

The source parameters record the missing fiber state, its location along the chain, and the optional extra state; no completeness claim for all contraction-minimal nonlearning partial cubes is made.

Recognition:

A construction certificate verifies the chain and punctured fiber, the two explicit endpoint defects, and root repair after every coordinate contraction.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/learning-spaces/sections/chain_family.tex?#prop:learning-space-critical-pendant-obstructions

Comments:

Every member is contraction-minimal outside learning-space graphs. The twelve-state member is an illustrative instance, not a minimum-order theorem.

Crossed-Subcube Pendant Contraction Obstructions

Name: Crossed-Subcube Pendant Contraction Obstructions

Symbol:
\[\{\mathcal Y_{d+1}^{\alpha,\beta}(U,V;p,q)\}\]

Description Type: Parameterized

Definition:

Let $X=\{a,b,p,q\}\mathbin{\dot\cup}U\mathbin{\dot\cup}V$. Delete from $2^X$ every $\{a\}\cup S$ and $\{a,b\}\cup S$ for $S\subseteq U$, and every $\{b\}\cup T$ and $\{a,b\}\cup T$ for $T\subseteq V$. If $\alpha=1$, also delete $\{a,p\}\cup S$ for $S\subseteq U$; if $\beta=1$, also delete $\{b,q\}\cup T$ for $T\subseteq V$. From the remaining learning space, attach a new pendant coordinate above every state while retaining the lone lower state $R=\{b,p\}\cup V$.

Parameters:

$d=|X|\ge4$; disjoint coordinate blocks $U,V$; and $\alpha,\beta\in\{0,1\}$. The resulting family has $d+1$ active coordinates and $2^d-(2+\alpha)2^{|U|}-(2+\beta)2^{|V|}+2$ states.

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Canonical Form:

The source records the ordered crossed blocks, the two distinguished free coordinates, and the two optional half-wing flags. At $U=V=\varnothing$, only $\alpha+\beta$ distinguishes the three pointed base types up to the stated identifications.

Recognition:

A construction certificate lists the two deleted wings, verifies the complementary endpoints, and checks that their exact contraction-repair sets cover all base coordinates.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/survey/main.tex?#prop:learning-space-crossed-subcube-pendant-obstructions

Comments:

Every member is contraction-minimal outside learning-space graphs. This is not a pc-minimal forbidden family because learning-space graphs are not closed under convex restrictions.
