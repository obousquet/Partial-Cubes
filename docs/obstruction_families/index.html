Antipodally Punctured Hypercubes

Name: Antipodally Punctured Hypercubes

Symbol:
\[\{Q_n^{--}:n\geq3\}\]

Description Type: Parameterized

Definition:

For $n\geq3$, choose an antipodal pair $x,\overline{x}$ in $Q_n$ and take the subgraph induced by $V(Q_n)\setminus\{x,\overline{x}\}$. Its isomorphism type is denoted $Q_n^{--}$.

Parameters:

Integer $n\geq3$; all choices of the deleted antipodal pair are equivalent under cube automorphisms.

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Canonical Form:

Up to partial-cube isomorphism there is one member $Q_n^{--}$ in each dimension $n\geq3$.

Recognition:

A certificate is an irredundant $n$-cube embedding whose complement consists of two ambient-antipodal vertices.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/daisy-cubes/sections/daisy_pc_minors_body.tex?#thm:periphery-free; PartialCubes:papers/cover-recursive/main.tex?#prop:punctured-cubes

Comments:

These are exactly the nonample members of both the complete daisy basis and the complete convex-treelike basis. They are periphery-free and pc-minimal outside each target class.

Peripheral Daisy Obstructions

Name: Peripheral Daisy Obstructions

Symbol:
\[O(P,R,A;\mathcal W)\]

Description Type: Generative

Definition:

Choose disjoint finite sets $P,R$ with $P\ne\varnothing$, a down-set $\mathcal W\subseteq2^R$, and a set $A\subseteq R$ such that $A$ and $R\setminus A$ are distinct inclusion-maximal members of $\mathcal W$. Form $O(P,R,A;\mathcal W)=(2^P\times\mathcal W)\setminus\{(P,A),(\varnothing,R\setminus A)\}$.

Parameters:

The displayed tuple must satisfy the down-set, nonempty cube-factor, complementary-maximal-pair, and irredundancy conditions in the coordinate normal-form theorem.

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Canonical Form:

Every daisy obstruction with a peripheral coordinate has this form. Once a peripheral coordinate and reduced roots are fixed, the parameters are uniquely recoverable; distinct presentations may still describe isomorphic graphs.

Recognition:

A certificate gives $(P,R,A,\mathcal W)$ and verifies downward closure, complementary inclusion-maximality, and the two deleted vertices. Equivalently, delete a suitably maximal ambient-antipodal pair from a daisy cube with at least two roots.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/daisy-cubes/sections/daisy_pc_minors_body.tex?#thm:peripheral-normal-form

Comments:

The single-root factor $\mathcal W$ may vary; this is a complete generative family rather than a finite list.

Ample pc-Minimal Periphery-Free Partial Cubes

Name: Ample pc-Minimal Periphery-Free Partial Cubes

Symbol:
\[\mathcal O_{\mathrm{per}}^{\mathrm{amp}}\]

Description Type: Predicate

Definition:

The finite ample partial cubes $O$ with no peripheral coordinate for which every proper pc-minor has a peripheral coordinate in every nontrivial pc-minor, equivalently every proper pc-minor belongs to $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{per}}$.

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Canonical Form:

Members are considered up to cube isomorphism. Together with the antipodally punctured hypercubes, these are all pc-minimal obstructions to $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{per}}$.

Recognition:

For an irredundant family on $X$, inspect every feasible state $\tau:X\to\{\mathsf K,\mathsf C,0,1\}$. The original state must be periphery-free and every other nontrivial state must expose a peripheral coordinate. A direct implementation uses at most $4^{|X|}$ states.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/cover-recursive/sections/cover_recursive_peripheral_basis.tex?#thm:coordinate-state-basis; PartialCubes:papers/cover-recursive/main.tex?#prop:obstruction-structure

Comments:

This family is exact but not reduced to a finite list. It includes the pentagonal obstruction and several infinite explicit subfamilies retained in the source paper.

Two-Control Directed-Cycle Obstructions

Name: Two-Control Directed-Cycle Obstructions

Symbol:
\[\{D_m:m\geq4\text{ even}\}\]

Description Type: Parameterized

Definition:

For an even integer $m\geq4$, orient the cycle $G_m$ consistently and form the two-control class $D_m=C_2(G_m)$. It has one private coordinate per cycle vertex and controls $0,1$. The empty private support has the full control square; a singleton support at a vertex in the left (respectively right) bipartition has the square with $00$ (respectively $11$) deleted; an oriented edge has the singleton control word $01$ from left to right and $10$ from right to left; every other private support has empty fiber.

Parameters:

Even integer $m\geq4$; $D_m$ has $m+2$ coordinates and $4m+4$ concepts.

Ambient Class: Convex-Treelike Partial Cubes

Canonical Form:

There is one member $D_m$ for each even cycle length $m\geq4$, up to coordinate permutations and independent bit flips. Distinct lengths have distinct cardinalities.

Recognition:

A construction certificate gives a consistently directed chordless even cycle. Equivalently within a two-control class $C_2(G)$, condition all private coordinates outside a shortest directed cycle to zero.

References:

[TwoControlEvidence]

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/coefficient-topology/two_control_graphs.tex?#thm:two-control-minors

Comments:

Every member is ample, dismantlable, finite convex-treelike, union-closed-orientable, and pc-minimal outside realizable ample classes. This is an infinite subfamily of the global obstruction basis; no global completeness or minimum-cardinality claim is made.
