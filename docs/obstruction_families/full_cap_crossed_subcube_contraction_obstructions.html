Full-Cap Crossed-Subcube Contraction Obstructions

Name: Full-Cap Crossed-Subcube Contraction Obstructions

Symbol:
\[\{\mathcal Y_{d+1}^{\mathrm{cap},\epsilon}(U,V;p,q)\}\]

Description Type: Parameterized

Definition:

On $X=\{a,b,p,q\}\mathbin{\dot\cup}U\mathbin{\dot\cup}V$, start with the crossed base having $\alpha=1,\beta=0$. Delete in addition the full cap $\mathcal P_0(U)=\{\{a,b,p\}\cup S:S\subseteq U\}$ when $\epsilon=0$, or $\mathcal P_1(U)=\{\{a,p,q\}\cup S:S\subseteq U\}$ when $\epsilon=1$. Attach a new pendant coordinate above the retained base, with lone lower endpoint $R=\{b,p\}\cup V$.

Parameters:

$d=|X|\ge4$ and $\epsilon\in\{0,1\}$; require $V\ne\varnothing$ when $\epsilon=0$. The pendant family has $2^d-4\cdot2^{|U|}-2\cdot2^{|V|}+2$ states.

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Canonical Form:

The source gives exact rooted-union hole sets and complementary repair sets; the extra condition $V\ne\varnothing$ in the zero-cap case is essential to the construction.

Recognition:

A construction certificate lists the deleted crossed wings and decoration, verifies the exact rooted-union hole sets at the complementary endpoints, and checks that their contraction-repair sets cover every base coordinate.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/survey/main.tex?#prop:learning-space-crossed-cap-pendant-obstructions

Comments:

Every member is contraction-minimal outside learning-space graphs. No pc-minimality or completeness claim is made because learning-space graphs are not closed under convex restrictions.
