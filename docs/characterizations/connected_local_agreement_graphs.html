Connected Local Agreement Graphs

Name: Connected Local Agreement Graphs

Class: Maximum Classes

Type: Algorithmic

Model: Sign System

Criterion:

Hypotheses:

Finite binary coordinates, $1\le r

Status: Established

Recognition Complexity:

For fixed $r$, polynomial in $N$: inspect $\binom{N}{r+2}$ graphs with $r+2$ vertices and compare at most $r$ signs per potential edge.
