Extremal-Generation Characterization

Name: Extremal-Generation Characterization

Class: Conditional Antimatroids

Type: Definitional

Model: Set Family

Criterion:

A finite family $\mathcal C\subseteq2^E$ contains $\varnothing$, is closed under intersections, and every $c\in\mathcal C$ is generated by its extremal elements, where $x\in c$ is extremal when $c\setminus\{x\}\in\mathcal C$ and a subset generates $c$ when $c$ is the least member of $\mathcal C$ containing it.

Hypotheses:

Finite ground set; graph statements identify coordinate reflections and suppress constant coordinates.

Status: Established
