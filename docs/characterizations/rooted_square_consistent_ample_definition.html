Rooted Square-Consistent Coupling Classes Definition

Name: Rooted Square-Consistent Coupling Classes Definition

Class: Rooted Square-Consistent Coupling Classes

Type: Definitional

Model: Set Family

Criterion:

For a finite nonempty ample $C\subseteq Q_X=\{0,1\}^X$, let $I(C)=\{(v,S):v\in C,\ Q(v,S)\subseteq C\}$, where $Q(v,S)$ fixes the coordinates outside $S$ at $v$. The matrix $M_C$ has one row per distinct positioned cube $B\subseteq C$ and entries $M_C(B,(v,S))=\mathbf1\{v\in B,\operatorname{dir}(B)\subseteq S\}$. Write $\mathcal A(C)=\{\mu\ge0:M_C\mu=\mathbf1\}$. A support selection chooses $R_v$ with $Q(v,R_v)\subseteq C$ at every $v\in C$. It is rooted if some $q\in C$ has $R_q=\varnothing$ and $R_v\cap\{i:v_i\ne q_i\}\ne\varnothing$ for every $v\ne q$. Its owner count on $B$ is $|\{v\in B:\operatorname{dir}(B)\subseteq R_v\}|$. There is one probability law on rooted selections with owner count one on every actual cube of dimension at most 2 in every sample, and whose mean $\mu(v,S)=\Pr(R_v=S)$ belongs to $\mathcal A(C)$.

Hypotheses:

Finite nonempty ample classes on the full specified ambient cube, including original constant coordinates. Empty complementary shores have the unique empty allocation. Ordinary witnesses are the integral points of the full native allocation system.

Status: Established
