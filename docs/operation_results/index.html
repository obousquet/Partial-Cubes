Partial Cubes Are Projection-Closed

Name: Partial Cubes Are Projection-Closed

Class: Partial Cubes

Operation: Coordinate Projection

Result Type: Closed Under

Status: Established

Statement:

Every coordinate projection of the set-family representation of a partial cube is again a partial cube.

Proof:

Deleting one coordinate contracts the corresponding Θ-class. A Θ-class contraction of a partial cube is a partial cube, and iterating gives projection along any finite coordinate set.

References:

[KnauerMarc20] (Section 2.1)

Partial Cubes Are Conditioning-Closed

Name: Partial Cubes Are Conditioning-Closed

Class: Partial Cubes

Operation: Coordinate Conditioning

Result Type: Closed Under

Status: Established

Statement:

Every nonempty coordinate conditioning of a partial cube is again a partial cube.

Proof:

Conditioning restricts the graph to one semicube. Semicubes of a partial cube are convex, hence isometric, and the restricted hypercube embedding remains isometric.

Partial Cubes Are pc-Minor-Closed

Name: Partial Cubes Are pc-Minor-Closed

Class: Partial Cubes

Operation: Partial-Cube Minor

Result Type: Closed Under

Status: Established

Statement:

Every pc-minor of a partial cube is a partial cube.

Proof:

Both elementary pc-minor steps preserve the class: a nonempty convex restriction is an isometric subgraph, and a Θ-class contraction is a partial cube. The conclusion follows by induction over the finite sequence.

Class: Daisy Cubes

Operation: Coordinate Conditioning

Result Type: Closed Under

Status: Established

Statement:

Every nonempty coordinate conditioning of a daisy-cube representation is again a daisy cube, after reorienting the retained section when necessary.

The Conditioning Closure of Learning Spaces Is the Convex-Geometry Ideal Class

Name: The Conditioning Closure of Learning Spaces Is the Convex-Geometry Ideal Class

Class: Learning-Space Graphs

Operation: Coordinate Conditioning

Result Type: Closure Equals

Target Class: Convex-Geometry Ideals

Status: Established

Statement:

Up to signed-coordinate equivalence and suppression of constants, $\operatorname{Cl}_c(\mathsf{Learn})=\mathsf{Icg}$.

Proof:

Every conditioning of a learning space is an upper ideal in its surviving orientation. Conversely, if $\mathcal C$ is an upper ideal of a learning space $\mathcal L$, then $(\mathcal L\times\{0\})\cup(\mathcal C\times\{1\})$ is a learning space whose conditioning at the marker value $1$ is $\mathcal C$.

Maximum Classes Are Projection-Closed

Name: Maximum Classes Are Projection-Closed

Class: Maximum Classes

Operation: Coordinate Projection

Result Type: Closed Under

Status: Established

Statement:

Every nonempty coordinate projection of a maximum class is maximum at its resulting VC dimension.

Proof:

For a split at one coordinate, write the two shores as $C_i^0,C_i^1$, their union as $U$, and their intersection as $I$. The VC dimensions of $U$ and $I$ are at most $d$ and $d-1$. Since $|C|=|U|+|I|$ and the corresponding Sauer bounds sum to the maximum size of $C$, equality holds in both bounds; in particular $U$ is maximum. Iterate over projected coordinates.

Proof Source:

Partial Cubes Satisfy the Factorwise Product Theorem

Name: Partial Cubes Satisfy the Factorwise Product Theorem

Class: Partial Cubes

Operation: Cartesian Product

Result Type: Closed Under

Status: Established

Statement:

For finite connected graphs $G,H$, the product $G\square H$ is a partial cube if and only if both $G$ and $H$ are partial cubes.

Proof:

Products of irredundant cube embeddings give the forward closure. Conversely, every factor layer is convex in the product, so a partial-cube product has partial-cube factors.
