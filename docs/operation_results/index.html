Partial Cubes Are Projection-Closed

Name: Partial Cubes Are Projection-Closed

Class: Partial Cubes

Operation: Coordinate Projection

Result Type: Closed Under

Status: Established

Statement:

Every coordinate projection of the set-family representation of a partial cube is again a partial cube.

Proof:

Deleting one coordinate contracts the corresponding Θ-class. A Θ-class contraction of a partial cube is a partial cube, and iterating gives projection along any finite coordinate set.

References:

Partial Cubes Are Conditioning-Closed

Name: Partial Cubes Are Conditioning-Closed

Class: Partial Cubes

Operation: Coordinate Conditioning

Result Type: Closed Under

Status: Established

Statement:

Every nonempty coordinate conditioning of a partial cube is again a partial cube.

Proof:

Conditioning restricts the graph to one semicube. Semicubes of a partial cube are convex, hence isometric, and the restricted hypercube embedding remains isometric.

Class: Partial Cubes

Operation: Partial-Cube Minor

Result Type: Closed Under

Status: Established

Statement:

Every pc-minor of a partial cube is a partial cube.

Proof:

Both elementary pc-minor steps preserve the class: a nonempty convex restriction is an isometric subgraph, and a Θ-class contraction is a partial cube. The conclusion follows by induction over the finite sequence.

Daisy Cubes Are Projection-Closed

Name: Daisy Cubes Are Projection-Closed

Class: Daisy Cubes

Operation: Coordinate Projection

Result Type: Closed Under

Status: Established

Statement:

Every coordinate projection of a daisy-cube representation is again a daisy cube.

Proof:

For a down-set $D$, projecting away coordinates preserves downward closure: if a projected set occurs, all of its subsets occur by projecting the corresponding subsets in $D$.

Daisy Cubes Are Conditioning-Closed

Name: Daisy Cubes Are Conditioning-Closed

Class: Daisy Cubes

Operation: Coordinate Conditioning

Result Type: Closed Under

Status: Established

Statement:

Every nonempty coordinate conditioning of a daisy-cube representation is again a daisy cube, after reorienting the retained section when necessary.

Proof:

For a down-set $D$ and coordinate $x$, the zero-section is a down-set. The one-section, after deleting $x$, is also a down-set because $B\subseteq A$ and $A\cup\{x\}\in D$ imply $B\cup\{x\}\in D$.

Learning-Space Graphs Are Projection-Closed

Name: Learning-Space Graphs Are Projection-Closed

Class: Learning-Space Graphs

Operation: Coordinate Projection

Result Type: Closed Under

Status: Established

Statement:

Every coordinate projection, equivalently every $\Theta$-class contraction, of a learning-space graph is again a learning-space graph.

Proof:

Projection preserves the empty state and union closure. Project a tight path and suppress its possible repeated vertex to obtain a tight path between the projected endpoints, so well-gradedness is preserved as well.

The Conditioning Closure of Learning Spaces Is the Convex-Geometry Ideal Class

Name: The Conditioning Closure of Learning Spaces Is the Convex-Geometry Ideal Class

Class: Learning-Space Graphs

Operation: Coordinate Conditioning

Result Type: Closure Equals

Target Class: Convex-Geometry Ideals

Status: Established

Statement:

Up to signed-coordinate equivalence and suppression of constants, $\operatorname{Cl}_c(\mathsf{Learn})=\mathsf{Icg}$.

Proof:

Every conditioning of a learning space is an upper ideal in its surviving orientation. Conversely, if $\mathcal C$ is an upper ideal of a learning space $\mathcal L$, then $(\mathcal L\times\{0\})\cup(\mathcal C\times\{1\})$ is a learning space whose conditioning at the marker value $1$ is $\mathcal C$.

The PC-Minor Closure of Learning Spaces Is the Convex-Geometry Ideal Class

Name: The PC-Minor Closure of Learning Spaces Is the Convex-Geometry Ideal Class

Class: Learning-Space Graphs

Operation: Partial-Cube Minor

Result Type: Closure Equals

Target Class: Convex-Geometry Ideals

Status: Established

Statement:

Up to signed-coordinate equivalence and suppression of constants, $\operatorname{Cl}_{pc}(\mathsf{Learn})=\mathsf{Icg}$.

Proof:

Projection preserves learning spaces, so every pc-minor is a conditioning of a projected learning space and lies in the conditioning closure. The reverse inclusion holds because every conditioning is a pc-minor. The exact conditioning closure is $\mathsf{Icg}$.
