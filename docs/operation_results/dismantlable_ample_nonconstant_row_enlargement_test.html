Exact Dismantlable-Ample Test for Nonconstant-Row Enlargement

Name: Exact Dismantlable-Ample Test for Nonconstant-Row Enlargement

Class: Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operation: Nonconstant-Row Enlargement

Result Type: Preserves Between

Target Class: Dismantlable Ample Classes

Status: Established

Statement:

For nested ample input data, $V(H,D)$ is dismantlable ample exactly when both $H$ and $D$ are dismantlable ample. Moreover $V(H,D)$ peels relatively to its enlarged $D$-row exactly when $H$ peels.

Proof:

Lift a peeling of $H$ while retaining the $D$-row: an old corner with a mate deletes one constant-row word, while a corner without a mate deletes its $10$ lift and then its constant lift. This leaves the $D$-row. Conversely reduction in the first new coordinate maps any such relative chain to a peeling of $H$. Appending a peeling of $D$ proves sufficiency, while the $D$ face and the $H$ reduction prove necessity.
