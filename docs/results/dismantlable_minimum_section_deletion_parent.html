Minimum-Section Deletions Have Controlled Dismantlable Parents

Name: Minimum-Section Deletions Have Controlled Dismantlable Parents

Classes:

  • Maximum Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes
  • Sections of Dismantlable Maximum Parents

Operations:

  • Concept Deletion

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

Let $M$ be maximum of rank $r$, and let a pattern $p$ on a $q$-coordinate block, $1\le q\le r$, have the smallest section $M[p]=\partial_JM=S$. Put $C=M\setminus(S\times\{p\})$. If $M,C\in\mathcal D_k$, then $C$ has a rank-$r$ maximum parent in $\mathcal D_{\max(k,r-1)}$ with exactly $r+1-q$ auxiliaries, optimal among rank-$r$ parents. If both inputs are dismantlable, then $C$ has a dismantlable maximum parent.

Status: Established

Proof:

Choose a compatible maximum predecessor of the minimum section and run the prescribed-section recursion, which keeps both the predecessor and its punctured form in level $\max(k,r-1)$. One auxiliary replacement lowers the marked minimum rank by one while retaining a level-bounded decomposition of both the parent and its deleted section. After $r-q+1$ repetitions the marked minimum is empty and the zero mark recovers $C$. The fixed-rank auxiliary lower bound from the minimum-section theorem proves optimality.
