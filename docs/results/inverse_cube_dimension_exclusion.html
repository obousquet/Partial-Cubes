Cube-Dimension Exclusion for Proper Minors of an Expansion

Name: Cube-Dimension Exclusion for Proper Minors of an Expansion

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Partial-Cube Minor
  • Full Reduction

Result Type: Implication

Statement:

Under the marked-reduction hypotheses, every forbidden proper pc-minor $H$ of $X$ has an active coordinate $f$ such that $\operatorname{VC}(H^{\{f\}})\leq\operatorname{VC}(O)$. Hence, if $\operatorname{VC}(O)\leq d$, no ample forbidden class in which every active coordinate lies in a cube of dimension at least $d+2$ can be a proper pc-minor of $X$. In particular, overlap VC dimension at most three excludes every member of the 1364-vertex maximum-pair rectangular obstruction family.

Status: Established

Proof:

Choose $f$ to be the surviving marked coordinate. The marked reduction theorem identifies $H^{\{f\}}$ with the pc-minor $\nu O$, so its VC dimension is at most that of $O$. A cube of dimension at least $d+2$ through $f$ would reduce to a cube of dimension at least $d+1$, contradicting this bound. In the rectangular family every coordinate belongs to a five-cube: combine a three-cube in its maximum rank-three factor with a two-cube in the opposite rank-two factor.

Comments:

The rectangular exclusion uses only the product structure, independently of the signed realization of the factors.
