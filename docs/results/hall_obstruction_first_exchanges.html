The Hall Obstruction and Its Four First Nonpeelable Exchanges

Name: The Hall Obstruction and Its Four First Nonpeelable Exchanges

Classes:

  • Maximum Classes
  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes
  • Cornerless Ample Classes

Operations:

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Hall's 299-concept maximum VC-dimension-three class $C_H\subseteq Q_{12}$ is an ample forbidden pc-minor for dismantlable ampleness. It has exactly 16 ample one-concept extensions: 12 are corner-peelable with four corners, while four are nonpeelable with exactly two corners. The latter have (adjoined concept, other corner) equal to $(373,1011)$, $(1105,192)$, $(1360,3120)$, and $(1525,252)$. For each pair $(a,c)$, both $C_H\cup\{a\}$ and $(C_H\setminus\{c\})\cup\{a\}$ are ample forbidden pc-minors; the exchanged 299-concept family is cornerless and has the same shattered complex as $C_H$. The four exchanged families are pairwise nonisomorphic and none is isomorphic to $C_H$.

Status: Established

Proof:

The cited construction makes $C_H$ maximum of VC dimension three and cornerless, hence ample and nonpeelable. Exact evaluation of its 24 signed restrictions and 12 contractions peels every elementary pc-minor, proving forbiddenness. Exhausting all 3,797 absent concepts and testing isometry, Sandwich equality, corners, and peelability gives exactly 16 ample extensions and the stated 12+4 split. The one-concept obstruction theorem makes each nonpeelable extension forbidden. In every exceptional extension, deleting the added corner returns $C_H$, so deleting its only other corner also remains nonpeelable; the same-shadow exchange criterion preserves the shattered complex. Direct corner and elementary-minor audits prove that each exchange is cornerless and forbidden, while canonicalization under coordinate permutations and independent reversals distinguishes all five source types.
