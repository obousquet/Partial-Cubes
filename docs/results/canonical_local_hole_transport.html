Canonical Transport and Reconstruction of Local Holes

Name: Canonical Transport and Reconstruction of Local Holes

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Punctured Expansion Profile

Operations:

  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Coordinate Projection

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

For an ample $H$ and coordinate $e$, conditioning transports the canonical local minimal nonfaces at a retained vertex by discarding exactly those supports containing $e$. A support $A$ is a canonical local hole at a vertex of the contraction exactly when its side data $\varepsilon_{H,e}(z,A;\cdot)$ is a punctured expansion profile. In every switched profile, the double-fibre set $D$ and exclusive sets $Z_0,Z_1$ are nonempty, no punctured-cube edge joins the two exclusive sets, every component outside $D$ is monochromatic, $D$ is ample, and $\operatorname{Sh}(D\cup Z_0)\cap\operatorname{Sh}(D\cup Z_1)=\operatorname{Sh}(D)$. Conversely these conditions construct an ample two-layer family with the prescribed switched contraction hole.

Status: Established

Proof:

Conditioning takes an induced subcomplex of the local cube-support complex. For contraction, an $A$-cube exists exactly when every profile value is nonempty, giving the minimal-hole equivalence. Oppositely coloured adjacent exclusive vertices would have no length-two path between their lifts, contradicting isometry; connectivity then forces a nonempty separator. Restricting to the profile coordinates gives a two-layer ample family whose union is the punctured cube and whose intersection is $D$. The exact two-layer shadow and order identities force $D$ to be ample and its shadow to equal the intersection of side shadows. Conversely those identities give Sandwich equality for the constructed two-layer family and hence ampleness and the desired canonical hole.

Comments:

The theorem explains why piecewise images of source one-hole covers are insufficient after contraction: switched holes can be assembled jointly by the two sections.
