Component-Label Criterion for Lifting a Repairing Addition

Name: Component-Label Criterion for Lifting a Repairing Addition

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Fiber Product
  • Peripheral Expansion

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $\varnothing\ne D\subseteq C$ be ample, let $K_1,\ldots,K_k$ be the components of $C\setminus D$, and suppose $A=C\cup\{d\}$ is ample. Lift each $K_i$ to layer $t_i$, double $D$, and lift $d$ to layer $b$. If $I(d)$ indexes the components containing a neighbour of $d$, the lift is ample exactly when $t_i=b$ for all $i\in I(d)$. Then the lifted repair is a corner with the same acquired support as $d$ over $C$. If additionally $A,D$ are corner-peelable but the ample lift is not, some untouched component $j\notin I(d)$ has label $1-b$. Thus a repair meeting every component cannot yield an ample forbidden lift when the repaired contraction and reduction are positive.

Status: Established

Proof:

After adjoining $d$, it merges exactly the touched components of $C\setminus D$. The component-sign parametrization of ample expansions forces, and is forced by, equality of their lift labels with the label of $d$. All old neighbours of the lifted repair then remain in its layer or lie over the doubled reduction, so its incident and acquired supports are unchanged. If no untouched opposite-layer component exists, the lift is the peripheral expansion $(A\times\{b\})\cup(D\times\{1-b\})$, which is corner-peelable when both $A$ and $D$ are.
