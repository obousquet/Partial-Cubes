Signed Missing-Pattern Polarization Characterizes Ampleness

Name: Signed Missing-Pattern Polarization Characterizes Ampleness

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Maximum-Generated Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Projection
  • Coordinate Conditioning

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

For a nonempty binary class $C$ over any field, ampleness is equivalent to the paired differences $x_{i,1}-x_{i,0}$ forming a regular sequence on the signed minimally-missing-pattern ring $S/Q_C$ in some coordinate order, and equivalently in every order. In that case $Q_C$ is a two-fold polarization of the Stanley--Reisner ideal of $\operatorname{Sh}(C)$. The condition is preserved by projection and nonempty conditioning and cannot separate ample classes from maximum sections.

Status: Established

Proof:

For an ample class, after any earlier pair identifications a monomial annihilated by the next paired difference would encode a shattered set in a nonempty conditioning but forbid both values of the next coordinate. A contained cube for that shattered set fixes one of those values and contradicts the annihilation, proving regularity in every order. Conversely, identifying all pairs sends $Q_C$ to the Stanley--Reisner ideal of $\operatorname{Sh}(C)$. The multigraded Hilbert-series identity for a regular sequence equates the coefficient of $t_1\cdots t_n$ on the two sides; those coefficients are $|C|$ and $|\operatorname{Sh}(C)|$. Equality is ampleness by the Sandwich theorem.

Comments:

The homogeneous differences differ from the affine forms $x_{i,0}+x_{i,1}-1$, which are regular for every neural ideal. The theorem does not claim that $S/Q_C$ is Cohen--Macaulay. The same Koszul reduction makes the coordinate-graded Tor algebra of $S/Q_C$ depend only on the shattered complex.
