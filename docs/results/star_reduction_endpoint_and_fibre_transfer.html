Star Reductions Preserve Positivity and Every Attainable Endpoint

Name: Star Reductions Preserve Positivity and Every Attainable Endpoint

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Projection
  • Coordinate Fiber Product
  • One-Point Amalgam

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $X$ be ample with active coordinate $e$ and suppose the reduction $D=X^{\{e\}}$ has a star as its one-inclusion graph, including a singleton or edge. Then $X$ is corner-peelable exactly when $\pi_eX$ is. For every $a\in X$, $X$ peels to $a$ exactly when the contraction peels to $\pi_ea$; for every $z\in D$, $X$ peels to the whole two-point fibre over $z$ exactly when the contraction peels to $z$. Consequently a coordinate edge with an attainable endpoint that cannot be retained has at least four edges, and equality forces a four-vertex-path reduction. No active reduction of an ample pc-minimal obstruction or rooted factor is a star.

Status: Established

Proof:

Induct on the number of star edges. Exclusive contraction corners lift directly; a leaf corner can be cleared in the layer opposite its common external-component label. If the centre appears first, its corner cube separates all remaining complement components into a core and one-vertex attachments on disjoint coordinate blocks. A USO of the core selects a leaf whose attachments peel to it, after which one lift of that leaf can be removed and induction applies. The same induction while retaining a chosen fibre proves relative transfer. Endpoint transfer follows by gluing two copies at a hypothetical failing endpoint and applying ordinary star closure after contracting one copy. The size-four conclusion uses the classification of ample families on at most four vertices.
