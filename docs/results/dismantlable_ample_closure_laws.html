Dismantlable-Ample Closure Laws

Name: Dismantlable-Ample Closure Laws

Classes:

  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Projection
  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Partial-Cube Minor
  • Full Reduction
  • Cartesian Product
  • Peripheral Expansion

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Dismantlable ample classes are closed under nonempty convex restrictions, coordinate projections, pc-minors, strong projections, Cartesian products, cube symmetries, and specified peripheral expansions whose base and site are dismantlable ample. They are not closed under arbitrary induced or isometric subgraphs or under ambient-family complement.

Status: Established

Proof:

Ample-prefix orders restrict to coordinate faces and project by first appearance. Product vertices ordered by the sum of factor-order indices give isometric prefixes. For a peripheral expansion, concatenate a base order with a site order in the new layer. Conversely, factors, bases, and sites arise as convex restrictions or contractions. The cube $Q_3$ contains the isometric induced $C_6$, which is not ample; Hall's class and its dismantlable complement disprove complement closure.
