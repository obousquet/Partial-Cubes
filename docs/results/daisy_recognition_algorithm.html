Daisy Cubes Are Recognizable with a Proper Labelling in Quadratic Time

Name: Daisy Cubes Are Recognizable with a Proper Labelling in Quadratic Time

Classes:

  • Daisy Cubes

Result Type: Algorithm

Statement:

An arbitrary $n$-vertex graph can be tested for being a daisy cube in $O(n^2)$ time. On acceptance, the algorithm also outputs an isometric hypercube labelling whose label family is a down-set.

Status: Established

Proof:

First apply the quadratic partial-cube recognition algorithm to recover the intrinsic $\Theta$-coordinates. For every $\Theta$-class, compute both semicubes and their boundaries and require one boundary to equal its semicube. Orient that peripheral semicube as the 1-side. Crossing any present 1-coordinate gives its 0-neighbor, so repeated crossings prove that the resulting labels form a down-set. The recognition and output scans remain quadratic.
