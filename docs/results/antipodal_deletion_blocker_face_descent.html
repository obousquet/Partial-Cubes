Antipodal Deletion Blockers Descend Along Their Faces

Name: Antipodal Deletion Blockers Descend Along Their Faces

Operations:

  • Concept Deletion

Result Type: Implication

Statement:

Suppose $v\in A\subseteq Q_X$ and $A\cap Q_J(v)=Q_J(v)\setminus\{h\}$, where $h=v^{\oplus J}$ and $|J|\ge2$. For every proper $S\subset J$, putting $w=v^{\oplus S}$ gives $A\cap Q_{J\setminus S}(w)=Q_{J\setminus S}(w)\setminus\{h\}$. If $|J\setminus S|\ge2$, then $A\setminus\{w\}$ is nonample.

Status: Established

Proof:

The vertices of $Q_{J\setminus S}(w)$ are $v^{\oplus(S\cup T)}$ for $T\subseteq J\setminus S$; all belong to the original punctured $J$-face except the unique choice $T=J\setminus S$, which gives $h$. After deleting $w$, the complement on this subface is the antipodal pair $\{w,h\}$. In dimension at least two that pair is nonample, and conditioning together with complement duality transfers the failure to $A\setminus\{w\}$.
