External Section Types around a Marked Four-Cube

Name: External Section Types around a Marked Four-Cube

Classes:

  • Maximum Classes
  • Cornerless Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Coordinate Projection
  • Full Reduction

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $X\subseteq Q_{\{1,2,3,4\}\sqcup J}$ be a cornerless maximum class of VC dimension three. Suppose its zero external face is $Q_4\setminus\{7\}$ and the marked vertices $(0,0),(1,0),(8,0)$ have no neighbours in directions from $J$. For each $j\in J$, the first-four-coordinate projection of the section $x_j=1$ is exactly $Q_4\setminus\{0,1,3,7,8\}$ or $Q_4\setminus\{0,1,5,7,8\}$. If $A,B$ are the coordinates of these two types, then $|A|,|B|\ge2$; the fibre over pattern 3 uses only $B$, and that over pattern 5 uses only $A$. Thus for $|J|=7$ the type sizes are $2+5$ or $3+4$, up to symmetry. If $|B|=2$, the pattern-3 fibre is the full $B$-square and the fibres over 2 and 11 contain that square; symmetrically, the same holds for patterns 5, 4, and 13 when $|A|=2$. These are necessary conditions, not an existence or sufficiency result.

Status: Established

Proof:

Projection to the marked four coordinates is the fifteen-vertex face, since realizing pattern 7 would shatter four coordinates. Each marked fibre is an ample connected coordinate face whose zero vertex is isolated, hence is a singleton. Projecting additionally to an external coordinate gives a 26-word maximum rank-three class; its one-section is therefore an eleven-word maximum rank-two class. Connectivity of its five-word complement forces its extra omitted pattern to be 3 or 5. The type and fibre-support conclusions follow. Minimum degree four at the base vertices over patterns 3 and 5 forces at least two coordinates of each type. When one type has size two, cornerlessness at every nonzero square vertex forces the whole square and its two possible horizontal neighbours, yielding the final containments.
