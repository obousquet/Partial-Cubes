Feasible Uncounted Two-Unit Profiles Are Pair-Rich

Name: Feasible Uncounted Two-Unit Profiles Are Pair-Rich

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

For a two-unit holonomy wing with lower family $K$ of order at most nine, the three profiles having an uncounted lower vertex behave as follows. The $\eta=2$ profile has the two-apex $P_7$ normal form and both terminal pairs are acquired; the $\eta=1$ profile with two two-concept shores meeting in one concept is impossible; and the $\eta=1$ profile with disjoint shores of orders two and three has $|L|=1$, $|K|=9$, and an acquired terminal pair from each shore. In every feasible case the two acquired pairs are disjoint.

Status: Established

Proof:

In the $\eta=2$ profile, the terminal pairs from the two-apex normal form are disjoint, so the disjoint-shore terminal-square lemma makes both acquired. In the intersecting $\eta=1$ profile, exact counting gives a singleton active slice and one uncounted vertex; deleting it leaves a square-free shared-owner eight-cycle. The unicyclic square-link theorem forces this cycle to be the subdivision of a four-cycle link, whose direction sequence has no direction on opposite edges, whereas the shore provenance puts every direction on opposite edges. For the disjoint $2$--$3$ profile, exact counting again gives $|L|=1$ and $|K|=9$; each shore contains an active edge, the disjoint-shore lemma makes both terminal pairs acquired, and the one-uncounted direction lemma makes them disjoint.
