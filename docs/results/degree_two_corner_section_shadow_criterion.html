Four Shadow Conditions Characterize a Marked Degree-Two Corner

Name: Four Shadow Conditions Characterize a Marked Degree-Two Corner

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Shattered Complex

Operations:

  • Coordinate Fiber Product

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let ample $A,D,C\subseteq Q_F$ contain $0$, put $U=A\cup D\cup C$, and set $Y=\{(0,00)\}\cup(A\times\{01\})\cup(D\times\{10\})\cup(C\times\{11\})$. Then $Y$ is ample exactly when (i) $\operatorname{Sh}(U)=\operatorname{Sh}(A)\cup\operatorname{Sh}(D)\cup\operatorname{Sh}(C)$, (ii) $\operatorname{Sh}(A\cup C)=\operatorname{Sh}(A)\cup\operatorname{Sh}(C)$, (iii) $\operatorname{Sh}(D\cup C)=\operatorname{Sh}(D)\cup\operatorname{Sh}(C)$, and (iv) $\operatorname{Sh}(A)\cap\operatorname{Sh}(D)\subseteq\operatorname{Sh}(C)$. Every ample family with a marked degree-two corner has this presentation after translating that corner to zero, uniquely up to exchanging its two directions and the side sections $A,D$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Sorting shattered supports by the two new coordinates gives $|\operatorname{Sh}(Y)|=|\operatorname{Sh}(U)|+|\operatorname{Sh}(A)\cap\operatorname{Sh}(D\cup C)|+|\operatorname{Sh}(D)\cap\operatorname{Sh}(A\cup C)|+1$. If $Y$ is ample, its two adjacent coordinate faces force conditions (ii) and (iii). Subtracting $|Y|$ then expresses the shadow excess as the sum of two nonnegative terms: the excess of $\operatorname{Sh}(U)$ over the union of the three shadows, and $|(\operatorname{Sh}(A)\cap\operatorname{Sh}(D))\setminus\operatorname{Sh}(C)|$. Equality forces (i) and (iv), and conversely the four conditions make the excess zero. For recovery, fixing both corner directions at the corner value leaves a connected ample face in which the marked vertex is isolated, so that face is a singleton; the other three sections are then uniquely determined.
