Interval Gaps and First Syzygies

Name: Interval Gaps and First Syzygies

Classes:

  • Partial Cubes

Invariants:

  • Interval Gap
  • Rounded Resolution-Slope Defect
  • Ordering Width

Operations:

  • Partial-Cube Minor
  • Cartesian Product
  • Common-Section Gluing

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

The first Betti multiplicity at a face label $u_F$ is one exactly when $C\cap F$ is an antipodal pair in $F$, and is zero otherwise. Hence $g(C)=\max(\{j-n-1:\beta_{1,j}(J_C)\neq0\}\cup\{0\})\leq\nu_k(C)\leq\sigma(C)$. The zero set of $g$ is exactly the isometric cube embeddings. Moreover, $g$ is pc-minor monotone and takes maxima under Cartesian products and common-section gluings satisfying the projection hypotheses.

Status: Established

Proof:

First Betti multiplicity is reduced zero-dimensional homology of the face complex. Its graph joins every non-antipodal pair and is disconnected exactly for a two-vertex antipodal trace. Empty intervals survive lifting through projection or conditioning, while a product pair varying in both factors has a mixed intermediate point. In a common-section union, pairs from different pieces have a base lift strictly between them, so every empty interval lies in one piece.
