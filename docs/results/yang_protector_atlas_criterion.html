Colored Yang Protector-Atlas Criterion

Name: Colored Yang Protector-Atlas Criterion

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Concept Deletion

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

For an ample $H$, associate to every latent punctured face $(y,S)$ its punctured cross-polytopal chart in the Yang complex, with antipodal facet $A(y,S)=F_{y^S}$, protector facets $P_q(y,S)=F_{y^{S\setminus\{q\}}}$, and, when $y\in H$, escape facet $E(y,S)=F_y$. In every chart choose either the precedence $A(y,S)\prec E(y,S)$ or one precedence $P_q(y,S)\prec A(y,S)$. Then the Yang complex is shellable exactly when the local precedences can be chosen with acyclic union. Whenever they are acyclic, every linear extension is the Yang-facet order corresponding to a corner peeling of $H$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Under the facet--concept bijection, the escape precedence is exactly the latent-block escape arc and each protector precedence is exactly its facet-protector arc. The finite facet-protector acyclicity criterion characterizes corner peelability, and the Yang shellability--corner-peeling dictionary identifies those topological orders with shelling orders.
