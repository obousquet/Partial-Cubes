Terminal Edge-Product Attachments Have an Exact Reduction Test

Name: Terminal Edge-Product Attachments Have an Exact Reduction Test

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Common-Section Gluing
  • Cartesian Product
  • Full Reduction
  • Partial-Cube Minor

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

Let $(R,0)$ be a rooted factor and let nonempty ample peelable $D\subseteq A$ satisfy the relative-attachment forbiddenness criterion, with $D$ an edge in direction $e$ and $r=\pi_eD$. Put $X=P_R(A,D)=(A\times\{0_F\})\cup(D\times R)$. Then every nonempty coordinate reduction of $X$ is dismantlable ample exactly when $A^e$ peels to $r$. There is a certified $289$-concept choice of $A\subseteq Q_{12}$ with $D=\{0,2\}$ satisfying this condition. For that input, every rooted factor $R$ produces a forbidden pc-minor of order $2|R|+287$ in $12+\dim R$ active coordinates, all of whose nonempty reductions are dismantlable ample. Every output has minimum degree at least three; if the root is the only corner of $R$, the output is cornerless.

Status: Established

Proof:

For every old direction other than $e$, the reduction is $A^f\times\{0_F\}$; for every direction of $R$, it is $D\times R^f$. These are positive by reduction and product closure. In direction $e$, the reduction is $(A^e\times\{0_F\})\cup(\{r\}\times R)$, a one-point amalgam of two positive pieces. Since $R$ cannot peel to its root, the vertex-gluing criterion makes this reduction positive exactly when $A^e$ peels to $r$. Forbiddenness follows from the relative-attachment minimality theorem. For the fixed input, stored relative orders verify every required elementary image and a separate order makes $A^e$ peel to $r$. Minimum degree follows because $A$ has minimum degree three and the two-connected rooted factor has minimum degree two; vertices in $D\times R$ gain the edge direction of $D$. When the root is the factor's only corner, all nonroot missing-cube witnesses survive and mixed absent squares destroy the remaining possible corner at the root.
