Two-Unit Wings Reduce Cross-Wing Comparisons to Pair-Rich Support Graphs

Name: Two-Unit Wings Reduce Cross-Wing Comparisons to Pair-Rich Support Graphs

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Result Type: Implication

Statement:

Let $H=(A_0\times\{0\})\cup(A_1\times\{1\})$ be ample, put $B=A_0\cap A_1$, and suppose $|B|\le9$. If $B\subsetneq A_0$ has two-cycle holonomy of excess two, it has two disjoint acquired direction pairs. If the opposite wing is sharp, has the uncounted $\eta=1$ one-unit form, or has any feasible two-unit profile, then the two wings either supply three pairwise disjoint acquired pairs or their four acquired pairs form an alternating $C_4$, a four-edge path, or two disjoint two-edge paths. The only remaining apparent pair-poor profile is the one-unit extra-owner wing with disjoint shores of orders two and three.

Status: Established

Proof:

The uncounted two-unit classification excludes its intersecting profile and supplies two disjoint pairs in the other uncounted profiles; the vertex-free classification excludes its intersecting profile and does the same for every remaining profile. Sharp wings and uncounted one-unit wings also provide two disjoint acquired pairs. The opposite-wing support-graph theorem applied to either pair-rich comparison gives a three-edge matching or exactly one of the three alternating support graphs. The one-unit anomaly trichotomy and exclusion of the shared-owner branch leave only the extra-owner form outside this reduction.
