Removing a Uniform Inactive Coordinate Preserves Relative Peeling

Name: Removing a Uniform Inactive Coordinate Preserves Relative Peeling

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Concept Deletion

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Let $\varnothing\ne O\subsetneq B\subseteq Q_F$ be ample. Suppose every concept of $B\setminus O$ has value $b$ in coordinate $j$ and $O_j^b$ is nonempty. If $j$ belongs either to every relative shattered support in $\mathcal T=\operatorname{Sh}(B)\setminus\operatorname{Sh}(O)$ or to none of them, then restriction to the $j=b$ section preserves exactly the relative corner-deletion orders. Its relative supports are $\{S\setminus\{j\}:S\in\mathcal T\}$ without repetition, and deleting $j$ preserves the residual graph and all other edge labels.

Status: Established

Proof:

Write $A=O_j^b$, $D=B_j^b$, and $C=O_j^{1-b}=B_j^{1-b}$. The two-section shadow identity shows that each support in $\operatorname{Sh}(D)\setminus\operatorname{Sh}(A)$ contributes exactly one relative support, containing $j$ precisely when it also lies in $\operatorname{Sh}(C)$. If no relative support contains $j$, cardinality equality shows that no residual concept has a $j$-neighbor, so every section corner lifts. If every support contains $j$, then $D\setminus A\subseteq C$ and $D\cup C=A\cup C$; for every ample intermediate $D'$ in a section order, the shadow union and intersection count proves that the family with sections $D'$ and $C$ remains ample. Thus every section deletion lifts, while restricting any whole-family order gives the converse.
