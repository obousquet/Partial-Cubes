Terminal Iterated Common-Seed Covers Are Antipodal Two-Repair Towers

Name: Terminal Iterated Common-Seed Covers Are Antipodal Two-Repair Towers

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Coordinate Projection
  • Partial-Cube Minor
  • Full Reduction

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $G\subseteq Q_F$ be an irredundantly embedded ample forbidden pc-minor. Let $S$ be a forbidden common-seed cover over $G$ on a nonempty layer set $D$, with distinct-support resolving tips and nonempty proper occurrence rows, and let $X$ be a second such cover over $S$ on a disjoint nonempty set $E$. If $X$ is corner-descent-terminal, then both stages have exactly two repair tips and antipodally punctured rows. For distinct tips $a,b$, words $u\in Q_D$ and $v\in Q_E$, one has $S=(G\times Q_D)\cup(\{a\}\times(Q_D\setminus\{u\}))\cup(\{b\}\times(Q_D\setminus\{\bar u\}))$ and $X=(G\times Q_{D\cup E})\cup(\{a\}\times(Q_{D\cup E}\setminus\{(u,v)\}))\cup(\{b\}\times(Q_{D\cup E}\setminus\{(\bar u,\bar v)\}))$. Thus $D\cup E$ is successful for $X$. A terminal chain of two or more such covers is a single antipodal two-repair tower over its initial seed.

Status: Established

Proof:

A resolving addition to the inner cover must change its full reduction along $D$, which equals the nonpeelable seed $G$. It must therefore fill the unique missing word of an occurrence row $R_a=Q_D\setminus\{u_a\}$; conversely each such completion peels by the common-seed criterion. Flatten the outer cover over $G$: a selected row becomes $(R_a\times Q_E)\cup(\{u_a\}\times T_a)$ and an unselected row becomes $R_a\times Q_E$. Every flattened row is nonempty, proper, and ample. Terminality forces these flattened rows to partition the signed facets of $D\cup E$. Reducing along $E$ recovers the inner rows and shows that they partition the signed facets of $D$; the outer rows likewise partition those of $E$. The nested-recovery theorem then forces two owners at each stage, opposite punctures, the displayed flattening, and success of $D\cup E$. Applying the two-stage argument repeatedly flattens any longer terminal chain.

Comments:

The inner rows need not be facet shells and may have overlapping facet ownership. Applied to the canonical largest recovered layer set of a terminal obstruction, the theorem prevents a further nontrivial common-seed cover satisfying these hypotheses.
