The 527- and 528-Concept Classes Are Minimal under Ample Inclusion

Name: The 527- and 528-Concept Classes Are Minimal under Ample Inclusion

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • One-Point Amalgam
  • Graph Expansion
  • Coordinate Projection
  • Full Reduction

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

Let $(A,r)$ be the certified $264$-concept rooted input with $r=750$. Gluing two copies at their roots gives a cornerless $527$-concept ample forbidden pc-minor for which every proper ample subfamily is dismantlable ample. Joining the two roots by a fresh edge instead gives a cornerless $528$-concept negative ample class with the same proper-ample-subfamily property. It is not a forbidden pc-minor because contracting the new edge produces the negative $527$-concept class. Every coordinate reduction of both examples is dismantlable ample.

Status: Established

Proof:

The certified rooted input is positive, has $r$ as its only corner, cannot peel to $r$, and every proper ample subfamily $B$ peels relatively to $B\cap\{r\}$. The ample-inclusion cut-vertex criterion therefore proves the $527$ assertion. For the bridge example, attach a new leaf $s$ at $r$. The resulting piece peels ordinarily but cannot peel to $s$; every proper ample subfamily peels relatively to its intersection with $s$, by reducing its old-coordinate part to the corresponding certified subfamily of $A$. Gluing this leaf-rooted piece to the other copy of $A$ gives the $528$ class and the same inclusion-minimality criterion proves its negativity and cornerlessness. Its bridge contraction is the $527$ class, which disproves pc-minimality. Positivity of all reductions follows from the general inclusion-minimal theorem.
