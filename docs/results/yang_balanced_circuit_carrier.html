Balanced Circuit Carriers of Yang Obstructions

Name: Balanced Circuit Carriers of Yang Obstructions

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Yang Order-Sphere Obstruction

Result Type: Implication

Statement:

If an ample family $H$ is nonpeelable, some nonempty collection $\mathcal I$ of latent charts has every generating failure relation on a directed cycle and satisfies $|\mathcal I|+k_{\mathcal I}=|H|+1$, where $k_{\mathcal I}$ is the number of strongly connected classes of the generated preorder. If $r_{\mathcal I}$ concepts are incident with generating relations and these concepts form $c_{\mathcal I}$ nontrivial strongly connected components, the balance equation is equivalently $|\mathcal I|=r_{\mathcal I}-c_{\mathcal I}+1$. Such balanced circuit collections are necessary carriers of the global obstruction class; their existence alone is not asserted sufficient.

Status: Established

Proof:

For a nonpeelable family, the top order-sphere class survives the failure-cover spectral sequence. Some first-page summand of total degree $|H|-2$ supports it. A nonzero summand comes from an antichain strongly connected quotient, has bidegrees $p=|\mathcal I|-1$ and $q=k_{\mathcal I}-2$, and therefore gives the balance equation. An antichain condensation has no arc between distinct components, so every generating relation lies on a directed cycle. Counting the isolated concepts and nontrivial components gives $k_{\mathcal I}=|H|-r_{\mathcal I}+c_{\mathcal I}$ and the equivalent form.
