Directed Coordinate Spaces Are Products over Cycle Arrangements

Name: Directed Coordinate Spaces Are Products over Cycle Arrangements

Invariants:

  • Separator Coefficient Space

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

For a finite loopless digraph $G$, let $B_G$ be the complement in $\mathbb R^{V(G)}$ of every diagonal on an inclusion-minimal directed-cycle vertex set. The difference map $q(x,y)=x-y$ from $\mathcal Z(G)$ is surjective and fits an explicit homeomorphism $\mathcal Z(G)\cong B_G\times\mathbb R^{V(G)}$. It has a continuous rationality-preserving section and a fiberwise strong deformation retraction.

Status: Established

Proof:

After changing to difference and sum coordinates, each fiber is a strict difference-constraint system. It is feasible exactly when no directed cycle has constant difference coordinate. Shortest-path minima give a continuous center, and a positively homogeneous gauge gives mutually inverse radial maps between the fiber and Euclidean space.
