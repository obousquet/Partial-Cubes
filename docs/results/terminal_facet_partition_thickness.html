Thickness of Terminal Facet Partitions

Name: Thickness of Terminal Facet Partitions

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Conditioning

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

In a terminal signed-facet partition on $r=|E|$ layer coordinates, let $K_a$ be the layer coordinates represented in owner block $\mathcal O_a$, and let $\lambda(e,i)$ own signed facet $(e,i)$. For the projected $e$-sections $H_i=\rho_e^iX$, one has $|H_i\setminus H_{1-i}|=2^{r-|K_{\lambda(e,i)}|}$. A layer coordinate has symmetric-difference order two exactly when there are two repair tips, in which case every layer coordinate does. With at least three repair tips, every coordinate of $X$, including each seed coordinate, has two nonempty exclusive sections whose total order is at least three.

Status: Established

Proof:

For the owner of $(e,i)$, the $i$-section of its row is full and the opposite section misses the face that fixes every other represented coordinate opposite its owned sign. That face has $2^{r-|K_a|}$ words; all nonowners have equal $e$-sections, proving the formula. Both exclusive parts have order one exactly when the two owners represent every coordinate, which exhausts the signed-facet partition and leaves exactly two tips. For an old coordinate, periphery-freeness of the seed supplies one exclusive old word on each side. The product core supplies all layer occurrences of it, and an opposite repair row can fill all but $2^{r-|K_a|}$ occurrences. Both sides remain nonempty; total order two would force two full-support owners and exclude a third tip.
