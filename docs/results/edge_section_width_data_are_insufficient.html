Two Scalar Widths Do Not Determine Edge Gluing

Name: Two Scalar Widths Do Not Determine Edge Gluing

Classes:

  • Dismantlable Ample Classes
  • Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Ordering Width
  • Prescribed-Subset Ordering Width

Operations:

  • Common-Section Gluing

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

There are dismantlable ample pieces $A,B_u,B_v$ over one common edge $D$ such that $\sigma(B_u)=\sigma(B_v)=0$ and $\sigma(B_u;D)=\sigma(B_v;D)=s>0$, while $\sigma(A\cup B_u)=s$ and $\sigma(A\cup B_v)=0$. Moreover $\sigma(A;D)>0$, and reflecting the shared coordinate exchanges $B_u$ and $B_v$ while preserving $D$ setwise.

Status: Established

Proof:

Take $A=R$, the rooted 289-vertex ample factor with only corner $u=0$ and a peeling ending at its neighbor $v=2$, and let $D=\{u,v\}$. Attach a private copy of $R$ at either endpoint to form $B_r=(D\times\{0\})\cup(\{r\}\times R)$. Both $B_r$ peel and have width zero, while paired conditioning and an explicit $D$-first order give the common positive value $\sigma(B_r;D)=s=\sigma(R;\{u\})$. Exact root gluing gives width $s$ when the second copy is attached at $u$ and width zero when it is attached at $v$.

Proof Source:

Witnesses:

  • Endpoint-Sensitive Edge Attachments
  • Rooted 289-Vertex Peeling Factor

Comments:

The failure comes from the pieces' placement relative to the labelled common edge. Any quantitative composition rule for larger sections needs more information than absolute and prescribed-section widths, even after adjoining invariants unchanged by a reflection of the piece.
