Representation Maps Can Be Repaired at Corners of Dimension at Most Two

Name: Representation Maps Can Be Repaired at Corners of Dimension at Most Two

Classes:

  • Representation-Map Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Concept Deletion

Result Type: Algorithm

Statement:

Let $D$ be ample with a representation map and let $v$ be a corner with support $J$, $|J|\le2$. At most three adjacent label exchanges inside the corner cube produce a representation map assigning $J$ to $v$, without changing labels outside that cube. Consequently deleting $v$ preserves existence of a representation map, with no bound on the VC dimension of $D$.

Status: Established

Proof:

A safe exchange swaps adjacent labels $A$ and $A\cup\{i\}$; the support bijection is unchanged and any supposed new clash would already have been an old clash. The owner of $J$ lies inside the corner cube, and incoming neighbors carry no directions outside $J$. For a one-edge corner one exchange suffices. For a square corner, the four possible positions and labels reduce by a case table to at most three safe exchanges. The corner-deletion representation criterion then applies.

References:

\cite[Corollary~6.7 and Theorem~6.8]{ChalopiChepoiMoran22}
