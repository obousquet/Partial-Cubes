Complete Rank-Three Local Trace-Cover Catalogue

Name: Complete Rank-Three Local Trace-Cover Catalogue

Classes:

  • Cornerless Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Maximal-Cube Intersection Graph

Result Type: Algorithm

Statement:

Up to coordinate permutations and reversals, the irredundant covers of $Q_r$ by nonempty proper subcubes have respectively 1, 4, and 43 orbits for $r=1,2,3$. The labelled cover counts are 1, 11, and 869; the proper-subcube counts are 2, 8, and 26; and the cover-size ranges are 2, 2--4, and 2--8. Hence local cornerlessness for every cube family of VC dimension at most three reduces to 48 fixed cover types.

Status: Established

Proof:

Encode a proper subcube by a nonempty set of fixed coordinates and their bits, giving $3^r-1$ candidates. The exhaustive verifier branches at the first uncovered cube vertex, retains exactly covers in which every member has a private vertex, and takes the lexicographically least image under all $2^r r!$ cube automorphisms. It reproduces all displayed counts and the archived canonical catalogue.
