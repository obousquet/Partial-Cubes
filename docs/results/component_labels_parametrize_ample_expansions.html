Component Labels Parametrize Ample One-Coordinate Expansions

Name: Component Labels Parametrize Ample One-Coordinate Expansions

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Shattered Complex
  • Signed Minimal-Nonface Presentation

Operations:

  • Graph Expansion

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $\varnothing\ne C\subseteq A\subseteq Q_F$ be ample, let $e\notin F$, and let $K_1,\ldots,K_k$ be the connected components of $A\setminus C$. For $b\in\{0,1\}^k$, set $X_b=(C\times\{0,1\})\cup\bigcup_i(K_i\times\{b_i\})$. Then $X_b$ is ample and $\operatorname{Sh}(X_b)=\operatorname{Sh}(A)\cup\{T\cup\{e\}:T\in\operatorname{Sh}(C)\}$. Conversely, these are exactly the ample families with contraction $A$ and reduction $C$ in coordinate $e$, and the component labels are unique. More precisely, put $\mathcal I=\mathcal N(\operatorname{Sh}(C))\cap\operatorname{Sh}(A)$ and let $\tau_T$ be the unique trace missing from $C|_T$. The fiber $A(T,\tau_T)$ lies in a unique component $K_{i(T)}$; the new signed minimal nonfaces are exactly $(T\cup\{e\},(\tau_T,1-b_{i(T)}))$ for $T\in\mathcal I$, every component occurs, and a signing is admissible exactly when its new bits agree on equal component indices. Hence there are exactly $2^k$ expansions. If $A=C$, the unique construction is $A\times\{0,1\}$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Every cube $Q\subseteq A$ has $Q\setminus C$ connected when nonempty, because it is the complement of an ample face section inside a cube. It therefore lies in one component and survives in one layer. This proves that old-coordinate supports shattered by $X_b$ are strongly shattered. A support $T\cup\{e\}$ is shattered exactly when $T$ is shattered by $C$: if a trace is missing from $C|_T$, its nonempty connected fiber in $A$ lies in one component and occurs in only one layer. The displayed shadow formula and Sandwich equality give ampleness. Conversely, adjacent vertices of $A\setminus C$ cannot receive different lift bits, since the resulting square section would be an antipodal pair and would not be ample, so lift bits are constant on components. The minimal-nonface formula follows from the shadow identity. Every component occurs because a point outside $C$ violates a minimal signed clause of $C$; cardinality equality forces its support to lie in $\mathcal I$. Finally, the old clauses and the component-consistent new clauses force contraction and reduction into $A$ and $C$, while equal cardinality forces equality, proving sufficiency and uniqueness.
