Universality of Peelable Occurrence Rows

Name: Universality of Peelable Occurrence Rows

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Partial-Cube Minor

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

Suppose a cornerless ample forbidden pc-minor $S$ has three dismantlable-ample one-concept repairs $a_0,a_+,a_-$ with pairwise distinct acquired supports. Then every nonempty dismantlable ample family $K$ is isomorphic to a proper pc-minor of an explicitly constructed ample forbidden pc-minor $X_K$. If $K\subseteq Q_D$, add a layer bit $z$, set $E=D\dot\cup\{z\}$, choose $u\in Q_E$, and use occurrence rows $R_0=K\times\{0\}$, $R_+=Q_E\setminus\{u\}$, and $R_-=Q_E\setminus\{\bar u\}$ at the three tips. Fixing the old coordinates to $a_0$ and $z=0$ recovers $K$. Hall's class with tips 183, 315, and 1836 supplies the required seed triple, so the conclusion is unconditional.

Status: Established

Proof:

The first row is isomorphic to $K$, and the other two are singly punctured cubes, so all three are nonempty proper ample and corner-peelable. For each layer coordinate, the signed facet opposite the chosen bit of $u$ lies in $R_+$ and the other facet lies in $R_-$. The common-seed occurrence-cover criterion therefore makes the assembly an ample forbidden pc-minor independently of the shape of $R_0$. Only $R_0$ occurs at old word $a_0$, and restricting its added bit to zero gives $K$ as a proper face. The certified Hall repair atlas verifies the three distinct-support peelable repairs.

Comments:

This is a universality barrier for generative classification: using membership of the arbitrary row $K$ in the dismantlable ample class as an admissibility condition would remain circular.
