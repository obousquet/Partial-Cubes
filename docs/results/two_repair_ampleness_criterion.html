Exact Ampleness Criterion for Two One-Concept Extensions

Name: Exact Ampleness Criterion for Two One-Concept Extensions

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Shattered Complex

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $S\subseteq Q_F$ be ample and let the distinct one-concept extensions $A=S\cup\{a\}$ and $B=S\cup\{b\}$ be ample. Each adds one unique shattered support, locally given by $P_a=\{f:a^{\{f\}}\in S\}$ and $P_b=\{f:b^{\{f\}}\in S\}$. For a new coordinate $e$, the opposite-layer lift $X=(B\times\{0\})\cup(A\times\{1\})$ is ample exactly when $P_a\ne P_b$. Under this condition $C=S\cup\{a,b\}$ is ample, $\operatorname{Sh}(C)=\operatorname{Sh}(S)\mathbin{\dot\cup}\{P_a,P_b\}$, and $\operatorname{Sh}(A)\cap\operatorname{Sh}(B)=\operatorname{Sh}(S)$.

Status: Established

Proof:

The one-concept extension test gives the unique supports and their local formulas. If they are distinct, the two-tip union strongly shatters the old shadow and both new supports, already $|S|+2$ sets. The Sandwich inequalities force equality and ampleness, yielding the exact union and intersection formulas. A support on old coordinates is shattered by the lift exactly when it is shattered by $C$, while $D\cup\{e\}$ is shattered exactly when $D$ is shattered by both sections. With distinct supports this gives $|\operatorname{Sh}(X)|=|C|+|S|=|X|$, so $X$ is ample. If the supports agree, the section-shadow intersection contains the old shadow plus their common new support, and Sauer's inequality gives at least $|X|+1$ shattered sets, excluding ampleness.
