Missing Triple Traces Form a Tree on Four Coordinates

Name: Missing Triple Traces Form a Tree on Four Coordinates

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Shattered Complex
  • Signed Minimal-Nonface Presentation

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Let $\Sigma$ be a two-dimensional simplicial complex with complete one-skeleton and at most two triangular faces on every four-set, and let an ample $H$ have shadow $\Sigma$. On any four-set $U$, represent each missing triple by the cube edge consisting of the two words extending its omitted trace. These edges have connected union. Hence two missing triples in $U$ have omitted traces agreeing on their overlap; if exactly three missing triples share a coordinate, all three omitted traces take the same value there.

Status: Established

Proof:

If $t$ triangles occur in $U$, the projection has $11+t$ concepts, so its complement has $5-t$ vertices and is the union of the $4-t$ missing-triple edges. Their directions are distinct, making the union a forest. A forest with $m$ edges and $m+1$ vertices is connected. Intersecting cube edges have compatible prescribed traces, and connectivity propagates the shared-coordinate value.
