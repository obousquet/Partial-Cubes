Fibers of the Three-Vertex Directed Path

Name: Fibers of the Three-Vertex Directed Path

Classes:

    Invariants:

    • Separator Coefficient Space

    Operations:

    • Coordinate Conditioning

    Result Type: Recognition

    Statement:

    For the restriction $f:\mathcal A(B)\to\mathcal A(D)$ from the three-vertex directed-path class to its endpoint section, write $\delta=z_2-z_0\in\mathbb R^2$ for the difference of the endpoint log positions. The fiber $f^{-1}(a)$ has two contractible components when $\delta_1\le0$ and $\delta_2\le0$, and is contractible otherwise. The two walls are $a_{v_2,i}a_{v_0,3}=a_{v_0,i}a_{v_2,3}$ for $i=1,2$.

    Status: Established

    Proof:

    Protected-row reduction identifies the fiber with $\mathbb R\times W$, where an intermediate point $w$ must make one of two control increments positive on each directed edge. The four choices cover $W$ by open convex sets. If both endpoint differences are nonpositive, only the two opposite choices survive and are disjoint. With one positive difference their nerve is a path, and with both positive all four sets meet. The open good-cover theorem gives the stated component and contractibility alternatives.
