Forced Wing Words Certify Active Coordinates

Name: Forced Wing Words Certify Active Coordinates

Classes:

  • Cornerless Ample Classes

Result Type: Implication

Statement:

Let a cornerless ample two-layer family have overlap $B$ and wing $D_b$, and suppose all members of $D_b$ share one word $\sigma$ on passive coordinates $Z$. Form $W_b$ from any shore concepts whose opposite $Z$-fibres occur in $B$, together with $o_v=v^{\oplus z}$ for every corner $v$ of $B$ whose passive word differs from $\sigma$ only in $z$. Then $W_b\subseteq D_b$. If $D_b$ is connected, every coordinate varying on $W_b$ is active on $D_b$; if $D_b$ is two-connected, then $|D_b|\ge 2|Y(W_b)|$.

Status: Established

Proof:

The shore concepts lie in the wing by definition. Passive radius-one shielding makes $v^{\oplus z}$ the unique possible shield of each distance-one overlap corner, so cornerlessness forces every such owner into $D_b$. A path inside connected $D_b$ between two words differing in coordinate $x$ must traverse an $x$-edge, hence every coordinate varying on $W_b$ is active. The two-connected cube-coordinate cost bound then gives $|D_b|\ge2|Y(W_b)|$.
