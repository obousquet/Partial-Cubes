A Companion Tip Excludes Rank-Three Resolving Neighbors

Name: A Companion Tip Excludes Rank-Three Resolving Neighbors

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes
  • Cornerless Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Concept Deletion

Result Type: Implication

Statement:

In the adjacent same-support construction that turns a cornerless ample forbidden pc-minor $G$ into the one-corner obstruction $G\cup\{c\}$, every resolving addition adjacent to $c$ has acquired support of size at least four. If the original acquired support $I$ has size three, every such resolving tip $u=c^{\{i\}}$ completes the full $I\cup\{i\}$-cube, while $G\cup\{u\}$ is itself an ample one-corner forbidden pc-minor acquiring $I$. Therefore a two-neighbor falsifier at support three requires three tips $c,u,v$ with the same acquired support over $G$: all individual extensions are nonpeelable, but both pair extensions $G\cup\{c,u\}$ and $G\cup\{c,v\}$ must peel.

Status: Established

Proof:

The square-completion corollary excludes original support at most two. Let the companion tip be $h=c^{\{f\}}$. Its punctured cube over $G$ shows that adding $h$ to $G\cup\{c\}$ acquires $I\cup\{f\}$, of size at least four. Any resolving neighbor has support at least three by rank-two invariance. If it had size three, the rank-three forced-deletion theorem would apply with companion $h$ and $K=I$, excluding resolution. Thus every resolving neighbor has support at least four. For original support three, the punctured-cube formula bounds an adjacent addition's support by $I\cup\{i\}$, so equality follows. Removing $c$ leaves the punctured $I$-cube at $u$, making $G\cup\{u\}$ ample with acquired support $I$; the adjacent same-support theorem makes it one-corner and forbidden. Applying this to both resolving neighbors yields the three-tip condition.

Comments:

This excludes support three only in the companion construction. It does not exclude rank-three resolving neighbors of arbitrary one-corner families or settle cooperation among support-four repairs.
