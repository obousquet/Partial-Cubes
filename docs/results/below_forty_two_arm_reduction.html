Cornerless Ample Obstructions Below Forty Reduce to Two-Arm Holonomy

Name: Cornerless Ample Obstructions Below Forty Reduce to Two-Arm Holonomy

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Dismantlable-Ample Relative Corner Threshold

Result Type: Implication

Statement:

Let $H$ have minimum cardinality among nonempty cornerless ample families and suppose $|H|\le39$. In any active-coordinate section decomposition with common part $B$ and relative wings $D_0,D_1$, one has $|B|\le9$. If an order-ten wing is two-connected, its pair $B\subsetneq A_b$ is terminal under one-concept addition and deletion, its lower holonomy is a two-cycle, it satisfies the exact two-arm excess budget, and $|B|\ge7$. The same conclusion holds for a two-connected order-eleven wing when addition terminality is assumed.

Status: Established

Proof:

Both wings have at least the relative-corner threshold, now at least ten, so $2|B|=|H|-|D_0|-|D_1|\le19$ and $|B|\le9$. For an order-ten wing, an ample one-concept addition to $B$ would be dismantlable and leave a trapped interval of size nine, contradicting the threshold. An ample deletion would make the deleted wing concept a corner of $A_b$, contrary to all corners being trapped in $B$. The pair is therefore terminal. Two-connectivity invokes the passive-gate normalization and small-lower-family theorem, giving the two-cycle, exact budget, and $|B|\ge|L|+6\ge7$. The assumed terminal order-eleven case is identical.
