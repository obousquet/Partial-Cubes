Ample Assembly and USO Compatibility over a Coordinate Face

Name: Ample Assembly and USO Compatibility over a Coordinate Face

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Common-Section Gluing

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let nonempty $D\subseteq Q_E$ be the common zero section of ample pieces $A_i\subseteq Q_{E\mathbin{\dot\cup}F_i}$ with disjoint exclusive coordinate blocks, and put $X=\bigcup_iA_i$. Then $X$ is ample exactly when $\operatorname{Sh}(A_i)\cap\operatorname{Sh}(A_j)=\operatorname{Sh}(D)$ for every $i\ne j$; this is automatic when $D=Q_E$. Under this condition, USOs on the pieces glue exactly when they agree on $D$ and at every $d\in D$ at most one piece directs an edge from $d$ into its exclusive part. The glued USO is acyclic exactly when the union orientation is acyclic; acyclicity of the pieces alone suffices when $|D|\le2$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Every contained cube lies in one piece, so the strongly shattered supports of the union are the union of the piece shadows. Inclusion-exclusion and the Sandwich inequality give the exact pairwise shadow-intersection test. The same cube localization proves the unique-sink axiom piecewise. At a boundary vertex, outgoing directions in two private blocks would require a missing mixed square, while directions from at most one private block and the common face lie in one piece. A directed cycle crossing a singleton is impossible; over an edge it would combine the common directed edge with a reverse directed path inside one piece, contradicting that piece's acyclicity.
