Exact Criterion and Count for Horizontal USO Lifts

Name: Exact Criterion and Count for Horizontal USO Lifts

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Fiber Product

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $X$ be ample, let $e$ be active, write $A=\pi_eX$ and $D=X^{\{e\}}$, and fix an acyclic USO $\mathcal O$ of $A$. An acyclic USO of $X$ preserving the orientation of every old-direction edge exists exactly when the induced orientation $\mathcal O|_D$ is a USO of $D$. If $s$ vertices $d\in D$ have no outgoing neighbour in $A\setminus D$, exactly $2^s$ such lifts exist. At every other $d$, the vertical edge is forced toward the lift label of its outgoing external neighbours; the remaining $s$ vertical directions are independent.

Status: Established

Proof:

Necessity follows from the outgoing-cube condition at the source of each vertical edge and from restriction to cubes of $D$. Conversely, the complement of an ample intersection inside any cube is connected, so all external outgoing neighbours of a doubled vertex lie in one labeled component. Direct the vertical edge toward that label, with a free choice when there is no external outgoing neighbour. The outgoing-cube and unique-sink conditions then hold on every cube, and a directed cycle would project to a directed closed walk in the acyclic base orientation. Reversing any forced vertical edge creates an outgoing pair with a missing opposite lift, proving exhaustiveness and the count.
