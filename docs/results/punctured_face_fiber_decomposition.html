Fiber Decomposition of Punctured-Face Data

Name: Fiber Decomposition of Punctured-Face Data

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Let $S$ be ample. Every minimal nonface $I$ of its shattered complex has a unique omitted trace $\sigma_I$. The punctured-face atlas of $S$ is the disjoint union over such $I$ of outside-assignment fibers: an assignment $y$ on $F\setminus I$ belongs to the $I$-fiber exactly when every nonempty flip of $\sigma_I$ within $I$, paired with $y$, lies in $S$, while the missing tip $(\sigma_I,y)$ has no neighbour in any coordinate outside $I$. Thus all punctured faces with support $I$ share the same missing $I$-trace; the outside assignment alone locates the punctured cube.

Status: Established

Proof:

For a punctured face $(a,I)$, all $I$-traces except possibly $a|_I$ occur, while nonshattering says some trace is absent. Hence $a|_I$ is the unique omitted trace $\sigma_I$. Its outside restriction $y$ satisfies the punctured-cube membership conditions, and equality between $I$ and the incident-direction set of $a$ excludes all outside neighbours. Conversely these two fiber conditions put the punctured $I$-cube around $(\sigma_I,y)$ inside $S$, keep its tip outside $S$, and make its incident-direction set exactly $I$, recovering a unique atlas entry.
