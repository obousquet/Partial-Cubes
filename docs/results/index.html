First-Two Face Formula

Name: First-Two Face Formula

Invariants:

  • Ordering Width
  • Prescribed-Subset Ordering Width

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

For any order $\pi$ of a finite nonempty class, its width is the maximum of $d_H(a_F,b_F)-1$ over coordinate faces $F$ containing at least two concepts, where $a_F,b_F$ are the first two concepts of $C\cap F$ in the order, together with zero.

Status: Established

Proof:

A minimal predecessor disagreement $\operatorname{diff}(c,b)$ means that $b,c$ are the first two concepts in their ambient interval face. Conversely, if $a_F,b_F$ are first in a face, an earlier proper subdisagreement would supply a concept of that face before $b_F$. These two correspondences preserve the disagreement size.

Proof Source:

Ordering Width Decreases Under PC-Minors

Invariants:

  • Ordering Width
  • Prescribed-Subset Ordering Width

Operations:

  • Coordinate Projection
  • Coordinate Conditioning

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Every nonempty pc-minor $M$ of $C$ satisfies $\sigma(M)\leq\sigma(C)$. For paired projection or conditioning of $(C,I)$ to a nonempty pair $(M,L)$, one also has $\sigma(M;L)\leq\sigma(C;I)$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Under conditioning, restrict an order; any surviving minimal disagreement was already minimal. Resolution-Slope and Ordering-Width Laws

Name: Resolution-Slope and Ordering-Width Laws

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Resolution Slope
  • Rounded Resolution-Slope Defect
  • Ordering Width

Operations:

  • Coordinate Projection
  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Partial-Cube Minor
  • Cartesian Product

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

For every nonempty binary class $C$, $\lambda_k(C)\leq\sigma(C)+1$ and $\nu_k(C)\leq\sigma(C)$. Both slope invariants are unchanged by cube symmetries and constant-coordinate extensions, are nonincreasing under pc-minors, and take the maximum under Cartesian products on disjoint coordinates. Moreover, $\nu_k(C)=0$ exactly when $C$ is ample.

Status: Established

Proof:

An order of width $t$ gives colon ideals generated in degrees at most $t+1$; Taylor resolutions of the colons and the graded horseshoe construction bound every shift of $J_C$ by $n+i(t+1)$. Literal multigraded resolutions restrict under conditioning and localize under projection without increasing excess shifts. Minimal resolutions tensor on disjoint variables, which gives the product maximum. Finally slope one is linear resolution, and the Alexander-dual Yang correspondence identifies that condition with ampleness.

Face-Homology Formula for Literal-Ideal Betti Numbers

Name: Face-Homology Formula for Literal-Ideal Betti Numbers

Invariants:

  • Resolution Slope
  • Rounded Resolution-Slope Defect

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Let $F$ be a positive-dimensional coordinate face with nonempty trace $V=C\cap F$, and let $\mathcal B(C,F)$ be the simplicial complex of subsets of $V$ lying in a proper coordinate face of $F$. If $u_F$ is the literal label of $F$, then for $i\geq1$, $\beta_{i,u_F}(J_C)=\dim_k\widetilde H_{i-1}(\mathcal B(C,F);k)$. Every positive-step Betti label arises from a face this way. Thus nonzero homology for an $r$-face certifies $\nu_k(C)\geq\lceil r/i\rceil-1$.

Status: Established

Proof:

The $u_F$ strand of the Taylor resolution is the relative augmented chain complex of the full simplex on $V$ modulo the subsets whose least common multiple strictly divides $u_F$, which are precisely $\mathcal B(C,F)$. Contractibility of the full simplex gives the displayed reduced homology. Every squarefree least common multiple of literal generators determines a coordinate face.

Betti Numbers under Common-Section Gluing

Name: Betti Numbers under Common-Section Gluing

Invariants:

  • Resolution Slope
  • Rounded Resolution-Slope Defect

Operations:

  • Common-Section Gluing

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

For common-section pieces $A_1,\ldots,A_m$ over $D$ satisfying the projection hypotheses, embed their literal ideals $I_j$ and the base ideal $I_0$ in the common literal ring and let $I$ be the ideal of the union $X$. Then $\beta_{h,u}(I)=\sum_j\beta_{h,u}(I_j)-(m-1)\beta_{h,u}(I_0)$ for every $h$ and literal multidegree $u$. Consequently $\lambda_k(X)=\max_j\lambda_k(A_j)$ and $\nu_k(X)=\max_j\nu_k(A_j)$, and labels varying in two private blocks have multiplicity zero.

Status: Established

Proof:

Distinct piece ideals intersect in the embedded base ideal, yielding an exact sequence from $I_0^{\oplus(m-1)}$ to the direct sum of the piece ideals and then to $I$. At a Betti label of $I_0$, the Taylor strands for the base and each piece coincide, so the induced Tor maps are injective. The long exact Tor sequence therefore splits into short exact sequences in every multidegree and gives the formula. Embedding a piece raises its shifts and generator degree equally, so the slope formulas follow.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/structural-invariants/main.tex?#thm:betti-gluing

Comments:

No ampleness assumption is required. The projection equality $\pi_E(A_i)=D$ is essential.

Interval Gaps and First Syzygies

Name: Interval Gaps and First Syzygies

Classes:

  • Rounded Resolution-Slope Defect

Operations:

  • Partial-Cube Minor
  • Cartesian Product
  • Common-Section Gluing

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

The first Betti multiplicity at a face label $u_F$ is one exactly when $C\cap F$ is an antipodal pair in $F$, and is zero otherwise. Hence $g(C)=\max(\{j-n-1:\beta_{1,j}(J_C)\neq0\}\cup\{0\})\leq\nu_k(C)\leq\sigma(C)$. The zero set of $g$ is exactly the isometric cube embeddings. Moreover, $g$ is pc-minor monotone and takes maxima under Cartesian products and common-section gluings satisfying the projection hypotheses.

Status: Established

Proof:

First Betti multiplicity is reduced zero-dimensional homology of the face complex. Its graph joins every non-antipodal pair and is disconnected exactly for a two-vertex antipodal trace. Empty intervals survive lifting through projection or conditioning, while a product pair varying in both factors has a mixed intermediate point. In a common-section union, pairs from different pieces have a base lift strictly between them, so every empty interval lies in one piece.

Quantitative Common-Section Gluing Bound

Invariants:

  • Ordering Width
  • Prescribed-Subset Ordering Width

Operations:

  • Common-Section Gluing

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Statement:

Dismantlable ample classes are closed under nonempty convex restrictions, coordinate projections, pc-minors, strong projections, Cartesian products, cube symmetries, and specified peripheral expansions whose base and site are dismantlable ample.   • Horn-Renaming Defect

Breadth-First Corner Peeling of Conditional Antimatroids

Name: Breadth-First Corner Peeling of Conditional Antimatroids

Classes:

  • Conditional Antimatroids
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Result Type: Algorithm

Statement:

If $\mathcal C$ is a finite conditional antimatroid, any ordering of its members by nondecreasing cardinality, equivalently any breadth-first order from $\varnothing$ with ties broken arbitrarily, is an ample-prefix order. Reversing it is a corner peeling.

Status: Established

Proof:

At the moment a member $c$ is inserted, its earlier neighbors are exactly $c\setminus\{x\}$ for extremal $x$. Intersection closure contains $c\setminus S$ for every subset $S$ of its extremal set, so those earlier neighbors and $c$ span a full cube. Thus $c$ is a corner of its prefix.
