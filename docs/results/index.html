First-Two Face Formula

Name: First-Two Face Formula

Invariants:

  • Ordering Width
  • Prescribed-Subset Ordering Width

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

For any order $\pi$ of a finite nonempty class, its width is the maximum of $d_H(a_F,b_F)-1$ over coordinate faces $F$ containing at least two concepts, where $a_F,b_F$ are the first two concepts of $C\cap F$ in the order, together with zero.

Status: Established

Proof:

A minimal predecessor disagreement $\operatorname{diff}(c,b)$ means that $b,c$ are the first two concepts in their ambient interval face. Conversely, if $a_F,b_F$ are first in a face, an earlier proper subdisagreement would supply a concept of that face before $b_F$. These two correspondences preserve the disagreement size.

Zero Ordering Width Characterization

Name: Zero Ordering Width Characterization

Invariants:

  • Ordering Width

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

$\sigma(C)=0$ if and only if $C$ is dismantlable ample, equivalently its concepts admit an order in which every nonempty prefix is ample.

Status: Established

Proof:

Minimal predecessor disagreements are the supports of minimal colon generators of the Alexander-dual Yang ideal. Width zero is therefore linear quotients. Alexander duality identifies this with shellability, and the established shelling dictionary identifies the relevant shelling orders with ample-prefix orders.

Ordering Width Decreases Under PC-Minors

Name: Ordering Width Decreases Under PC-Minors

Invariants:

  • Ordering Width
  • Prescribed-Subset Ordering Width

Operations:

  • Coordinate Projection
  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Partial-Cube Minor

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Every nonempty pc-minor $M$ of $C$ satisfies $\sigma(M)\leq\sigma(C)$. For paired projection or conditioning of $(C,I)$ to a nonempty pair $(M,L)$, one also has $\sigma(M;L)\leq\sigma(C;I)$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Under conditioning, restrict an order; any surviving minimal disagreement was already minimal. Under projection, order distinct images by first occurrence and lift each minimal projected disagreement to an original one of at least the same size. Prescribed initial subsets remain first under the paired construction.

  • Prescribed-Subset Ordering Width

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

For nonempty $I\subseteq C$ and $J\subseteq D$, $\sigma(C\times D)=\max\{\sigma(C),\sigma(D)\}$ and $\sigma(C\times D;I\times J)=\max\{\sigma(C;I),\sigma(D;J)\}$.
