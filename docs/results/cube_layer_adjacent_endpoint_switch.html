A Cube Layer Can Switch Endpoints across Adjacent Clause Signs

Name: A Cube Layer Can Switch Endpoints across Adjacent Clause Signs

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Shattered Complex
  • Signed Minimal-Nonface Presentation

Operations:

  • Peripheral Expansion
  • Concept Deletion

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

Let ample $S\subseteq Q_F$ peel to $a$, contain the coordinate cube $C=Q_I\times\{y\}$, and put $P_0=\{u\in Q_I:S_u=\{y\}\}$. For a fresh $f$, let $R=(S\times\{0\})\cup(C\times\{1\})$. Then $R$ is an ample single-clause completion on $J=I\cup\{f\}$ with outside family $\{y\}$ and admissible traces $P=(P_0\times\{0\})\cup(Q_I\times\{1\})$. All members have common shadow $\operatorname{Sh}(S)\cup\{T\cup\{f\}:T\subsetneq I\}$. Every upper omission peels to $(a,0)$; a lower omission at $u\in P_0$ peels to $(a,0)$ exactly when $S\setminus\{(u,y)\}$ peels to $a$. The root-retaining admissible-trace graph is connected, yet a corner deletion in $S$ that destroys the endpoint produces adjacent retained traces of opposite endpoint status.

Status: Established

Proof:

The construction is the peripheral expansion of $S$ along $C$. Its outside slices are $S_u$ below and the singleton $\{y\}$ above, so the single-clause completion theorem gives exactly the displayed admissible set and shadow. A punctured cube peels by increasing distance from its missing vertex. Applying this first to the upper layer proves every upper choice positive and reduces a lower choice to the corresponding deletion in $S$; restricting any hypothetical lower-side endpoint order back to the lower face proves the converse. The intact upper trace cube connects every retained lower trace to the trace graph. A destructive corner $c=(u,y)$ has no neighbor outside $C$, so $u\in P_0$ and its two vertical choices give the adjacent opposite-status pair.
