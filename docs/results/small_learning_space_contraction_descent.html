Small Contraction Descent and the Eleven-State Minimum

Name: Small Contraction Descent and the Eleven-State Minimum

Classes:

  • Learning-Space Graphs
  • Partial Cubes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Projection

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $G$ be a closed-orientable, five-dimensional partial cube containing an antipodal pair. If $G$ is nonlearning and has at most eleven vertices, then some $\Theta$-class contraction remains nonlearning. Consequently $G$ has exactly eleven vertices and is one of the three pendant extensions of $\mathcal V_4$ or $\mathcal Z_4$ described by the contraction-reduction theorem. The bound is sharp: the twelve-state obstruction $\mathcal H_\star$ has all five contractions learning.

Status: Established

Proof:

If all contractions were learning, the endpoint-cover theorem would make the two repair sets cover five directions. Repair sets of size at least four force too many states; the $(3,3)$ pattern forces at least twelve; and the remaining disjoint $(2,3)$ pattern is excluded by the two-by-three endpoint-cover lemma. The eleven-state contraction reduction then yields the three types, while $\mathcal H_\star$ proves sharpness.
