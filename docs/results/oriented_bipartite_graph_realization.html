Oriented Bipartite Graphs Realize Directed Coordinate Spaces

Name: Oriented Bipartite Graphs Realize Directed Coordinate Spaces

Classes:

  • Realizable Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes
  • Convex-Treelike Partial Cubes

Invariants:

  • Separator Coefficient Space
  • VC Dimension

Operations:

  • Coordinate Conditioning

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

For every finite oriented simple bipartite graph $G$, the class $C(G)$ is convex-realizable, dismantlable ample, finite convex-treelike, and has VC dimension three. With $\mathcal Z(G)=\{z:\max_i(z_{w,i}-z_{v,i})>0\text{ for every }v\to w\}$, one has $\mathcal A(C(G))\cong\mathcal Z(G)\times\mathbb R^{d_G-2|V(G)|}$. Conditioning away private vertices gives the construction on the induced subgraph.

Status: Established

Proof:

Decreasing ample control fibers prove ampleness and give an ample-prefix order. Adding vertex fibers and their pendant edge concepts yields the finite convex-treelike recursion. The complete missing-pattern calculation and private-coordinate product reduce each edge concept to its directed coordinate inequality; explicit integer positions give rational realizability.
