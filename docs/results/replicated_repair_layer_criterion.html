Replicated Repair-Layer Criterion

Name: Replicated Repair-Layer Criterion

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $S\subseteq Q_F$ be ample, let $\mathcal A$ be finitely many absent tips whose ample one-concept extensions acquire pairwise distinct supports $P_a$, and give each tip a nonempty occurrence family $R_a\subseteq Q_E$ on nonempty disjoint layer coordinates. For $X(\mathbf R)=(S\times Q_E)\cup\bigcup_{a\in\mathcal A}(\{a\}\times R_a)$, one has $\operatorname{Sh}(X(\mathbf R))=\{D\cup L:D\in\operatorname{Sh}(S),L\subseteq E\}\dot\cup\dot\bigcup_{a\in\mathcal A}\{P_a\cup L:L\in\operatorname{Sh}(R_a)\}$. Hence $X(\mathbf R)$ is ample exactly when every occurrence family $R_a$ is ample. In that case, writing $\Sigma^+=\operatorname{Sh}(S)\dot\cup\{P_a\}$ and $\Gamma_a=\operatorname{Sh}(R_a)$, its minimal nonfaces are $\mathcal N(\Sigma^+)\dot\cup\dot\bigcup_a\{P_a\cup J:J\in\mathcal N(\Gamma_a)\}$; the corresponding mixed sign is $(a|_{P_a},\sigma_{R_a}(J))$, with old signs inherited from $S\cup\mathcal A$. Thus old repair clauses and layer clauses compose without another compatibility condition. If every $R_a$ is proper, all parameters are recovered from the marked sections; in general, absorbing every full occurrence row $R_a=Q_E$ into the common core gives the unique normalized presentation.

Status: Established

Proof:

For each layer trace, the union of compatible sections is the core together with exactly those tips whose occurrence family realizes that trace. Distinct-support Sandwich equality says this union shatters $P_a$ exactly when it contains $a$. Trace transport therefore makes $P_a\cup L$ shattered exactly when $R_a$ shatters $L$, proving the shadow formula. The global Sandwich defect is the sum over $a$ of $|\operatorname{Sh}(R_a)|-|R_a|$, so it vanishes exactly when every occurrence family is ample. Minimal nonfaces then split into the old minimal nonfaces and a repair support joined to a minimal layer nonface. The unique old missing trace for $P_a$ occurs over precisely $R_a$, which gives the product sign. Proper rows are absent from some section and are separated from the common intersection; full rows lie in every section and are therefore absorbed uniquely into the normalized core.
