Constrained Width of Level-Sum Maximum Sequences

Name: Constrained Width of Level-Sum Maximum Sequences

Classes:

  • Maximum Classes

Invariants:

  • Ordering Width
  • Filtration Width

Operations:

  • Cartesian Product

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Let $A_0\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq A_n=\{0,1\}^X$ and $B_0\subsetneq\cdots\subsetneq B_m=\{0,1\}^Y$ be maximum sequences whose indices are their ranks. For $L_R=\{(x,y):\rho(x)+\lambda(y)\leq R\}$, put $j_0=\max\{0,R-n\}$ and $j_1=\min\{m,R\}$. Then $L_R=\bigcup_{j=j_0}^{j_1}A_{R-j}\times B_j$ is maximum, and the minimum width of an order that introduces these rectangles successively is $\max(\{\sigma(A_{R-j}):j_0\leq j\leq j_1\}\cup\{w(B_{j_0},\ldots,B_{j_1})\})$. In particular this quantity bounds $\sigma(L_R)$ from above; swapping the factors gives a second bound.

Status: Established

Proof:

The level-sum maximum theorem gives the displayed class and its rectangular decomposition. The outer classes $A_{R-j}$ decrease as the inner classes $B_j$ increase, so the exact rectangular-filtration formula applies directly. Dropping the rectangle-prefix constraints can only decrease ordering width, yielding the absolute-width bound; symmetry gives the factor-swapped version.

Comments:

Dismantlability of the individual levels removes their absolute-width terms but does not by itself provide a zero-width order compatible with every inclusion in either maximum sequence.
