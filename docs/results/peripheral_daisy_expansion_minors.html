Elementary Minors of a Peripheral Daisy Expansion

Name: Elementary Minors of a Peripheral Daisy Expansion

Classes:

  • Daisy Cubes

Operations:

  • Peripheral Expansion
  • Coordinate Projection
  • Coordinate Conditioning

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

For nonempty down-sets $K,L\subseteq2^E$ with $\delta\triangle L\subseteq K$, the peripheral expansion $C(K,L,\delta)$ is a daisy cube exactly when $\delta\subseteq\operatorname{Free}(K)\cup\operatorname{Free}(L)$. Restriction in an old coordinate $i$ gives $C(K_i^b,L_i^{b\oplus\mathbf1_{i\in\delta}},\delta\setminus\{i\})$, contraction gives $C(K_i^0,L_i^0,\delta\setminus\{i\})$, and the new-coordinate restrictions and contraction are respectively $K$, $\delta\triangle L$, and $K$ after suppressing constants.

Status: Established

Proof:

The expansion is daisy precisely when one reorientation makes both layers down-sets, which is the common-root criterion. Taking an old-coordinate section tracks whether its bit is flipped by $\delta$; projection of a down-set equals its zero section because its one section is contained there. The new-coordinate formulas follow directly from the two-layer construction.
