Inverse Construction from One Relatively Peelable Wing

Name: Inverse Construction from One Relatively Peelable Wing

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Graph Expansion
  • Full Reduction
  • Concept Deletion
  • Partial-Cube Minor

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

Let $O$ be an ample forbidden pc-minor for dismantlable ampleness and use every coordinate of $Q_F$. Choose dismantlable ample $B_0,B_1$ with $B_0\cap B_1=O$, ample union $A=B_0\cup B_1$, and no edge between the exclusive wings $B_0\setminus O$ and $B_1\setminus O$. If at least one section peels to $O$, then $X=(B_0\times\{0\})\cup(B_1\times\{1\})$ is an ample forbidden pc-minor. Its sections, contraction, and reduction recover the marked expansion and its inputs. Equivalently, this recovers every ample forbidden expansion with negative reduction and at least one relatively peelable section.

Status: Established

Proof:

The component-expansion theorem makes $X$ ample, while its negative reduction $O$ and closure of dismantlable ampleness under full reduction make $X$ nonpeelable. Lift a peeling of, say, $B_0$ to $O$ through layer zero. The absent vertical mates and the separation of the exclusive wings preserve each corner cube, reducing $X$ to a peripheral expansion of $O$ and $B_1$; the same order shows the contraction is positive. Apply any old-coordinate elementary operation to this deletion sequence and omit repeated images. Its terminal family is a peripheral expansion of the positive families $\mu O$ and $\mu B_1$, so every proper elementary minor is positive. Pc-minor closure then proves forbiddenness. The sections, union, and intersection recover the construction data.

Comments:

It is enough that every one-concept intermediate $O\cup\{a\}$ in one wing is ample: positivity of the section then forces a relative peeling. No separate tests of old-coordinate minors of $X$ are needed.
