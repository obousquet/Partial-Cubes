Small Trapped Wings Need at Most One Marked Synchronization

Name: Small Trapped Wings Need at Most One Marked Synchronization

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $B\subsetneq A$ be ample with $\operatorname{Cor}(A)\subseteq B$ and $|A\setminus B|\le11$, and suppose every ample intermediate family belongs to $\mathsf{Damp}$. Then there is an ample $C$ with $B\subseteq C\subsetneq A$ and $|C\setminus B|\le1$ such that $R=A\setminus C$ has order ten or eleven, every $r\in R$ is blocked under both addition to $C$ and deletion from $A$, and the graph induced by $R$ is two-connected. Thus an order-ten trapped wing is terminal; an order-eleven wing is terminal or has a terminal ten-concept core above one marked ample addition.

Status: Established

Proof:

Choose $C$ maximal among proper ample intermediate families. Maximality blocks every one-concept addition $C\cup\{r\}$, while an ample deletion $A\setminus\{r\}$ would make $r$ a corner of $A$ outside $B$, so every residual concept is doubly blocked. Both endpoints lie in $\mathsf{Damp}$ and form a trapped pair. The relative threshold gives $10\le|R|\le11$, hence $|C\setminus B|\le1$. The terminal cut-layer theorem through eleven then makes the residual graph two-connected.
