Minimal Missing Support Does Not Bound Separation Margin

Name: Minimal Missing Support Does Not Bound Separation Margin

Classes:

  • Maximum Classes
  • Learning-Space Graphs
  • Realizable Ample Classes

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

There is no function $g$ with $g(2)>0$ such that every convex-realizable maximum learning space $C$ satisfies $\mu(C)\ge g(r(C))$, where $r(C)$ is the largest support size of a minimal missing pattern.

Status: Established

Proof:

The prefix-chain threshold classes $T_h$ are convex-realizable maximum learning spaces with $r(T_h)=2$ and positive separation margin at most $1/h$. Letting $h$ grow rules out any positive lower bound depending only on $r=2$.
