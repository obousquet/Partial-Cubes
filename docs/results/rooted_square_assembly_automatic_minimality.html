Rooted Square Assemblies Have Automatic Minor Minimality

Name: Rooted Square Assemblies Have Automatic Minor Minimality

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate-Block Replacement
  • Cartesian Product
  • Concept Deletion
  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Full Reduction

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

Let $A$ be ample and corner-peelable with unique corner $0$ and a marked incident coordinate $z$. Assume every zero restriction and contraction in an unmarked coordinate peels to the image of the marked edge, and that both $z$-sections and their contraction peel to the projected root. Replace $z$ by a square block $E$ to obtain $P$. For any rooted factor $(B,0)$ and any hole $q\ne00$, let $Q$ contain the full root square and the punctured square $Q_E\setminus\{q\}$ over every nonroot word of $B$. Then $X=P\cup Q$ is an ample forbidden pc-minor. Its corners are exactly $(b,t)$ with $b$ a nonroot corner of $B$ and $t$ a square neighbor of $q$, so it is cornerless exactly when the root is the unique corner of $B$. Its order is $3|C|+|A_0\cap A_1|+3|B|-3$. The marked pieces, inputs, and hole are recoverable from coordinate faces, block reduction, and square rows.

Status: Established

Proof:

Both pieces are ample: $P$ by block replacement, and $Q$ by adding the missing root-square vertex as a corner to a product. Their shadows meet exactly in the square shadow, proving ampleness of the union. A direct cube test gives the stated corners. Writing $K=T\setminus\{q\}$ identifies the union as a relative product attachment; the unique corner of $P$ lies in $K$, so no relative peeling can start. For a private coordinate of $A$, block transport peels its minor to the unchanged positive $Q$; for a private coordinate of $B$, rooted minimality peels $Q$ to the positive $P$. Square restrictions reduce to the assumed rooted orders on $A_0,A_1,C$, while square contractions are products of a positive vertex gluing with an edge. Thus every elementary proper minor is positive. Counting the two pieces and their common four-vertex square gives the order formula, and their coordinate faces give marked recovery.

Comments:
