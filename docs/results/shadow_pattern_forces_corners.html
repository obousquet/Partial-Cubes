A Shattered-Support Pattern Forces Exponentially Many Corners

Name: A Shattered-Support Pattern Forces Exponentially Many Corners

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Shattered Complex

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Let $H\subseteq Q_F$ be ample with all coordinates active, and let the graph of shattered pairs have a partition $F=\{e\}\mathbin{\dot\cup}I\mathbin{\dot\cup}A\mathbin{\dot\cup}B$ with $I,A,B$ nonempty. Assume $P=\{e\}\cup I$ is shattered, the induced pair graphs on $A$ and $B$ are connected, no pair $\{a,i\}$ with $a\in A,i\in I$ is shattered, and no pair $\{b,e\}$ with $b\in B$ is shattered. Then the unique cube of support $P$ contains at least $2^{|I|}-1$ corners. The hypotheses depend only on the shattered complex, so the conclusion holds for every ample realization with that same complex.

Status: Established

Proof:

The missing pairs exclude every shattered support $P\cup\{f\}$ outside $P$, making the ample $P$-cube unique. For any unshattered coordinate pair, all edges in one direction have a fixed value in the other coordinate; otherwise two such edges realize all four traces. Connectivity on $A$ makes every $A$-edge share one trace $\alpha\in Q_I$, and connectivity on $B$ makes every $B$-edge share one value $\beta$ of coordinate $e$. A vertex of the distinguished cube with $x_e\ne\beta$ and $x|_I\ne\alpha$ has no outside neighbor, so all cubes through it lie in the distinguished cube and it is a corner. Exactly $2^{|I|}-1$ cube vertices satisfy both inequalities.
