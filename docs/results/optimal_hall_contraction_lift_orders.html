Optimal Cornerless Lift Orders over Hall Contractions

Name: Optimal Cornerless Lift Orders over Hall Contractions

Classes:

  • Cornerless Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Projection

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Every cornerless ample one-coordinate lift of a single or double contraction of Hall's class has at least 291 concepts. For the single contraction $C_H/e$, the least order is exactly 291 when $e\le7$ and exactly 295 when $e\ge8$. Explicit VC-dimension-three lifts attain each bound, and therefore $c_{\min}=m_{\mathrm{amp}}\le291$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Every such lift has all base coordinates active, and its new coordinate must be active because every Hall contraction has a corner. Exactness of $L_{B,N}$ converts a hypothetical lift of order at most 290 into a satisfying assignment, contradicted by the checked certificates for all 12 single and 66 double contractions. Additional checked certificates exclude order at most 294 for the last two twin-pair contraction types. The listed classes directly attain orders 291 and 295; twin contractions transfer them to the odd coordinates.
