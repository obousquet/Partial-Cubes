Result Type: Implication

Statement:

$\mathsf M=\mathsf{Rep}\cap\mathsf{BFrac}$. For k >= 0, $\mathsf{Rep}\subseteq\mathsf E_{k+1}\subseteq\mathsf E_k\subseteq\mathsf{Frac}$, $\mathsf E_0=\mathsf{Frac}$, and $\bigcap_k\mathsf E_k=\mathsf{Rep}$. Also $\mathsf{Frac}\subseteq\mathsf{Pos}_{k+1}\subseteq\mathsf{Pos}_k\subseteq\mathsf{Amp}$, $\bigcap_k\mathsf{Pos}_k=\mathsf{Frac}$ and the inherited author theorem gives $\mathsf{Pos}_2=\mathsf{Amp}$. If every actual cube has dimension at most k, E_k = Rep and Pos_k = Frac on those inputs.

Status: Established

Proof:

The sample constraints and coefficient sign constraints strengthen with k. Once k reaches the finite largest cube dimension, all sample cube budgets are integral ordinary witnesses and all coefficients are constrained nonnegative, respectively. The level-zero coupling and level-two positivity endpoints are imported author results; no new independent review of those imported proofs is claimed.
