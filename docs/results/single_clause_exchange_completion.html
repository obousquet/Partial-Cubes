Every Single-Clause Exchange Has a Canonical Ample Completion and Outside Slice

Name: Every Single-Clause Exchange Has a Canonical Ample Completion and Outside Slice

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Shattered Complex
  • Signed Minimal-Nonface Presentation

Operations:

  • Concept Deletion

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let ample $H_s,H_t\subseteq Q_F$ have common shadow $\Sigma$ and signed presentations differing only on a minimal nonface $I$, with omitted traces $s\ne t$. If $R$ satisfies all unchanged clauses and $R_u$ is its outside slice at $u\in Q_I$, then $R=H_s\cup H_t$ is ample, $A:=R_s=R_t=\bigcap_uR_u$ is nonempty and ample, $Q_I\times A\subseteq R$, and $H_t\setminus H_s=\{s\}\times A$, $H_s\setminus H_t=\{t\}\times A$. Moreover $\operatorname{Sh}(R)\setminus\Sigma=\{I\cup J:J\in\operatorname{Sh}(A)\}$. The admissible omitted traces are exactly $P=\{u:R_u=A\}$, and conversely every ample $R$ and nonempty $I$ with nonempty $A=\bigcap_uR_u$ and $|P|\ge2$ arises this way. For $u\in P$, deleting the slice $\{u\}\times A$ by corners is possible exactly when $A$ is dismantlable ample. If $|I|\ge2$ and some resulting $H_u$ is peelable or a forbidden pc-minor, then $A$ is automatically dismantlable ample.

Status: Established

Proof:

Projection away from any coordinate of $I$ makes the two presentations identical. A difference vertex therefore has every one-bit neighbor in the other family; ampleness of the corresponding $I$-face forces a punctured cube, giving the common outside slice and the two difference formulas. The old shadow together with $I+\operatorname{Sh}(A)$ has cardinality $|R|$, so Sandwich equality proves that $R$ is ample with exactly this shadow. Imposing an omitted trace $u$ deletes $\{u\}\times R_u$; it has the original cardinality precisely when $R_u=A$, which is exactly admissibility. During deletion inside one minimal slice, corner tests factor as the corresponding corner tests in $A$, proving the relative-peeling equivalence. A second admissible slice exposes $A$ as a proper pc-minor when $|I|\ge2$.
