Cubical Bound for Prescribed Extensions

Name: Cubical Bound for Prescribed Extensions

Invariants:

  • Ordering Width
  • Prescribed-Subset Ordering Width
  • Cube-Completion Width
  • Coordinate-Cube Dimension

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Let $\emptyset\ne F\subsetneq\{0,1\}^X$ and $F\subseteq G\subseteq\{0,1\}^X$. After any initial order of $F$, every ordering of $G\setminus F$ has added-step cost at most $d_{\square}(F^{\mathrm c})$. Consequently $\sigma(G;F)\leq\max\{\sigma(F),d_{\square}(F^{\mathrm c})\}$ and $\sigma_{\square}(F)\leq\max\{\sigma(F),d_{\square}(F^{\mathrm c})\}$.

Status: Established

Proof:

At a newly added word $a$, let $S$ be an inclusion-minimal predecessor disagreement. No word obtained from $a$ by flipping a proper subset of $S$ can be a predecessor. After choosing $j\in S$, the coordinate cube based at $a$ and free on $S\setminus\{j\}$ therefore lies in $F^{\mathrm c}$. Hence $|S|-1\leq d_{\square}(F^{\mathrm c})$ for every added step; optimizing the initial order gives both inequalities.

Proof Source:

Comments:

The bound for a general target is sharp: if $F=\{0^n\}$ and $G=\{0^n,1^n\}$ with $n\geq2$, both sides equal $n-1$. This does not make the full-cube completion bound sharp for that $F$, since a cube has a zero-width order starting at $0^n$.
