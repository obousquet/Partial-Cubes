Hall Reverse-Mutation Census

Name: Hall Reverse-Mutation Census

Classes:

  • Maximum Classes
  • Cornerless Ample Classes

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

Hall's 299-concept cornerless maximum class has 40 legal one-word maximum-class exchanges. Their neighbouring classes have two, one, and zero corners in respectively 12, 24, and 4 cases. Every two-corner pair has Hamming distance two, and in every reverse exchange the removed neighbour vertex has degree four and the maximum-class corner-elimination formula returns to Hall's class without creating a corner. For each exchange, adjoining the proposed word before deletion gives an ample 300-word family with 300 shattered supports. In particular, $(C_H\setminus\{2\})\cup\{2049\}$ has corners 1026 and 2050, and the reverse exchange eliminates both.

Status: Established

Proof:

The archived replay starts from the database-owned Hall word list, reconstructs every legal VC-three maximum exchange, and independently recomputes full triple cubes, corner sets, degrees, the birth-and-loss terms in the mutation formula, and each 300-word union shadow. It verifies all 40 rows and the stated histogram; the displayed exchange is one of the twelve two-corner rows.
