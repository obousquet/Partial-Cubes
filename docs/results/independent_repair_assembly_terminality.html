Independent Repair Assemblies Are Minimal Only in Two Layers

Name: Independent Repair Assemblies Are Minimal Only in Two Layers

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes
  • Cornerless Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Partial-Cube Minor

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $S$ be a cornerless, irredundantly embedded ample forbidden pc-minor. For nonempty layer coordinates $E$, choose $T\subseteq Q_E$ and a repair $a_t\in\mathcal R(S)$ in every selected layer, with pairwise distinct tips and acquired supports, and form $X_T=(S\times Q_E)\cup\{(a_t,t):t\in T\}$. Then $X_T$ is a forbidden pc-minor exactly when $|E|=1$ and $T=Q_E$. Thus the opposite-layer two-extension lift is the only forbidden pc-minor among all independent one-tip-per-layer assemblies over a common seed.

Status: Established

Proof:

With one layer coordinate and both layers selected, the assembly is a two-extension lift with distinct supports and hence a forbidden pc-minor. If $T$ omits a layer word $v$, restricting all layer coordinates to $v$ produces the nonpeelable seed $S$ as a proper pc-minor, so the assembly is not minimal outside the dismantlable-ample class. If $T=Q_E$ and $|E|\ge2$, fix all but one layer coordinate. The resulting proper pc-minor is a two-extension lift associated with the repairs at the endpoints of the chosen layer edge; their support labels are distinct, so that minor is itself forbidden. These cases exhaust the possibilities.

Comments:

In particular, neither three tips in three layers of a square nor one tip in every layer of any cube of dimension at least two gives a new minimal obstruction.
