Five-Coordinate Passive-Owner Normal Form for Small Terminal Intervals

Name: Five-Coordinate Passive-Owner Normal Form for Small Terminal Intervals

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Active Cube-Coordinate Count

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $K\subsetneq A\subseteq Q_X$ be ample, put $D=A\setminus K$, and suppose $K\cup\{d\}$ is nonample for every $d\in D$. If the graph induced by $D$ is two-connected and $|D|\le11$, let $Y$ be its active coordinates and $Z=X\setminus Y$. Then $|Y|\le5$, every member of $D$ has one passive restriction $\sigma\in Q_Z$, and the conditioned families $L=\{k|_Y:k\in K,\ k|_Z=\sigma\}$ and $B=L\mathbin{\dot\cup}D$ are ample. The set $R=\{d\in D:L\cup\{d\}\text{ is ample}\}$ is nonempty. For every $d\in R$ there are $z\in Z$ and $c\in D$ such that $c^{\oplus z}\in K$, $d^{\oplus z}\notin K$, and $N_L(d)\cap\operatorname{Diff}(d,c)=\varnothing$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Connectedness makes every coordinate outside $Y$ constant on $D$, and the two-connected coordinate-cost inequality gives $2|Y|\le|D|\le11$. Conditioning $A$ and $K$ at the passive restriction gives the ample pair $L\subseteq B$. If $L$ is empty then $R=D$; otherwise five-coordinate relative peeling ends with an ample singleton extension of $L$, so $R$ is nonempty. For $d\in R$, the one-concept colon criterion makes $d$ teach $L$ but, by terminality, supplies a blocker in $K$. Linear presentation of the relative module gives an interval-exit path from $d$ through $D$ to $K$. Its last edge cannot use an active coordinate, since that would contradict the teaching and blocker conditions. Its passive coordinate and last residual endpoint give $z$ and $c$ with all three asserted properties.
