Geometric Characterizations of Substitution Crossing Width

Name: Geometric Characterizations of Substitution Crossing Width

Invariants:

  • Minimum Teaching Size
  • Substitution Crossing Width

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

For a proper nonempty test $T\subsetneq\{0,1\}^Y$, $g(T)$ is both the largest dimension of a coordinate face meeting one of $T,T^{\mathrm c}$ in exactly one concept and the largest size of an inclusion-minimal disagreement from a concept on one side to the other. Hence $1\leq g(T)\leq|Y|$. For every opposite-side pair $y,z$, there is a word $u$ on the side of $z$ with $\operatorname{diff}(y,u)\subseteq\operatorname{diff}(y,z)$ and $d_H(y,u)\leq g(T)$.

Status: Established

Proof:

A teaching set of size $s$ isolates its concept on a face of dimension $|Y|-s$, and every isolating face gives such a teaching set. A minimal crossing disagreement spans a face with a unique concept on one side; conversely, the antipode of the unique concept in a positive-dimensional isolating face lies on the other side and gives a minimal crossing. A crossing edge supplies the lower bound. Finally, minimize a crossing disagreement inside the support of any specified opposite-side pair.
