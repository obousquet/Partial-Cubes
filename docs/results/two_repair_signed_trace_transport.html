Signed Trace Transport and Recovery for a Two-Repair Lift

Name: Signed Trace Transport and Recovery for a Two-Repair Lift

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Shattered Complex
  • Signed Minimal-Nonface Presentation

Operations:

  • Coordinate Projection

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Under the distinct-support hypotheses of the two-repair ampleness criterion, put $\Sigma=\operatorname{Sh}(S)$ and $\Sigma^+=\Sigma\mathbin{\dot\cup}\{P_a,P_b\}$. The lift has $\operatorname{Sh}(X)=\Sigma^+\mathbin{\dot\cup}\{D\cup\{e\}:D\in\Sigma\}$. Every old-support trace fibre is the disjoint union of its fibres in the two sections, and every trace involving $e$ is the corresponding single section fibre. Its minimal nonfaces are $\mathcal N(\Sigma^+)\mathbin{\dot\cup}\{P_a\cup\{e\},P_b\cup\{e\}\}$; old signs are inherited from $C=S\cup\{a,b\}$, while the two vertical missing traces are $(a|_{P_a},0)$ and $(b|_{P_b},1)$. These signed clauses reconstruct $X$. Conversely, the marked pair $(X,e)$ recovers the two sections, their intersection $S$, both singleton tips, and both supports, uniquely up to reversing $e$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Substituting the exact union and section-intersection shadows into the two-section shattering formula gives the displayed complex, and the trace identities follow directly by separating words according to their $e$-coordinate. An old-coordinate nonface is minimal exactly when it is minimal in $\Sigma^+$. A vertical support $D\cup\{e\}$ is minimally absent exactly when $D$ is one of the two supports added to $\Sigma$, giving the two vertical nonfaces. The trace missing from $S$ on $P_a$ also remains missing from the opposite section, so the lower vertical sign is $(a|_{P_a},0)$; the other sign is symmetric. The fixed-shadow signed-clutter theorem reconstructs the family. Finally, projecting the two marked sections gives $B$ and $A$; their intersection is $S$, their singleton differences are the tips, and adjacency to $S$ recovers each support.
