Independent Repair Tips Give an Exact Component-Label Disjunction

Name: Independent Repair Tips Give an Exact Component-Label Disjunction

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Graph Expansion
  • Concept Deletion
  • Coordinate Conditioning

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $C\subseteq S\subseteq Q_F$ be nonempty ample families, where $C$ is corner-peelable and $S$ is not. Let $a_1,\ldots,a_r$ be distinct points outside $S$, with $r\ge1$, such that $A=S\cup\{a_1,\ldots,a_r\}$ is ample, every $\{a_i\}$ is a component of $A\setminus C$, and every $S\cup\{a_i\}$ is corner-peelable. In the component-label expansion, label every component in $S\setminus C$ by zero and label $\{a_i\}$ by $\varepsilon_i$. Then $X_\varepsilon$ is dismantlable ample exactly when at least one $\varepsilon_i$ is zero. For the all-one labeling, the zero section is the nonpeelable family $S$; hence this negative expansion has a proper nonpeelable section and is not a forbidden pc-minor.

Status: Established

Proof:

Every interpolant $S\cup U$ with $U\subseteq\{a_1,\ldots,a_r\}$ is ample, since it is a section of a component-label expansion. When $U$ is nonempty, delete all but one of its tips and use the assumed peeling of $S\cup\{a_i\}$; thus every such interpolant peels. If some $\varepsilon_i=0$, first delete all tips labeled one. Every intermediate family is an ample expansion over one of these peelable interpolants and the same site $C$. What remains is a peripheral expansion with peelable base and site, so it peels. If all labels are one, conditioning to the zero section gives $S$, and pc-minor closure of dismantlable ampleness rules out a peeling of $X_\varepsilon$.
