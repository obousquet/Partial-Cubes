A Unique Corner in Seven Coordinates Could Only Have Degree Three

Name: A Unique Corner in Seven Coordinates Could Only Have Degree Three

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Result Type: Implication

Statement:

If an ample class using seven coordinates has a unique corner, that corner has degree three. The exclusion of degree four is computer-assisted.

Status: Established

Proof:

Existing prescribed-endpoint and overlap results exclude degrees one and two. Full-cube arguments exclude degrees six and seven, and the codimension-two theorem excludes degree five. For degree four, normalize the root cube as $Q_4\times\{0\}$. A symbolic proper-fiber lemma yields a proper nontrivial three-coordinate fiber at a unit root neighbor. The 62 labelled ample possibilities form 17 coordinate-permutation orbits. For each representative, Lawrence's exact total-asymmetry encoding, unique-corner clauses, full coordinate support, the order bound, and fixed fiber literals give a formula with 10,943 variables and 65,378 clauses. All 17 necessary cases have independently checked DRAT refutations; an eighteenth redundant orbit is also certified.
