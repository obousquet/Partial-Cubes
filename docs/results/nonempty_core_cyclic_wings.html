Nonempty Cores Force Two Large Cyclic Wings

Name: Nonempty Cores Force Two Large Cyclic Wings

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Coordinate Projection

Result Type: Implication

Statement:

Let $H$ be an irredundantly embedded ample forbidden pc-minor for the dismantlable ample classes and suppose its unique-midpoint core is nonempty. At every active coordinate, both section cores relative to the overlap are nonempty, neither section admits a relative ample chain down to the overlap, and both induced wing graphs contain cycles. Each wing has at least nine concepts, so $|H|\ge2|A|+18$. These conclusions hold whether or not the overlap $A$ is corner-peelable. If $A$ is nonpeelable, then $|H|\ge2m_{\mathrm{amp}}+18$, where $m_{\mathrm{amp}}$ is the minimum order of an ample forbidden pc-minor.

Status: Established

Proof:

Both sections are proper pc-minors and therefore corner-peelable, so their ordinary unique-midpoint cores are empty. The one-sided criterion forces both relative cores to be nonempty and excludes a relative ample chain on either side. Irredundancy makes the overlap nonempty, and the gluing dichotomy makes it ample. The relative Yang modules are Cohen--Macaulay; the relative forest and eight-facet linear-quotient theorems would produce a relative ample chain if a wing were a forest or had at most eight concepts. Thus both wings contain cycles and have order at least nine. The section identity gives $|H|=2|A|+|D_0|+|D_1|$. If $A$ is nonpeelable, inherited overlap descent makes it an ample forbidden pc-minor, yielding $|A|\ge m_{\mathrm{amp}}$.

The certificate replays both wing conditions at all coordinates of Hall's 299-concept family and its 300-concept extension. These are necessary conditions on a terminal counterexample with nonempty core, not a classification.
