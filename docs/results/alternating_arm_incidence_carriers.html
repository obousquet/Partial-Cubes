One Signed Carrier Object Covers the Three Alternating Support Graphs

Name: One Signed Carrier Object Covers the Three Alternating Support Graphs

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

For four acquired direction pairs whose individual directions are shattered by the common section, form the bipartite incidence graph $J$ on the two pairs of each wing, joining opposite-wing pairs that share a direction. If the four pairs have no three-edge matching, then $J$ is $K_{2,2}$, $P_4$, or $2K_2$. Every edge of $J$ carries the two omitted trace labels, a forced common carrier slice, and, for distinct arm occurrences, a square-transport path avoiding both partner directions.

Status: Established

Proof:

Same-wing pairs are disjoint, so two opposite-wing pairs meet in at most one direction and $J$ is a simple subgraph of $K_{2,2}$. The three alternating support graphs have incidence shadows $K_{2,2}$, $P_4$, and $2K_2$. Applying crossed-pair carrier freezing to every incidence edge supplies its signed omitted traces, conditioned carrier, and square transport.
