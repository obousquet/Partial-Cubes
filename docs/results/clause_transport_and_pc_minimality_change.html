Clause Transport and Change of pc-Minimality

Name: Clause Transport and Change of pc-Minimality

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Full Reduction
  • Partial-Cube Minor

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

Fix a coordinate set $D$ and a simplicial complex $\Sigma\subseteq2^D$ containing every singleton. Apply the same reduced-base construction to every ample $K\subseteq Q_D$ with $0\in K$ and $\operatorname{Sh}(K)=\Sigma$. All outputs $X_K$ have the same shadow. If $P_0$ is the fixed repair support and $I$ is a minimal nonface of $\Sigma$, the inherited clause is $\sigma_{X_K}(P_0\cup I)=(a_0|_{P_0},\sigma_K(I))$; every other output clause is independent of $K$. The map $K\mapsto X_K$ is injective and preserves both the number of changed clauses and total changed sign-bit distance between two inputs. Every output is nonpeelable, and $X_K$ is a forbidden pc-minor exactly when $K$ is dismantlable ample. Consequently, two nonpeelable ample families with the same shadow can differ in one bit of one clause while exactly one is pc-minimal, even when both have minimum degree at least four, distinct signed columns up to sign, and complete crossing graph.

Status: Established

Proof:

The variable occurrence row is $R_0=L\times\{0_z\}$ with $L=(K\times Q_{\{w\}})\vee_0Q_{\{p,q\}}$. Its minimal nonfaces are those of $\Sigma$, the cross pairs $\{t,p\},\{t,q\}$ for $t\in D\cup\{w\}$, and $\{z\}$. Only the first group depends on $K$, and its missing traces are precisely $\sigma_K(I)$; the cross-pair and constant-coordinate traces are fixed. The replicated-repair formulas prepend the same seed trace to each input clause and fix all other output clauses, proving the transport and distance assertions. A fixed proper face recovers $K$, giving injectivity. Reduced-base universality gives nonpeelability for every output and forbiddenness exactly for peelable inputs. Applying this to the adjacent 1188-concept pair whose only changed clause has support 6531 and traces 2306 and 2307 yields two order-323,196,291,401 outputs in dimension 42 and VC dimension 33; their 1589-clause presentations differ in one bit of one ten-coordinate clause, while only the output from the peelable input is forbidden.

Comments:

The certificate replays the positive input peeling, all 78 elementary proper-minor orders of the negative input, two rooted obstructions, the actual input shadows and clauses, and both complete 1,589-clause output presentations. The order-323,196,291,401 outputs are specified compactly and are not materialized; their nonpeelability and minimality statuses use the uniform theorem rather than an output-minor enumeration.
