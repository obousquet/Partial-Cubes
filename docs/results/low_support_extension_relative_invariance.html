Low-Support One-Concept Additions Preserve Every Relative Peeling

Name: Low-Support One-Concept Additions Preserve Every Relative Peeling

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Concept Deletion
  • Full Reduction

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Let $S$ be nonempty and ample, and let $H=S\cup\{a\}$ be an ample one-concept extension whose acquired support $I_S(a)$ has size at most two. For every retained set $T\subseteq S$, including the empty set, $H$ corner-reduces to $T$ if and only if $S$ corner-reduces to $T$. Consequently $H$ is dismantlable ample exactly when $S$ is, and every old prescribed peeling endpoint is preserved. Any one-concept addition that makes a previously unreachable retained target reachable must acquire at least three directions.

Status: Established

Proof:

One direction deletes the new corner $a$ first. For the converse, take a smallest counterexample and let $z\ne a$ be the first deletion in a reduction of $H$. If $z$ is also a corner of $S$, delete it from both families and use minimality. Otherwise the unique maximal cube of $z$ in $H$ contains $a$ and equals the acquired cube. The two vertices cannot be adjacent, so the support has size two and $a,z$ are opposite vertices of a square with the same two neighbors. Swapping them is an automorphism fixing $T$ and transports the remaining reduction to one of $S$, a contradiction.
