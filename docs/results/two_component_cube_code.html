Two Complementary Components Force Antipodal Cube Rows

Name: Two Complementary Components Force Antipodal Cube Rows

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate-Block Replacement
  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Full Reduction

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $A\subseteq C$ be dismantlable ample, suppose $C\setminus A$ has exactly two connected components $K_0,K_1$, and assume the two-layer family $H=(A\times\{0,1\})\cup(K_0\times\{0\})\cup(K_1\times\{1\})$ is an ample forbidden pc-minor. For a nonempty new coordinate block $E$, form $Z$ by retaining every word over $A$ and deleting precisely $q_i\in Q_E$ over each word of $K_i$. Then $Z$ is forbidden exactly when $q_0,q_1$ are antipodal; in that case it is a coordinate-block replacement of $H$ up to reorientation and renaming. If $q_0=q_1$, then $Z$ is dismantlable ample. If they are distinct and nonantipodal, $Z$ has a nonpeelable proper coordinate-face restriction and is not forbidden.

Status: Established

Proof:

For antipodal omitted words, reorient the block so they are $1_E,0_E$; the displayed family is exactly the coordinate-block replacement of $H$. For equal words, reorient their common value to $0_E$; the family becomes the block replacement of the peripheral family $(A,C)$, which is dismantlable ample. Otherwise choose block coordinates $e,f$ on which the omitted words respectively agree and disagree. Restrict to their common $e$-value and reduce every remaining block coordinate except $f$. The full fibre over $A$ survives, while the fibres over $K_0,K_1$ retain opposite $f$-values, producing $H$ up to reorientation. This active proper face has a negative reduction, so $Z$ cannot be forbidden.
