Exact Ample-Preserving Chunk Deletion Criterion

Name: Exact Ample-Preserving Chunk Deletion Criterion

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Ample Chunk-Peeling Width

Operations:

  • Concept Deletion

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

For $T\in\operatorname{Sh}(C)$ and $a\in\{0,1\}^T$, let $F_{T,a}=\{c\in C:c|_T=a\}$ and let $L_C(S)$ be the shattered coordinate sets having some fiber contained in $S$. If $C$ is ample, then $C\setminus S$ is ample exactly when $|L_C(S)|=|S|$. In that case $S$ is a union of contained fibers, and $\chi(C)=\min_{\varnothing\ne S, |L_C(S)|=|S|}\max\{|S|,\chi(C\setminus S)\}$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Deleting concepts cannot create shattered sets, and a shattered set $T$ disappears exactly when one full fiber $F_{T,a}$ is deleted. Thus $\operatorname{Sh}(C\setminus S)=\operatorname{Sh}(C)\setminus L_C(S)$. Combine $|C|=|\operatorname{Sh}(C)|$ with the shattering inequality for the remainder to obtain $|L_C(S)|\le|S|$, with equality exactly for an ample remainder. The same inequality applied after restoring any point not covered by a contained fiber proves the union assertion. Splitting a peeling at its first deletion gives the recurrence in both directions.
