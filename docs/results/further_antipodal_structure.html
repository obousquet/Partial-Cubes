Further Structure of Antipodal Partial Cubes

Name: Further Structure of Antipodal Partial Cubes

Classes:

  • Antipodal Partial Cubes
  • Even Cycles

Invariants:

  • Isometric Dimension
  • Graph Girth

Result Type: Other

Statement:

A bipartite antipodal graph is a partial cube exactly when it is interval monotone; it suffices to test intervals of length at least $\operatorname{diam}(G)-1$. In an antipodal partial cube, an isometric cycle is principal exactly when its diameter, equivalently its isometric dimension, equals that of the graph, also exactly when its convex hull is the whole graph. For order $n$ and diameter $d$, equality $n=4d-4$ holds exactly for $C_4$ and prisms over even cycles. Every antipodal partial cube of girth greater than six is an even cycle; if a noncycle has diameter at least six, then its girth is smaller than its diameter.

Status: Established
