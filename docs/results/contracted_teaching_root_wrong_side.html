Wrong-Side Witnesses Localize on Relative Edges

Name: Wrong-Side Witnesses Localize on Relative Edges

Classes:

  • Partial Cubes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Projection

Result Type: Implication

Statement:

Let $C\subsetneq A\subseteq Q_E$ with $A$ isometric, contract coordinate $e$, and put $D=A\setminus C$. Suppose $d\in D$ is the unique relative member of its $e$-fibre and its image $y$ lies in $\pi_eA\setminus\pi_eC$. If $y$ teaches $\pi_eC$ but $d$ does not teach $C$, then some $c\in C$ has the following property: every contracted teaching direction $f$ that separates $y$ from $c$ satisfies $d^{\oplus f}\in D$ and $d^{\oplus\{e,f\}}\in C$. Thus every witness lost on lifting runs along a relative edge and completes the opposite square into the lower family.

Status: Established

Proof:

The other lift $d^{\oplus e}$ lies outside $A$. Since $d$ does not teach $C$, choose $c\in C$ with no $C$-neighbor of $d$ in a differing direction. For a contracted teaching direction $f$ separating $y$ from $c$, one lift over $y^{\oplus f}$ lies in $C$; the choice of $c$ rules out $d^{\oplus f}$, so the opposite vertex $d^{\oplus\{e,f\}}$ lies in $C$. Isometry supplies a two-edge path in $A$ from $d$ to that opposite vertex. Its other possible intermediate vertex $d^{\oplus e}$ is absent, forcing $d^{\oplus f}\in A\setminus C=D$.
