The Two-Unit Holonomy Budget Has Exactly Seven Profiles

Name: The Two-Unit Holonomy Budget Has Exactly Seven Profiles

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

For a directed two-cycle with excess $\varepsilon=2$, and up to interchanging its shores $S_1,S_2$, exactly seven profiles occur: (1) $\eta=2$ with disjoint two-concept shores; (2) $\eta=1$ with two two-concept shores meeting once; (3) $\eta=1$ with disjoint shores of orders two and three; (4) $\eta=0$ with shores of orders two and three meeting once; (5) $\eta=0$ with disjoint shores of orders two and four; (6) $\eta=0$ with disjoint three-concept shores; or (7) $\eta=0$ with disjoint two-concept cycle shores and a third passive coordinate having a singleton shore disjoint from their union. In profiles 1--6 there are exactly two passive directions, each cycle shore has a unique representative, and all other passive shores are empty. Precisely profiles 1--3 have an uncounted lower vertex.

Status: Established

Proof:

The exact excess identity writes $2$ as the sum of the nonnegative integers $\eta$, the cost of passive directions beyond two, the carrier-incidence excesses, and the two shore-size excesses. Extra passive directions cost at least two units, so there are at most three. Equality with three forces profile 7. With two directions, both cycle shores have unique representatives and the carrier excess equals $a=|S_1\cap S_2|$. Writing $x=|S_1|-2$ and $y=|S_2|-2$ reduces the identity to $\eta+a+x+y=2$. Enumerating its nonnegative solutions, excluding $a=2,x=y=0$ because the two shores must be distinct, gives profiles 1--6 and shows that exactly the first three have $\eta>0$.
