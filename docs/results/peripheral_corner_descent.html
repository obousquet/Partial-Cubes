Cornerlessness Descends through Peripheral Coordinates

Name: Cornerlessness Descends through Peripheral Coordinates

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Cornerless Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Full Reduction

Result Type: Implication

Statement:

Write an ample class with an active peripheral coordinate as $C=(B\times\{0\})\cup(S\times\{1\})$ with $\varnothing\ne S\subseteq B$. Then $S$ is ample, and $(s,1)$ is a corner of $C$ exactly when $s$ is a corner of $S$. In particular, a cornerless $C$ has a nonempty cornerless ample reduction $S$ on one fewer coordinate.

Status: Established

Proof:

The site $S$ is the full reduction in the peripheral coordinate and is ample. Every cube through $(s,1)$ extends across that coordinate because its lower copy lies in $B$. Thus the maximal cubes through $(s,1)$ are precisely $Q\times\{0,1\}$ for maximal cubes $Q$ of $S$ through $s$, preserving their number and hence the corner condition.
