Two Forced Deletions at a Rank-Three Neighbor

Name: Two Forced Deletions at a Rank-Three Neighbor

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Concept Deletion

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $S\subseteq Q_F$ be ample and nonpeelable with unique corner $c$, whose incident-coordinate set $I$ has size at least three. Suppose $u=c^{\{i\}}\notin S$ and $H=S\cup\{u\}$ is ample with a three-coordinate acquired support. Then that support is $P=J\cup\{i\}$ for a two-element proper subset $J\subsetneq I$. Put $x=u^J$ and $y=c^J$. If $H$ peels, every peeling starts by deleting $x$ and then $y$. Thus $x$ has no neighbor outside the completed $P$-cube, and $x$ is the only neighbor of $y$ in a direction outside $I$. Consequently, $u$ is not resolving if another adjacent ample addition $v=c^{\{j\}}$ has acquired support $K\cup\{j\}$ with $J\subseteq K\subseteq I$.

Status: Established

Proof:

The punctured-cube test gives $i\in P$, $i\notin I$, and $P\setminus\{i\}=J\subsetneq I$. In $H$, the old corner $c$ is blocked by any direction in $I\setminus J$. Any other old corner must lie in the completed $P$-cube and have no neighbor outside it. All candidates except $x$ retain an outside neighbor because $c$ was the unique corner of $S$; hence the only possible first corners are $u$ and $x$. Deleting $u$ returns nonpeelable $S$, so every peeling starts at $x$. Only neighbors of $x$ can become new corners. Two retain incompatible outside neighbors and $u$ loses a required cube vertex, leaving $y$ as the sole possible second deletion. The full $I$-cube at $y$ shows it becomes a corner exactly when its only outside-$I$ neighbor is $x$. A companion addition with $J\subseteq K$ supplies the distinct outside neighbor $v^J=y^{\{j\}}$, contradicting this necessary condition.

The controls verify both the forced two-deletion case and the companion-blocked case, and replay fifteen recorded rank-three neighbors. The constructed controls have proper nonpeelable minors and are local proof checks rather than forbidden witnesses.
