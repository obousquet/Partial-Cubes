Global Minimality of the Pentagonal Obstruction

Name: Global Minimality of the Pentagonal Obstruction

Classes:

  • Convex-Treelike Partial Cubes

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

The pentagonal obstruction has the smallest isometric dimension among the ample members of the complete basis, is the unique additional obstruction of dimension five and order at most twenty-one, and no additional obstruction has fewer than sixteen vertices.

Status: Established

Proof:

Exact coordinate-isomorphism enumeration excludes additional types through dimension four. In dimension five through order twenty-one it produces 14,259 partial-cube representatives and 3,115 periphery-free representatives, with $\mathcal P_5$ the unique additional minimal type. Exact searches in dimensions six through twelve exclude ample periphery-free families below order sixteen; the low-excess lemma and the dimension/order bound cover all remaining dimensions.
