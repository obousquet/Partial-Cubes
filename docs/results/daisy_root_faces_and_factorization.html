Root Faces and Canonical Cube Factors of Daisy Cubes

Name: Root Faces and Canonical Cube Factors of Daisy Cubes

Classes:

  • Daisy Cubes

Operations:

  • Cartesian Product

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

If $K\subseteq2^E$ is a nonempty down-set, then its roots are exactly $2^{\operatorname{Free}(K)}$. Hence two reoriented nonempty down-sets $K$ and $\delta\triangle L$ admit a common root exactly when $\delta\subseteq\operatorname{Free}(K)\cup\operatorname{Free}(L)$. More generally, every nonempty daisy representation $D$ has a unique root $r_0$ disjoint from $F=\operatorname{Free}(D)$ and a unique nonempty single-root down-set $U$ on $E\setminus F$ such that $D=r_0\triangle(2^F\times U)$; its full root set is $r_0\triangle2^F$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Toggling a free coordinate preserves a down-set. Conversely, if a coordinate belongs to a translating root, downward closure before and after translation forces both values to occur together, so that coordinate is free. The common-root condition is intersection of the two Boolean root faces. Factoring membership over all free coordinates yields $2^F\times U$; removing the free part from any root gives the unique distinguished root and leaves $U$ with no free coordinate and only the empty root.
