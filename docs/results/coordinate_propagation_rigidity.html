Connected Coordinate Propagation Forces the Canonical Face Family

Name: Connected Coordinate Propagation Forces the Canonical Face Family

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Shattered Complex
  • Signed Minimal-Nonface Presentation

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $\Sigma$ be a two-dimensional simplicial complex with complete one-skeleton and at most two triangular faces on every four-set. For each coordinate $e$, join two minimal nonfaces containing $e$ when their four-coordinate missing-triple configuration forces their omitted traces to agree at $e$. If each resulting propagation graph is connected whenever nonempty, then every ample family with shattered complex $\Sigma$ is a cube translate of the canonical face family $\{\mathbf1_D:D\in\Sigma\}$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Four-coordinate compatibility equates the omitted-trace values along every propagation edge. Connectivity therefore gives one bit $h_e$ shared by every minimal nonface containing $e$. The complete signing is the restriction of the word $h$ to each minimal nonface. Translating by $h+\mathbf1_F$ changes every omitted trace to all ones, whose avoidance family consists exactly of the indicator words of faces of $\Sigma$.
