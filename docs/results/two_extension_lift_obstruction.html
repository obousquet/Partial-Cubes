Two-Extension Lifts Are Ample Forbidden PC-Minors

Name: Two-Extension Lifts Are Ample Forbidden PC-Minors

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Graph Expansion
  • Concept Deletion
  • Partial-Cube Minor

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

Let $S\in\operatorname{Obs}(\mathcal D_{\mathrm{amp}})\cap\mathcal A$ be irredundantly embedded in $Q_F$, and let distinct absent concepts $a,b$ make $A=S\cup\{a\}$ and $B=S\cup\{b\}$ dismantlable ample. If their unique acquired supports $P_a$ and $P_b$ are distinct, then for a new coordinate $e$ the opposite-layer lift $X=(B\times\{0\})\cup(A\times\{1\})$ is an ample forbidden pc-minor for dismantlable ampleness. Moreover, if every old corner $z$ of $B$ has $Q_B(z)\nsubseteq A$ and every old corner $z$ of $A$ has $Q_A(z)\nsubseteq B$, then the only corners of $X$ are $(b,0)$ and $(a,1)$.

Status: Established

Proof:

The distinct-support criterion makes the two-tip union $S\cup\{a,b\}$ and the lift $X$ ample. The exclusive tips cannot be adjacent, since that would create a square face containing only its diagonal vertices. Each section peels to $S$ by deleting its added corner, so the one-relatively-peelable-wing inverse theorem makes $X$ a forbidden pc-minor. Each exclusive tip remains a corner because it has no vertical mate. For an old concept $z\in S$, its lower lift is a corner exactly when $z$ is a corner of $B$ and its unique maximal corner cube lies in the opposite section $A$; the upper criterion is symmetric. The two noncontainment hypotheses therefore exclude every additional corner.
