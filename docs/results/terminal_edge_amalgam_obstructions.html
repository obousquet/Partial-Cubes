Terminal Obstructions from an Edge Amalgam

Name: Terminal Obstructions from an Edge Amalgam

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Partial-Cube Minor

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

Let $C\subseteq Q_F$ be irredundantly embedded, two-connected, ample, corner-peelable, and have $0$ as its only corner. Fix an edge $D=\{0,0^{\{e\}}\}$. Assume that for every $f\ne e$, the zero restriction and contraction of $C$ peel to their copies of $D$; all three elementary images in coordinate $e$ peel to the singleton image of $D$; and distinct tips $a,b$ give ample extensions $C\cup\{a\},C\cup\{b\}$ that peel to $D$ and acquire distinct supports. Take two copies $C_1,C_2$ whose coordinate sets meet only in $e$ and whose copies of $D$ are identified. With new coordinates $p,q$, put $X=C_2\cup(C_1\times(Q_{\{p,q\}}\setminus\{00\}))\cup\{(a_1,10),(b_1,01)\}$, with $C_2$ in the $00$ section. Then $X$ is a two-connected, corner-descent-terminal ample forbidden pc-minor of order $4|C|+2$ and dimension $2|F|+1$, and its only corners are $(a_1,10)$ and $(b_1,01)$. If $|F|\ge4$ and $e$ lies in at least three minimal nonfaces of $\operatorname{Sh}(C)$, no coordinate of $X$ is balanced, so $X$ has no successful common-seed layer set even when cornered recovered seeds are allowed.

Status: Established

Proof:

Shadow decomposition first shows that the punctured three-layer piece is ample; its union with the second copy along $D$ remains ample because mixed supports across the exclusive coordinate blocks are not shattered. The prescribed relative orders combine with product peelings to peel every restriction and contraction of $X$. Away from the two repair tips, the unique-corner property of $C$, the three-layer product, and the two exclusive blocks prevent every candidate corner cube; the two tips remain corners even after either is removed. Thus corner deletion stops at a nonempty cornerless family, so $X$ is nonpeelable, while the elementary-minor peelings make it forbidden. After either tip deletion, one restriction and one contraction expose the cornerless amalgam $C_1\cup_D C_2$ as a proper nonpeelable minor, proving terminality. The punctured product and attachment of the two pieces along an edge preserve two-connectivity. Under the extra hypotheses, every coordinate belongs to at least three minimal nonfaces: exclusive coordinates lie in at least three missing cross-pairs, while the common coordinate retains three minimal nonfaces from $C_2$. Hence no coordinate has the two-clause profile required for a successful recovered layer.

Comments:

The construction gives two-connected terminal graph three-cores for which layer recovery does not exist. It assumes relative peeling certificates for the input family and its two repairs; intrinsic admissibility of those inputs remains a separate problem.
