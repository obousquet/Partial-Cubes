Algebraic and Topological Form of an Ample One-Facet Attachment

Name: Algebraic and Topological Form of an Ample One-Facet Attachment

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Result Type: Invariant Law

Statement:

Let $A=S\cup\{a\}\subseteq Q_F$ be ample and put $I=\{e:a^{\{e\}}\in S\}$. For the complementary-literal generator $m_a$ and new Yang facet $F_a$, one has $J_S:m_a=(v_{e,a_e}:e\in I)$ and $2^{F_a}\cap\Delta_S=\partial2^{\{v_{e,a_e}:e\in I\}}*2^{\{v_{e,a_e}:e\in F\setminus I\}}$. Equivalently, the intersection is the union of the ridges $F_a\setminus\{v_{e,a_e}\}$ for $e\in I$. If $S$ is an ample forbidden pc-minor for dismantlable ampleness, then $\varnothing\ne I\subsetneq F$ and this intersection is a shellable $(|F|-2)$-ball.

Status: Established

Proof:

Every $a$--$x$ geodesic in the isometric extension starts in a direction $e\in I$, so the monomial quotient for $x$ is divisible by $v_{e,a_e}$; the neighbour in direction $e$ gives the reverse divisibility and the colon formula. A face of $F_a$ lies in the old Yang complex exactly when it omits one of these literals, proving the ridge union. Connectedness gives $I\ne\varnothing$, while $I=F$ would make $S$ a punctured cube and hence corner-peelable. The displayed join is therefore a shellable ball.
