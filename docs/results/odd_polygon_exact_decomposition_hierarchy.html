Odd Polygon Complements Separate Every Decomposition Level

Name: Odd Polygon Complements Separate Every Decomposition Level

Classes:

  • Realizable Ample Classes
  • Maximum Classes
  • Both-Sides Dismantlable Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Yang Decomposition Level

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

For every $m\ge2$, the regular $(2m+1)$-tangent sign class $H_{2m+1}$ and its complement $C_{2m+1}$ are realizable maximum classes in the both-sides dismantlable family, with $\kappa(H_{2m+1})=1$ and $\kappa(C_{2m+1})=m-1$. Hence every consecutive inclusion $\mathcal D_k\subsetneq\mathcal D_{k+1}$ is strict, realizable and both-sides dismantlable classes have unbounded decomposition level, and no $\mathcal D_k$ for $k\ge1$ is cube-complement closed.

Status: Established

Proof:

The arrangement has the Sauer number of regions, all two-coordinate traces, and one missing trace on every triple, so it is maximum of rank two; maximum complementation and Lawrence duality handle its complement. Cyclic-interval geometry proves the dominant-shore lower bound. A sequence adjoining the length-$(m+1)$ coordinate cubes has recursively peripheral links described by endpoint-interval classes and gives the matching upper decomposition. Substituting $m=k+2$ yields every strict step, while complementing the rank-two shore proves failure of complement closure.
