Disconnected Pure Two-Apex Incidence Has One Path Falsifier

Name: Disconnected Pure Two-Apex Incidence Has One Path Falsifier

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

In the same two-decomposition pure $\eta=2$ setting, suppose the four terminal supports form two disjoint two-edge paths. For each decomposition $j$, let $O_j$ contain every word obtained by correcting the unique passive coordinate of a corner at passive distance one from its centre. Then either $O_0\cap O_1\ne\varnothing$ or $(O_0\cup O_1)\cap B\ne\varnothing$, or $G(B)=P_9$. In the path exception, up to reversal and swapping decompositions, the counted spines are $v_0\cdots v_6$ and $v_2\cdots v_8$, centred at $v_3,v_5$, and their inner arms share the collar support $\{x_4,x_5\}$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Continue the exact normal-form audit through the 42 labelled $2P_3$ comparisons. For every distance-one corner, compute its forced correcting word. Thirty-six comparisons have a word forced by both decompositions or already present in $B$, giving 20 embedded types. The remaining six labelled comparisons, or three embedded types, are all the stated $P_9$ role-reversal form. Reading the two overlapping $P_7$ direction sequences shows that the right inner arm of the first and left inner arm of the second both have collar pair $\{x_4,x_5\}$; no further comparison remains.
