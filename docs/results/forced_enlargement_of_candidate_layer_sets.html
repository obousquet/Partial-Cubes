Forced Enlargement of Candidate Layer Sets

Name: Forced Enlargement of Candidate Layer Sets

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Projection

Result Type: Algorithm

Statement:

Let $X$ be nonempty and ample, let $\mathcal B(X)$ be the coordinates occurring in exactly two signed minimal-nonface clauses with opposite signs, and start from nonempty $E_0\subseteq\mathcal B(X)$. For a current $E$, form every mixed-clause trace fibre $T_J^E$ in the old-coordinate projection and let $W(E)$ be the union of the coordinates on which words within one fibre vary. Fail if $W(E)\not\subseteq\mathcal B(X)$ or if two distinct mixed-clause fibres intersect; succeed and return $E$ if $W(E)=\varnothing$; otherwise replace $E$ by $E\cup W(E)$ and repeat. The procedure terminates in at most $|\mathcal B(X)|-|E_0|+1$ passes. On success it returns the unique smallest superset of $E_0$ satisfying the distinct-singleton recovery criterion; on failure no such superset exists. Successful layer sets are closed under nonempty intersections. Starting from every singleton in $\mathcal B(X)$ decides whether any successful nonempty layer set exists. Each failure has an explicit two-word outside-coordinate certificate or a two-clause common-word certificate.

Status: Established

Proof:

For $E\subseteq D\subseteq\mathcal B(X)$, projection maps every fibre $T_J^E$ into $T_J^D$. Therefore any successful $D$ must contain every coordinate on which a fibre at $E$ varies, and an intersection between two fibres at $E$ persists after projection, contradicting distinct singleton fibres. Inductively, every successful superset of $E_0$ contains every set generated by the procedure, while either failure excludes all such supersets. Each nonterminal successful pass adds a balanced coordinate. At termination every nonempty fibre has no varying coordinate and is therefore a singleton, and the collision test makes the singletons distinct. This proves termination, correctness, minimality, and uniqueness. Running from a member of any successful set must succeed; applying minimality to a nonempty intersection of successful sets proves intersection closure.

Comments:

The exhaustive certificate checks every nonempty ample family through dimension four, including forced growth and both failure-certificate types, and separately checks the Hall control. This theorem recognizes singleton recovery and does not require the recovered seed to be cornerless.
