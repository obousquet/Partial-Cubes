Maximum Parent for Opposite Consecutive Triples

Name: Maximum Parent for Opposite Consecutive Triples

Classes:

  • Maximum-Generated Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Fiber Product

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

Let $A_0\subseteq A_1\subseteq A_2$ be maximum of ranks $r,r+1,r+2$ and $B_0\subseteq B_1\subseteq B_2$ maximum of ranks $s,s+1,s+2$ on disjoint private blocks. The three path fibers $A_0\times B_2$ at $00$, $A_1\times B_1$ at $10$, and $A_2\times B_0$ at $11$ have a maximum parent of rank $r+s+2$ with at most $r+s+1$ auxiliary coordinates. With common private marks of sizes $t_X,t_Y$, the same rank is retained with at most $\max(r,t_X)+\max(s,t_Y)+1$ auxiliaries.

Status: Established

Proof:

Extend both triples to maximum sequences and pad their marked faces so entry levels on each face are exactly the three consecutive ranks. Over a pair of entry levels attach one of an explicit full cube, punctured cube, one of two three-edge paths, a singleton, or the empty class on three control coordinates. These control fibers nest with increasing levels. Vandermonde gives the rank-$(r+s+2)$ Sauer cardinality, while missing traces in the two private sequences bound the VC dimension. Fixing the final auxiliary control recovers precisely the three path fibers.
