Every Ample Obstruction Has a Canonical Graph Three-Core

Name: Every Ample Obstruction Has a Canonical Graph Three-Core

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Shattered Complex

Operations:

  • Concept Deletion
  • Partial-Cube Minor

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

In an ample pc-minimal obstruction to dismantlable ampleness, every degree-two vertex is a corner. Repeatedly deleting vertices of degree below three, in any order, performs only degree-two corner deletions and ends at a nonempty ample pc-minimal obstruction $K$. This $K$ is the unique largest induced subgraph of minimum degree at least three, independent of all choices. The original family and $K$ have the same active coordinates, the same shattered supports of size at least three, and the same VC dimension. Conversely, the original family is recovered from $K$ by ample one-concept square completions, and every such sequence from any minimum-degree-three ample obstruction preserves obstruction status and has that starting family as its three-core.

Status: Established

Proof:

In any ample family a degree-two vertex is either a corner or a cut vertex: if its two directions realize the opposite trace, ampleness supplies the square; otherwise the two nonempty shores disconnect after deleting the vertex. A cut vertex of an obstruction belongs to two two-connected rooted factors and has degree at least four. Periphery-freeness excludes leaves, so every low-degree deletion is a degree-two corner. Low-degree corner descent preserves pc-minimal obstruction status until minimum degree three is reached. Any induced subgraph of minimum degree three survives every such process, proving uniqueness and maximality. A degree-two corner deletion removes exactly its two-support from the shadow, preserving active coordinates and all supports of size at least three; VC dimension is at least three and remains fixed. Reversing the deletions gives square completions, whose local extension and rank-two invariance theorems preserve ampleness, nonpeelability, and minor minimality.
