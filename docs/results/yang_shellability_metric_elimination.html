Yang Shellability Equals Metric Elimination

Name: Yang Shellability Equals Metric Elimination

Classes:

  • Partial Cubes
  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Concept Deletion

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let nonempty $H\subseteq Q_F$ be isometric and let $G_H$ be its induced concept graph. The following are equivalent: the Yang complex $\Delta_H$ is shellable; it is strongly shellable; $G_H$ has an elimination order whose every nonempty suffix is isometric in $G_H$; and $G_H$ has an elimination order in which every deleted vertex is weakly $4$-simplicial, meaning that every two of its current neighbours remain at distance at most two after its deletion. Every isometric family satisfying these conditions is ample and corner-peelable. Its distance-preserving elimination orders are exactly its corner-peeling orders, and their reversals are the corresponding Yang shellings.

Status: Established

Proof:

For Yang facets, the pure shelling pairwise criterion automatically satisfies the extra containment required for strong shellability because each facet contains exactly one literal of every color. Thus shellability and strong shellability coincide. The codimension-one graph of the Yang complex is $G_H$, facet distance is Hamming distance, and isometry makes this graph harmonious; the strong-shellability graph theorem therefore gives distance-preserving elimination. A vertex deletion preserves all distances exactly when the vertex is weakly $4$-simplicial. Finally, reverse a distance-preserving order from its last singleton. Each successive cube-family extension is isometric, so the one-concept extension criterion preserves ampleness and adds a corner. Conversely every suffix of a corner peeling is ample and therefore isometric.
