Exact Root-Section Square-Row Criterion for Adjacent Endpoint Pivots

Name: Exact Root-Section Square-Row Criterion for Adjacent Endpoint Pivots

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Invariants:

  • Shattered Complex
  • Signed Minimal-Nonface Presentation

Operations:

  • Concept Deletion
  • Partial-Cube Minor

Result Type: Recognition

Statement:

Let $(B,0)$ be a rooted factor using every coordinate with unique corner zero, let $\alpha,\delta,\gamma$ be three compatible ample additions with distinct acquired supports and root-ending orders, and let a coordinate face $D$ contain zero and the punctured $\gamma$-cube while omitting a root neighbor. Form $Y_R$ over a fresh square by placing $D$ at $00$, the $\alpha$- and $\delta$-extensions at $01,10$, $B$ at $11$, and copies of $\gamma$ on row $R\subseteq Q_2$. Then $Y_R$ is ample exactly when $R$ is not a diagonal pair; for every ample row, $\operatorname{Sh}(Y_R)=\operatorname{Sh}(Y_\varnothing)\mathbin{\dot\cup}\{P_\gamma\cup J:J\in\operatorname{Sh}(R)\}$. Every such family is two-connected and ordinarily peelable, and all proper root-preserving pc-minors peel. It peels to $(0,00)$ exactly when $R$ contains $\{01,11\}$ or $\{10,11\}$. Thus eight of fourteen ample rows are rooted factors and six are endpoint-positive. For the four punctured-square rows, exactly omission $11$ is a rooted factor and the other three peel, yielding adjacent opposite-status signs with the same shadow.

Status: Established

Proof:

Build the empty-row family from the paired rooted factor by reversing a peeling of $D$; each new support lifts through the other three core copies. Adding a row corner at $\gamma$ acquires the disjoint union of its old support and row support, giving the shadow formula; diagonal pairs create three new shattered supports with only two vertices and are nonample. Active-coordinate additions preserve ordinary peelability and positivity of proper rooted minors. If neither required row edge occurs, any first deletion from a nonroot core copy would force an old corner of $B$, hence zero, but its fresh-square and omitted-neighbor directions demand an absent $(h,00)$ vertex. If a required edge occurs, clear any $00$ tip and $D$, then apply the partial-tip endpoint theorem. Attachment degrees preserve two-connectivity. The single-clause completion theorem identifies the four punctured-square signs.
