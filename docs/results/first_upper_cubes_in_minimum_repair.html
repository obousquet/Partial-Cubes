First Upper-Section Cubes in a Minimum Rank-Three Repair

Name: First Upper-Section Cubes in a Minimum Rank-Three Repair

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Concept Deletion
  • Coordinate Conditioning

Result Type: Implication

Statement:

In a minimum adjacent rank-three repair, normalize the switch root to $a=0$ and its marked corners to $c=\{p\}$ and $z=\{q\}$. A successful peeling can be chosen to begin $z$, $v=\{q,r\}$, and $x=\{q,r,e\}$. There are nonempty sets $J,F$, disjoint from $\{p,q,r\}$, with $e\in J$ and $F\cap J=\varnothing$, such that the second and third corner supports are $R=\{p,q\}\cup J$ and $R'=\{p,q\}\cup(J\setminus\{e\})\cup F$. Their cubes lie in the original upper section and meet in the $e=1$ facet of the first. Thus the upper section has an exclusive shattered support of size at least three containing $p,q$, while the lower section has an exclusive support of size at least three avoiding $p,q,r$.

Status: Established

Proof:

The switch reduction chooses $z$ first. Minimality and the pointed square-pair argument exclude every next corner except $v$. Its corner cube contains the upper $\{p,q\}$-square and has support $\{p,q\}\cup J$; the four-corner path theorem forces $J$ to be nonempty. After $z,v$ are removed, local cube defects exclude every third corner inside the switch cube. Commutation forces the next corner to neighbor $v$, hence it removes one $e\in J$ and may acquire only new directions $F$ disjoint from $J$. If $F$ were empty, the three deletions could be reordered to contradict minimality. The two cubes and their common facet therefore have the asserted form.
