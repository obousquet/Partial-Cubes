Inherited Repair Fibres Admit Complete Obstruction Descent

Name: Inherited Repair Fibres Admit Complete Obstruction Descent

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes
  • Cornerless Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Concept Deletion
  • Coordinate Projection
  • Full Reduction

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

Let $G\subseteq Q_F$ be an irredundantly embedded cornerless ample forbidden pc-minor, and suppose $S=G\cup\{c\}$ is ample and nonpeelable. Let $\mathcal A$ be compatible resolving tips of $G$ whose acquired supports, together with the acquired support of $c$, are pairwise distinct. Assign the tips rows $R_a$ forming a partition of the signed facets of a nonempty layer cube $Q_E$, set $Y=(G\times Q_E)\cup\bigcup_{a\in\mathcal A}(\{a\}\times R_a)$, and put $X=Y\cup(\{c\}\times Q_E)$. Then $X$ is an ample forbidden pc-minor and admits $2^{|E|}$ obstruction-preserving corner deletions that remove exactly the full fibre $\{c\}\times Q_E$ and end at $Y$. Thus $X$ is not corner-descent terminal, although reduction along $E$ recovers the cornered seed $S$.

Status: Established

Proof:

The signed-facet partition theorem makes $Y$ a terminal ample forbidden pc-minor. Choose a corner peeling of $Q_E$, with ample suffixes $T_0=Q_E,T_1,\ldots,T_m=\varnothing$. Compatible-repair union and the replicated-layer theorem make every $Y_i=Y\cup(\{c\}\times T_i)$ ample. Its strong reduction along $E$ is $S$ when $i=0$ and $G$ thereafter, so every $Y_i$ is nonpeelable. Traversed from $Y_m$ to $Y_0$, each step is an ample one-concept extension in the same irredundant ambient cube and remains nonpeelable; the one-concept atlas therefore makes every intermediate family a forbidden pc-minor. Reversing the sequence gives the required corner deletions. Since every $R_a$ is proper, $X^E=S$, and compatible-repair union preserves the distinct acquired supports, so the marked presentation is exactly the recovered-layer presentation claimed.

Comments:

For the certified Hall witness with one layer coordinate, the two fibre copies of the added seed corner delete successively from the 602-concept family and reach the terminal 600-concept two-repair lift. The general theorem supplies the full $2^{|E|}$ descent for arbitrary layer dimension.
