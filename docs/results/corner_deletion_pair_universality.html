Corner-Deletion Pair Universality in Reduced Forbidden Classes

Name: Corner-Deletion Pair Universality in Reduced Forbidden Classes

Classes:

  • Ample Classes
  • Dismantlable Ample Classes

Operations:

  • Coordinate Conditioning
  • Concept Deletion
  • Full Reduction
  • Partial-Cube Minor

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

Let $K$ be a nonempty ample family with corner $c$, and suppose both $K$ and $A=K\setminus\{c\}$ are nonempty and dismantlable ample. There are explicit ample forbidden pc-minors $X$ and $X'=X\setminus\{v\}$ such that $v$ is a corner of $X$ and one common coordinate-face restriction recovers $K$ from $X$ and $A$ from $X'$. Both parents have minimum graph degree at least four, pairwise distinct signed minimal-nonface columns up to sign, and complete crossing graph. If $K$ has $n$ active coordinates, then $|X|=301\cdot2^{n+4}+2|K|+1$, $|X'|=|X|-1$, and both parents have isometric dimension $n+16$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Use the reduced-base universality construction with its Hall three-repair seed. Replace the occurrence row $L=(K\times Q_{\{w\}})\vee_0Q_{\{p,q\}}$ by $L'=L\setminus\{(c,1_w,0_p,0_q)\}$. The deleted row vertex is a corner whose cube is the product of the corner cube of $c$ with the $w$-edge. The modified row equals $((K\times\{0_w\})\cup(A\times\{1_w\}))\vee_0Q_{\{p,q\}}$ and peels by clearing the three nonzero square vertices, then the upper copy of $A$, then the lower copy of $K$. The other two rows still cover both facets in every layer direction, so the common-seed criterion makes both parents forbidden. The corresponding parent vertex is a corner and its deletion gives exactly $X'$. Fixing the seed coordinates, setting $w=1$, and setting $p=q=z=0$ recovers $K$ and $A$. The intact seed product and the same separating clauses as in reduced-base universality prove the degree, crossing, and signed-column properties; counting gives the formulas.

Comments:

Applying the construction to the certified 289-concept all-sinks family and its 288-concept corner deletion gives parents of orders 19,726,915 and 19,726,914 in dimension 28. The certificate explicitly checks the 581/580 occurrence rows, their marked corner deletion, and recovery of the 289/288 faces; the two large parents and their forbiddenness are certified symbolically and are not enumerated. This result does not claim that every proper minor of either parent has every sink attainable.
