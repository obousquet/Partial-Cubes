Passive Shores Absorb into Ample Gates and Lower Blocker Rank

Name: Passive Shores Absorb into Ample Gates and Lower Blocker Rank

Classes:

  • Ample Classes

Result Type: Construction

Statement:

For every nonempty passive shore $S_z$ in the small terminal normal form, the gate $G_z=L\cup S_z$ is ample. There is a saturated ample chain from $L$ to $B$ that adds every member of $S_z$ before every member of $D\setminus S_z$. If $d\notin S_z$ has a peripheral blocker cell $C$ for the same $z$, with active support $W$ and duplicated endpoint $c=d^{\oplus W}$, then $C\cap G_z=\{c\}$ and $(G_z\cup\{d\})\cap Q_W(d)=\{d,c\}$. Thus the blocker descends to the active face when $|W|\ge2$ and disappears as a named blocker when $|W|=1$.

Status: Established

Proof:

Relative peeling of $L\cap M_z\subsetneq B\cap M_z$ orders the shore. Repeatedly applying the expansion-separator duplication lemma lifts each prefix to an ample family over $L$, ending at $G_z$. A second relative peeling appends the complement shore and gives the two-block saturated chain. The peripheral cell lies in $D$, meets $S_z$ only at $c$, and is otherwise disjoint from $G_z$; restricting $G_z\cup\{d\}$ to its active face therefore leaves exactly the antipodal pair $\{d,c\}$. Antipodal localization proves nonampleness in rank at least two, while rank one is the full edge.
