The Terminal Two-Connected Matching Row Closes

Name: The Terminal Two-Connected Matching Row Closes

Classes:

  • Cornerless Ample Classes

Result Type: Implication

Statement:

Let a cornerless ample two-layer family have overlap $B$ with $|B|\le9$. Suppose both proper pairs $B\subsetneq A_b$ are terminal under one-concept addition and deletion, each wing $D_b=A_b\setminus B$ is two-connected, and $|D_b|\in\{10,11\}$. Then the two lower gate-holonomy cycles cannot both have excess at most two. Consequently, no pair of terminal two-connected wings of orders ten or eleven occurs in the below-forty two-wing reduction.

Status: Established

Proof:

The local profile theorems reduce all excess-at-most-two comparisons to three independent acquired pairs; the sole central-square alternative is excluded by shielding. Adding actual shore concepts and forced distance-one owners to each wing gives sets $W_0,W_1$. Of 2886 two-unit matching comparisons, 302 fail passive-distance or owner-consistency tests; among the remaining 2584, the varying-coordinate counts are 504 of type $(5,5)$, 108 of type $(6,5)$, and 1972 of type $(6,6)$. A two-connected wing with six active coordinates needs at least twelve concepts, excluding the latter two types. In the low-excess comparison, all positioned wings likewise force six coordinates except the $P_8$ equality pattern. The remaining five-cube audit has fourteen two-unit side states and six low-excess side states. For wing orders ten and eleven it finds respectively 504 and 3346 two-connected two-unit completions, of which 238 and 1330 have ample upper families, and 744 and 4176 two-connected $P_8$ completions, of which 198 and 1026 have ample upper families. Every ample upper family has both an addable exclusive concept and a deletable exclusive concept, so none is terminal.
