Six-Cycle

Name: Six-Cycle

Symbol:
\[C_6\]

Object Type: Graph

Definition:

The cycle on six vertices, equivalently $Q_3$ with one antipodal pair of vertices deleted.

Properties:

It is $Q_3^{--}$, the first antipodally punctured hypercube. It is a two-dimensional partial cube, but it is neither ample, a daisy cube, nor a median graph; every proper pc-minor is ample, daisy, and median. It is periphery-free. In the standard embedding $\{000,001,011,111,110,100\}$, conditioning the first coordinate to zero gives $P_3$ after removing the fixed coordinate.

Vertex-Deleted Three-Cube

Name: Vertex-Deleted Three-Cube

Symbol:
\[Q_3^-\]

Object Type: Graph

Definition:

The subgraph of $Q_3$ induced by $\{0,1\}^3\setminus\{111\}$; every choice of the deleted cube vertex is isomorphic to this one.

Properties:

It is a partial cube, a daisy cube, an ample and maximum class of VC dimension two, a spread ample class, and a convex-geometry ideal, but it is not a hypercube or median: the coordinatewise majority of $110,101,011$ is the missing vertex $111$. Its three maximal facet cubes are the squares supported by the three coordinate pairs, and its corners are $110,101,011$, the neighbours of the missing vertex. More generally, the corners of $Q_n\setminus\{x\}$ are exactly the neighbours of $x$. Every proper pc-minor is median. Deleting any displayed coordinate by projection gives the full square $Q_2$, and conditioning on the side opposite the missing vertex also gives $Q_2$; neither is a vertex-deleted cube $Q_n^-$ with $n\ge2$.

A Nonmaximum Conditioning of a Maximum Class

Name: A Nonmaximum Conditioning of a Maximum Class

Object Type: Construction

Definition:

Let $K=\{\varnothing,\{1\},\{2\},\{3\}\}$ and $M=K\cup\{A\cup\{z\}:A\in K\}\cup\{\{1,2\},\{1,3,z\},\{2,3,z\}\}$. Condition $M$ on $z=0$.

Properties:

$M$ has $11=1+4+\binom42$ members and VC dimension $2$, so it is maximum. Its $z=0$ section is $K\cup\{\{1,2\}\}$, which has VC dimension $2$ but only five members on three active coordinates, below the maximum size seven. Thus the section is ample but not maximum.

Antipodal Pair Family

Name: Antipodal Pair Family

Symbol:
\[A_n=\{0^n,1^n\}\]

Object Type: Family

Definition:

For each integer $n\geq1$, the two-word class consisting of the two antipodal vertices of $Q_n$.

Parameters:

Integer $n\geq1$.

Properties:

Its ordering width is $n-1$. Its optimal daisy completion adds $2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}+2^{\lceil n/2\rceil}-3$ concepts, attaining the sharp completion bound. For $n>1$ its one-inclusion graph is disconnected, so it is not a partial cube. For $n\ge2$ its one-inclusion graph has two isolated vertices, so this family also witnesses that conditioning-minimal non-ample classes need not be partial cubes.
