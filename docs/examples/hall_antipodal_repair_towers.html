Hall Antipodal Repair Towers

Name: Hall Antipodal Repair Towers

Object Type: Family

Definition:

Use Hall's 299-concept cornerless obstruction with repair tips $a=183$ and $b=315$, whose zero-based acquired supports are $\{2,3,6,7\}$ and $\{2,3,8,9\}$. On $r\ge1$ new layer coordinates, place $a$ on $Q_r\setminus\{0^r\}$ and $b$ on $Q_r\setminus\{1^r\}$ over the full Hall product core.

Parameters:

The tower in layer dimension $r$ has $301\cdot2^r-2$ concepts and isometric dimension $12+r$. For $r=1,2,3$ the orders are respectively 600, 1,202, and 2,406, in dimensions 13, 14, and 15.

Properties:

Every member is an ample forbidden pc-minor. Its two occurrence rows are oppositely punctured layer cubes, and normalized layer recovery returns Hall's seed, the two repair tips, and the antipodal pair. Every pair of distinct-support Hall repair tips gives an analogous tower.
