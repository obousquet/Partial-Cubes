Seventeen-State Nonlinear Source-Domain Class

Name: Seventeen-State Nonlinear Source-Domain Class

Symbol:
\[C_{17}\]

Object Type: Set Family

Definition:

On coordinates $0,\ldots,5$, encode a subset by the sum of its powers of two and take $C_{17}=\{7,15,30,31,35,39,45,47,50,51,55,57,58,59,61,62,63\}$. Its ten minimal missing patterns have all-negative supports $01,13,04,124,15,25,035$ and positive-root/negative-support pairs $(2;03),(3;24),(4;35)$.

Properties:

It is union closed, ample, and has VC dimension two. Writing its three positive rows as $x_2<\alpha x_0+a x_3$, $x_31$; the other ratios are arbitrary. Thus $\mathcal A(C_{17})\cong\mathbb R^{15}$ although its logarithmic ratio domain is not convex. Its source indicators are $(\eta_0,\eta_\infty)=(1,0)$.
