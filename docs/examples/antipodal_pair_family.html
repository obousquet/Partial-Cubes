Antipodal Pair Family

Name: Antipodal Pair Family

Symbol:
\[A_n=\{0^n,1^n\}\]

Object Type: Family

Definition:

For each integer $n\geq1$, the two-word class consisting of the two antipodal vertices of $Q_n$.

Parameters:

Integer $n\geq1$.

Properties:

Its ordering width is $n-1$. Its optimal daisy completion adds $2^{\lfloor n/2\rfloor}+2^{\lceil n/2\rceil}-3$ concepts, attaining the sharp completion bound. For $n>1$ its one-inclusion graph is disconnected, so it is not a partial cube. For $n\ge2$ it is pc-minimal non-ample in the ambient universe of all concept classes, while the partial-cube obstruction basis excludes it. In particular, $A_3$ is conditioning-minimal and projects to $A_2$.
