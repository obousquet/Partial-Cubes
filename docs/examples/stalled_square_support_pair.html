A Stalled Pair Excluded from Unique-Corner Square Completions

Name: A Stalled Pair Excluded from Unique-Corner Square Completions

Object Type: Set Family

Definition:

In six-coordinate binary integer notation, let $K=\{0,1,3,4,5,8,9,10,11,12,13,20,21,28,29,40,42,44,46,52,53,58,60,62\}$ and $A=K\cup\{16,17,32,33,34,35,36,38,41,43,48,49,50,54\}$.

Parameters:

$|K|=24$ and $|A|=38$; the shattered-support counts by size are $(1,6,15,2)$ for $K$ and $(1,6,15,16)$ for $A$.

Properties:

$A$ and $K$ are ample. Every corner of $A$ lies in $K$, so a relative peeling of $A$ to $K$ cannot start. The fourteen supports in $\operatorname{Sh}(A)\setminus\operatorname{Sh}(K)$ meet every four-support, and the support complement allowed by the opposite-section bound is exactly $\{7,19,38,50\}$, of size four. Therefore $(A,K)$ cannot occur as $(A,A\cap C)$ in an ample square presentation whose marked degree-two corner is unique.
