A Minimal Rooted Failure Whose Root Is Not a Corner

Name: A Minimal Rooted Failure Whose Root Is Not a Corner

Object Type: Construction

Definition:

Let $G$ be the fixed cornerless 291-concept family stored as base in damp_fixed_291_repair_creation.json, put $H=G\cup\{91\}$, and distinguish the root $a=29$. Let $B=H\setminus\{61,125,93\}$.

Parameters:

$H$ has 292 concepts and VC dimension four; $B$ has 289 concepts and VC dimension three. Their self-amalgams have orders 583 and 577 in dimension 24.

Properties:

$H$ is ample and peelable, its root is not a corner, it cannot peel to that root, and all 24 elementary root-preserving pc-minors peel to the root image. The same rooted minimality survives the displayed corner deletions down to $B$, whose only corner is its root. The self-amalgams $H\vee H$ and $B\vee B$ are ample cut-vertex pc-minimal obstructions, joined by six obstruction-preserving corner deletions.
