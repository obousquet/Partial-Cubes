The 85/73 Trapped Relative Interval

Name: The 85/73 Trapped Relative Interval

Object Type: Construction

Definition:

In the 85-concept smaller section $Q$ of the certified order-291 obstruction, let $K$ be its 59-concept overlap. Adjoin, in order, the eleven-bit words $2038,898,910,398,386,1024,1958,1926,1974,902,390,1942,1152,1408$ to obtain the 73-concept lower family $B$.

Parameters:

$Q\subseteq Q_{11}$ has order 85, $B$ has order 73, and $Q\setminus B$ has order 12. The acquired support masks are $14,22,26,28,518,536,1030,1048,1538,1540,1544,1552$.

Properties:

Both endpoints are dismantlable ample, every corner of $Q$ lies in $B$, and none of the 4094 proper nonempty subsets of the twelve residual concepts gives an ample intermediate. On active coordinates $\{1,2,3,4,9,10\}$, the complementary rank-three support complex is the octahedral two-sphere; its opposite pairs $\{1,2\},\{3,4\},\{9,10\}$ are the directions of three vertex-disjoint residual squares.
