Two-Connected Terminal Towers with a Cornered Cut-Vertex Seed

Name: Two-Connected Terminal Towers with a Cornered Cut-Vertex Seed

Object Type: Construction

Definition:

Start with the 1,158-concept corner-descent-terminal cut-vertex obstruction $S=Y\vee C$. In its $C$ coordinate block use resolving tips $a=128\cdot2^{14}$ and $b=290\cdot2^{14}$, whose acquired-support bitmasks are $390\cdot2^{14}$ and $480\cdot2^{14}$. For $r=|E|\ge1$, form the antipodally punctured two-row tower $X_E=(S\times Q_E)\cup(\{a\}\times(Q_E\setminus\{0^E\}))\cup(\{b\}\times(Q_E\setminus\{1^E\}))$.

Parameters:

$|X_E|=1160\cdot2^r-2$, $\operatorname{idim}(X_E)=26+r$, $\operatorname{VCdim}(X_E)=4+r$, its minimum degree is $r+3$, and it has exactly $2^{r+1}+2r$ corners. The first two members have orders 2,318 and 4,638 and respectively six and twelve corners.

Properties:

Every $X_E$ is a two-connected, corner-descent-terminal ample forbidden pc-minor and its own graph three-core. Its unique largest successful layer set is $E$, whose recovered seed is the same 1,158-concept terminal family with two corners and one cut vertex. The only thin coordinates are the $r$ layer coordinates. The fixed $r=1,2$ certificate replays 165 elementary-minor peelings, four resolving-extension peelings, and 18 terminal corner-deletion witnesses.
