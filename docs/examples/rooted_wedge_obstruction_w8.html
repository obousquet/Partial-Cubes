Rooted-Wedge Obstruction

Name: Rooted-Wedge Obstruction

Symbol:
\[\mathcal W_8\]

Object Type: Set Family

Definition:

The displayed class in $Q_8$ whose low-bit vertex-incidence mask is 0x100010001000100010001000000010001000000010001000000000000fdb1; bit $j$ records the word with integer encoding $j$.

Properties:

A 21-vertex VC-dimension-two obstruction to the cover-recursively peripheral class. It has sixteen proper gated two-covers and seven cubical face decompositions: one over a vertex, four over an edge, and two over a square.
