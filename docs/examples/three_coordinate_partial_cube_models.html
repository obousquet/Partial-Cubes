Three Coordinate Models Separating Rank and Cube Structure

Name: Three Coordinate Models Separating Rank and Cube Structure

Object Type: Construction

Definition:

On coordinates $[3]$, compare the chain $\{\varnothing,\{1\},\{1,2\},[3]\}$ with graph $P_4$, the antipodally punctured cube $2^{[3]}\setminus\{\varnothing,[3]\}$ with graph $C_6$, and the full cube $2^{[3]}$ with graph $Q_3$.

Properties:

All three have isometric dimension three, while their ranks are respectively one, two, and three. Their crossing/square graph pairs are $(\overline K_3,\overline K_3)$, $(K_3,\overline K_3)$, and $(K_3,K_3)$. Restricting $C_6$ at one coordinate gives $P_3$, whereas projecting that coordinate gives $Q_2$, explicitly separating restriction from contraction.
