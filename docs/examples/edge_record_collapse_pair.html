Opposite Fully-Active Edge Records with Identical Gluing Behavior

Name: Opposite Fully-Active Edge Records with Identical Gluing Behavior

Symbol:
\[X\equiv_D Y\]

Object Type: Construction

Definition:

Let $(R,0)$ be the rooted 289-vertex factor, put $u=0$, $v=2$, and use disjoint copies of its twelve coordinates. Define $X=R\cup\{2+2^{12}r:r\in R\}$ and $Y=\{x\mathbin{\oplus}2:x\in X\}$, where $\oplus$ is bitwise addition modulo two. Regard both as pieces over the common edge $D=\{u,v\}$.

Parameters:

$|X|=|Y|=577$; the fully active product representative $\Omega_D=D\times R$ has 578 vertices.

Properties:

Both pieces are ample and peelable. Their complete record sets are the disjoint singletons $\operatorname{Tr}_D(X)=\{((u,v),D)\}$ and $\operatorname{Tr}_D(Y)=\{((v,u),D)\}$, yet $X\equiv_DY\equiv_D\Omega_D$. Thus even inclusion-minimal records do not classify gluing behavior. Cartesian products with any nonempty peelable ample family give the same failure over cubical common sections of every positive dimension.
