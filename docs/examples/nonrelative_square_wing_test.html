Positive Nonrelative Square Wings Do Not Produce a Rooted Factor

Name: Positive Nonrelative Square Wings Do Not Produce a Rooted Factor

Object Type: Construction

Definition:

For the normalized 289-concept core $B$, let $P=B\cup\{4096,4098\}$ and $Q=\{0,2,4096,4098\}$. Use two copies of $P$ as the side sections of a degree-two presentation, identify their copies of $Q$, and place the four possible pointed and direction-ordered copies in all ten unordered pairs.

Parameters:

Each presentation has 587 concepts; there are ten pointed assemblies.

Properties:

$P$ is ample and peels to both $2$ and $4098$ but has no corner outside $Q$, so it cannot peel relatively to $Q$. All ten assemblies are ample, both overlaps and both side endpoints are positive, and neither side peels relatively to its overlap. Three assemblies peel to the singleton root; the other seven are not ordinarily peelable. None is a rooted factor. Thus positive sections alone do not recover the relative hypotheses of the two-overlap transfer theorem.
