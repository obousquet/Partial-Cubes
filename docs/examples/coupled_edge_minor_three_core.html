Coupled-Edge-Minor Three-Core of Order 1162

Name: Coupled-Edge-Minor Three-Core of Order 1162

Object Type: Construction

Definition:

Let $C\subseteq Q_{12}$ be the certified 289-concept core and $Y_0\subseteq Q_{14}$ the certified 870-concept rooted factor. In binary integer notation put $v=1024$, $Y=Y_0\cup\{v\}$, $C'=2^{14}C$, $K'=2^{14}\{0,2,4,6\}$, $B=C'\cup(v+K')$, and $X=Y\cup B$.

Parameters:

$|X|=1162$, isometric dimension $26$, VC dimension $4$; its canonical edge pieces have orders $871$ and $293$.

Properties:

$X$ is an ample two-connected pc-minimal obstruction to dismantlable ampleness, has minimum degree three, and is its own graph three-core. For the factor-exclusive restriction $\rho_0^0$, both resulting edge pieces have zero relative peelability to the common edge, yet their union is peelable because the changed piece peels to one endpoint and the unchanged piece has the complementary endpoint profile. Thus edge-minor compatibility cannot be replaced by positivity of the changed factor alone.
