Hall Terminal Obstruction without a Resolving Tip

Name: Hall Terminal Obstruction without a Resolving Tip

Object Type: Construction

Definition:

Use Hall's 299-concept cornerless seed and repair tips $a_0=183$, $a_+=315$, and $a_-=1836$. On layer coordinates $E=\{p,q,r\}$ assign owner blocks $\mathcal O_0=\{(p,0),(q,0)\}$, $\mathcal O_+=\{(q,1),(r,0)\}$, and $\mathcal O_-=\{(p,1),(r,1)\}$, and take each occurrence row to be the union of its owned facets.

Parameters:

The family has $8\cdot299+3\cdot6=2410$ concepts and isometric dimension 15.

Properties:

It is a corner-descent-terminal ample forbidden pc-minor. Every owner support is a proper subset of $E$, so it has no one-concept extension in the dismantlable ample class. This realizes zero resolving tips; the related order-1,203 construction realizes one, and an antipodally punctured two-row tower realizes two.
