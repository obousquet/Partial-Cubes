Union-Closed Coefficient-Sphere Family

Name: Union-Closed Coefficient-Sphere Family

Symbol:
\[U_m\]

Object Type: Family

Definition:

Apply the union-closed directed-graph construction to a directed $m$-cycle, using the two opposite arcs when $m=2$.

Parameters:

Integer $m\ge2$.

Properties:

The class has $3m+3$ coordinates, $30m+8$ concepts, VC dimension four, and coefficient space $S^{m-2}\times\mathbb R^{d_m-m+2}$, where $d_m=26m+\binom{3m}{2}$. It is union closed, realizable, dismantlable ample, and finite convex-treelike. Both reflected source indicators equal one. In particular $U_2$ has 68 concepts and two coefficient-space components $\mathbb R^{67}$, while $U_3$ has 98 concepts and coefficient space $S^1\times\mathbb R^{113}$.
