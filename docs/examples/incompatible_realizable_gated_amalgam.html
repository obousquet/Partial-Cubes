Incompatible Realizable Gated Amalgam

Name: Incompatible Realizable Gated Amalgam

Symbol:
\[X_{33}\]

Object Type: Set Family

Definition:

On coordinates $(a_1,a_2,b_1,b_-,i,j,b_+)$ in low-bit order, take the 33 words $\{0,1,2,3,16,17,18,19,20,24,25,28,32,33,34,35,36,37,40,42,44,48,52,56,60,80,82,84,96,97,100,112,116\}$. Its sections $A=X_{b_+=0}$ and $B=X_{b_-=0}$ each have 25 words and meet in the common 17-word section $D=X_{b_-=b_+=0}$.

Properties:

The union and both pieces are ample partial cubes. Each piece is gated in the union, is downward closed in its private coordinate, has an exact rational separation certificate of margin at least $1/240$, and has separator coefficient ratio zero. Their section-row cycle ratios are respectively $1/3$ and $3$, forcing disjoint extension spaces; hence the 33-word gated amalgam has separation margin zero and is not realizable ample. More exactly, opposite zero-entropy section circulations force its coefficient ratio to be one.
