Hall Iterated Common-Seed Cover with a Redundant Path Row

Name: Hall Iterated Common-Seed Cover with a Redundant Path Row

Object Type: Construction

Definition:

Use Hall's 299-concept cornerless seed with repair tips $a=183$, $b=315$, and $d=1836$. On three layer coordinates put $R_a=Q_3\setminus\{000\}$, $R_b=Q_3\setminus\{111\}$, and $R_d=\{000,100,110,111\}$. Lift the two resolving additions $(a,000)$ and $(b,111)$ as opposite owners on a new coordinate, and flatten the result back over Hall.

Parameters:

The inner cover has 2,410 concepts in dimension 15. Its outer lift has 4,822 concepts in dimension 16; deleting the eight occurrences of the redundant flattened path row reaches a terminal 4,814-concept antipodal tower.

Properties:

The inner family is an ample forbidden pc-minor with exactly two resolving additions and eight corners. Only the two endpoints of the path row give obstruction-preserving corner deletions, and neither predecessor has a successful layer set. The outer lift has the new coordinate as its unique successful layer set and recovers the inner family. Its eight verified corner deletions preserve forbiddenness and end at the terminal antipodal two-repair tower, demonstrating the iterated-cover theorem even when the inner rows are not a facet partition.
