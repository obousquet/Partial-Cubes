Complete Forbidden pc-Minor Basis for Daisy Cubes

Name: Complete Forbidden pc-Minor Basis for Daisy Cubes

Target Class: Daisy Cubes

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Minor Operation: Partial-Cube Minor

Obstruction Families:

  • Antipodally Punctured Hypercubes
  • Peripheral Daisy Obstructions

Basis Claim: Complete

Minimality: pc-minimal

Statement:

A finite partial cube is a daisy cube if and only if none of its pc-minors is isomorphic to an antipodally punctured hypercube $Q_n^{--}$ with $n\geq3$ or to a graph $O(P,R,A;\mathcal W)$ satisfying the peripheral normal-form hypotheses. Every listed graph is non-daisy and all of its proper pc-minors are daisy cubes.

Status: Established

Proof:

Daisy cubes are pc-minor-closed. A pc-minimal non-daisy graph either has no peripheral coordinate, in which case it is $Q_n^{--}$, or has a peripheral coordinate, in which case the coordinate normal-form theorem gives $O(P,R,A;\mathcal W)$. The two source theorems also prove that every generated graph is a partial cube, is not daisy, and has only daisy proper elementary pc-minors.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/daisy-cubes/sections/daisy_pc_minors_body.tex?#cor:complete-basis; PartialCubes:papers/survey/main.tex?#cor:daisy-obstruction-reduction

Comments:

The equivalent intrinsic description deletes a suitably maximal ambient-antipodal pair from a daisy cube with more than one root. The full dimension-free proof remains in the LaTeX paper.

Two-Obstruction PC-Minor Basis for Median Graphs

Name: Two-Obstruction PC-Minor Basis for Median Graphs

Target Class: Median Graphs

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Minor Operation: Partial-Cube Minor

Obstruction Families:

  • Vertex-Deleted Three-Cube
  • Six-Cycle Median Obstruction

Basis Claim: Complete

Minimality: pc-minimal

Statement:

A finite partial cube is median if and only if it has no pc-minor isomorphic to $Q_3^-$ or $C_6$. Both listed graphs are nonmedian and all of their proper pc-minors are median.

Status: Established

Proof:

The avoidance equivalence is the cited two-obstruction theorem. The graph $Q_3^-$ fails majority closure at the three neighbors of its missing vertex, while $C_6$ has triples with no common interval vertex. Their elementary proper projections, conditionings, and $\Theta$-class contractions are cubes, paths, or four-cycles, hence median; the same then holds for every further proper pc-minor.

Name: One-Obstruction PC-Minor Basis for Trees

Target Class: Trees

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Minor Operation: Partial-Cube Minor

Obstruction Families:

  • Four-Cycle Obstruction

Basis Claim: Complete

Minimality: pc-minimal

Statement:

A finite partial cube is a tree if and only if it has no pc-minor isomorphic to $C_4$; the obstruction is pc-minimal.

Status: Established

Proof:

A tree remains a tree under convex restriction and coordinate contraction. Conversely, a cyclic partial cube contains a convex even cycle, and contracting all but two of its coordinate classes yields $C_4$. Every proper pc-minor of $C_4$ is $K_1$ or $K_2$.

Three-Cube PC-Minor Basis for Two-Dimensional Partial Cubes

Name: Three-Cube PC-Minor Basis for Two-Dimensional Partial Cubes

Target Class: Two-Dimensional Partial Cubes

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Minor Operation: Partial-Cube Minor

Obstruction Families:

  • Three-Cube

Basis Claim: Complete

Minimality: pc-minimal

Statement:

A finite partial cube has VC dimension at most two if and only if it has no pc-minor isomorphic to $Q_3$. The obstruction $Q_3$ is pc-minimal outside the class.

Status: Established

Proof:

A shattered triple projects to the full cube $Q_3$, and a $Q_3$ pc-minor shatters three coordinates. A proper pc-minor of $Q_3$ cannot shatter all three coordinates: a proper restriction omits a required cube vertex, while a proper contraction deletes a coordinate. Hence every proper pc-minor has VC dimension at most two.

Known pc-Minimal Obstructions to Dismantlable Ampleness

Name: Known pc-Minimal Obstructions to Dismantlable Ampleness

Target Class: Dismantlable Ample Classes

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Minor Operation: Partial-Cube Minor

Obstruction Families:

  • Antipodally Punctured Hypercubes
  • Hall Maximum-Class Obstruction

Basis Claim: Partial

Minimality: pc-minimal

Statement:

Every $Q_n^{--}$ for $n\geq3$ and Hall's class $C_H$ is pc-minimal outside the dismantlable ample class. Moreover, the $Q_n^{--}$ are exactly all nonample members of the complete excluded basis. The remaining ample pc-minimal obstructions are not classified.

Status: Established

Proof:

Each $Q_n^{--}$ is nonample, while every elementary restriction is $Q_{n-1}^-$ and every elementary contraction is $Q_{n-1}$, both dismantlable ample. Any nonample pc-minimal failure contains some $Q_n^{--}$ by the ample-class obstruction theorem and therefore equals it. Hall's class has no corner, while the exact verifier supplies corner peelings for every elementary restriction and contraction; pc-minor closure then handles all proper minors.

Proof Source:

PartialCubes:papers/survey/main.tex?#prop:dismantlable-ample-obstructions

The complete ample obstruction family is open. The current exact size bound excludes additional ample failures on at most fourteen vertices.

Complete pc-Minor Basis for Convex-Treelike Partial Cubes

Name: Complete pc-Minor Basis for Convex-Treelike Partial Cubes

Target Class: Convex-Treelike Partial Cubes

Ambient Class: Partial Cubes

Minor Operation: Partial-Cube Minor

Obstruction Families:

  • Antipodally Punctured Hypercubes
  • Ample pc-Minimal Periphery-Free Partial Cubes

Basis Claim: Complete

Minimality: pc-minimal

Statement:

A finite partial cube belongs to $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{per}}$ if and only if it has no pc-minor in $\{Q_n^{--}:n\geq3\}\cup\mathcal O_{\mathrm{per}}^{\mathrm{amp}}$. These are precisely all pc-minimal nontrivial periphery-free partial cubes. The ample part is equivalently the collection of two-layer lifts whose layers and contraction belong to $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{per}}$ and whose all-retained state is the unique state preserving every oriented section defect.

Status: Established

Proof:

A graph lies outside $\mathcal R_{\mathrm{per}}$ exactly when it has a nontrivial periphery-free pc-minor; finite descent yields a pc-minimal one. If such an obstruction is nonample, the complete ample-class basis supplies a pc-minor $Q_n^{--}$, and minimality forces equality. Every remaining obstruction is ample.

Formal pc-Minor Basis for Spread Ample Classes

Name: Formal pc-Minor Basis for Spread Ample Classes

Target Class: Spread Ample Classes

Ambient Class: Ample Classes

Minor Operation: Partial-Cube Minor

Obstruction Families:

  • Minimal Non-Spread Ample Classes

Basis Claim: Complete

Minimality: pc-minimal

Statement:

A finite ample class is spread ample if and only if none of its pc-minors is isomorphic to a member of $\mathcal B_{\mathrm{spr}}$, the family of all pc-minor-minimal non-spread ample classes.

Status: Established

Proof:

Spread ampleness is inherited by pc-minors. If a finite ample class is not spread, repeatedly take a proper non-spread pc-minor; each step removes a coordinate, so the process terminates at a pc-minimal non-spread ample class.

Proof Source:

Comments:

Completeness is formal: this record does not assert that the obstruction family has a finite or explicit classification.
