Yang Dimension

Name: Yang Dimension

Symbol:
\[\operatorname{hd}\]
Definition:

For a finite nonempty concept class $\mathcal H$, the Yang dimension is the projective dimension of the Alexander-dual Yang ideal $I^*_{\Delta_{\mathcal H}}$ over the chosen coefficient field.

Status: Established

Codomain: Nonnegative integers

Parameters:

A coefficient field may be specified; the value can depend on its characteristic in general.

Normalization:

The singleton class on the empty domain has Yang dimension zero.

Category: Algebraic

Isomorphism Invariant: Yes

Comments:

This is distinct from the Yang depth defect already catalogued.

References:

yang2017homological; chen2019free
