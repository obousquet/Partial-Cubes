VC Dimension

Name: VC Dimension

Symbol:
\[\dim_{\mathrm{VC}}\]
Definition:

For a finite family $C\subseteq2^X$, $\dim_{\mathrm{VC}}(C)$ is the largest cardinality of a coordinate set $T\subseteq X$ whose trace $C|_T$ is all of $2^T$.

Status: Established

Codomain: Nonnegative integers

Normalization:

The empty coordinate set is shattered by every nonempty family, so every nonempty family has VC dimension at least zero.

Category: Combinatorial

Isomorphism Invariant: Yes

Comments:

For partial cubes this is also called rank; it is invariant under coordinate permutations and independent bit complements.

Ordering Width

Name: Ordering Width

Symbol:
\[\sigma\]
Definition:

For an ordering $\pi=(c_1,\ldots,c_m)$ of a nonempty finite binary class $C$, take at step $j$ the inclusion-minimal disagreement sets $\operatorname{diff}(c_j,c_i)$ with earlier concepts, assign the step the largest such cardinality minus one (and zero at the first step), and let $w(\pi)$ be the maximum step cost. Then $\sigma(C)=\min_\pi w(\pi)$.

Status: Established

Codomain: Nonnegative integers

Normalization:

The first step has cost zero; constant coordinates are ignored by the value.

Category: Combinatorial

Isomorphism Invariant: Yes

Comments:

The value is invariant under coordinate permutations, bit complements, and adding constant coordinates. Its zero set is the dismantlable ample classes, a class not yet promoted in the database.

Daisy Completion Defect

Name: Daisy Completion Defect

Symbol:
\[\delta_D\]
Definition:

For $C\subseteq\{0,1\}^n$ and a root $r$, let $D_r(C)=\bigcup_{c\in C}[r,c]$ in the ambient cube. Then $\delta_D(C)=\min_r(|D_r(C)|-|C|)$, the fewest concepts that must be added on the same coordinates to obtain a daisy cube.

Status: Established

Codomain: Nonnegative integers

Normalization:

The minimum ranges over every ambient cube vertex, whether or not it belongs to $C$.

Category: Combinatorial

Isomorphism Invariant: Yes

Comments:

It is nonincreasing under nonempty pc-minors and vanishes exactly on daisy cubes.

Edge Defect

Name: Edge Defect

Symbol:
\[\eta\]
Definition:

For a finite binary class $C$, let $n_{i,b}(C)$ count its concepts with bit $b$ in coordinate $i$, and let $e(C)$ count pairs at Hamming distance one. The edge defect is $\eta(C)=\sum_i\min\{n_{i,0}(C),n_{i,1}(C)\}-e(C)$.

Status: Established

Codomain: Nonnegative integers

Normalization:

Coordinates are binary; constant coordinates contribute zero.

Category: Combinatorial

Isomorphism Invariant: Yes

Comments:

Equivalently, this is the minimum over roots of the number of missing cube edges directed toward the root. It is nonincreasing under nonempty pc-minors and vanishes exactly on daisy cubes.

Horn-Renaming Defect

Name: Horn-Renaming Defect

Symbol:
\[H\]
Definition:

For a nonempty binary class $C\subseteq\{0,1\}^E$, let $\mathcal M(C)$ be its complete family of minimal missing partial patterns. For a coordinate reflection $v\in\{0,1\}^E$ and $p\in\mathcal M(C)$, put $t_v(p)=|\{i\in\operatorname{dom}(p):p_i\neq v_i\}|$. Then $H(C)=\min_v\sum_{p\in\mathcal M(C)}\binom{t_v(p)}2$.

Status: Established

Codomain: Nonnegative integers

Normalization:

Repeated canonical 2-CNF clauses are counted with multiplicity. The empty missing-pattern family has value zero, including the zero-coordinate cube.

Category: Combinatorial

Isomorphism Invariant: Yes

Comments:

The zero test is classical renamable Horn recognition. Positive values depend on the complete minimal-missing-pattern presentation and measure its minimum number of violated pair clauses.

Separation Margin

Name: Separation Margin

Symbol:
\[\mu\]
Definition:

For nonempty $C\subseteq\{0,1\}^n$, let $\mathcal M(C)$ be its minimal missing partial patterns. A $t$-certificate chooses $x^c\in\mathbb R^n$ for each $c\in C$ and coefficients $a_{p,i}\ge0$ summing to one on each $\operatorname{supp}p$, subject to $t\le(2c_i-1)x_i^c\le1$ and $\sum_i a_{p,i}\epsilon_{p,i}x_i^c\le-t$ for every $p,c$. Then $\mu(C)$ is the largest feasible $t\in[0,1]$.

Status: Established

Codomain: Real interval $[0,1]$

Normalization:

Separator rows have $\ell_1$ norm one, witnesses have $\ell_\infty$ norm at most one, and the full cube, including the zero-dimensional cube, has margin one.

Category: Geometric

Isomorphism Invariant: Yes

Comments:

Defined for every nonempty binary class. Positivity is exactly affine-convex realizability plus ampleness; no class of all binary set families has yet been promoted as a database endpoint.

Separator Coefficient Space

Name: Separator Coefficient Space

Symbol:
\[\mathcal A(C)\]
Definition:

For every minimal missing pattern $p$ of a nonempty binary class $C$, choose a positive coefficient row $a_p$ on its support, normalized to sum one. The coefficient space consists of the row families for which every concept $c$ has a full-sign witness $x^c$ satisfying $\sum_i\epsilon_{p,i}a_{p,i}x_i^c<0$ for every $p$. It carries the Euclidean subspace topology inside the product of open row simplices.

Status: Established

Codomain: Relatively open semialgebraic spaces, considered up to homeomorphism or homotopy equivalence as stated

Normalization:

Every row is positive and has coefficient sum one. A full cube has the one-point empty coefficient product; the coefficient space itself may be empty.

Category: Topological

Isomorphism Invariant: No

Comments:

Invariant under coordinate permutations and independent bit flips. It depends on the positioned binary-class structure, so no claim of invariance under arbitrary abstract graph isomorphism is made. Its nonemptiness is equivalent to realizable ampleness.

Separator Coefficient Ratio

Name: Separator Coefficient Ratio

Symbol:
\[\tau\]
Definition:

For positive separator coefficients $a_{p,i}$ and positive witness magnitudes $z_{c,i}$, take for every concept and minimal missing pattern the ratio of the weighted terms where the concept agrees with the missing pattern to the weighted terms where it disagrees. Let $R_C(a,z)$ be the maximum of these ratios, $T_C(a)=\inf_{z>0}R_C(a,z)$, and $\tau(C)=\inf_{a>0}T_C(a)$.

Status: Established

Codomain: Real interval $[0,\max(0,|E|-1)]$

Normalization:

Coefficient rows may be normalized to sum one because row and witness rescaling leave every ratio unchanged. Empty numerators and the empty maximum are zero.

Category: Geometric

Isomorphism Invariant: No

Comments:

Defined for every nonempty positioned binary class. The infima need not be attained. The threshold $\tau<1$ characterizes realizable ampleness; $\tau=0$ has a strict tropical term-dominance characterization. Whenever a union-closed orientation exists, $\tau(C)^2=\delta_{\mathrm{sp}}(C)$.
