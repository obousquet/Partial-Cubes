Shattered Complex

Name: Shattered Complex

Symbol:
\[\operatorname{Sh}(C)\]
Definition:

For a set family $C\subseteq\{0,1\}^F$, the shattered complex is the collection of coordinate sets $I\subseteq F$ for which every binary pattern on $I$ occurs as the restriction of some member of $C$. It is a simplicial complex because every subset of a shattered set is shattered.

Status: Established

Codomain: Finite simplicial complexes on the active coordinate set

Parameters:

A displayed hypercube embedding, considered up to coordinate permutation and coordinate sign changes.

Normalization:

Inactive coordinates may be retained in the ground set but occur only as isolated vertices outside the complex; comparisons in this database normally use active coordinates.

Category: Combinatorial

Isomorphism Invariant: Yes

Comments:

For an ample family, the Sandwich equality gives |C|=|Sh(C)|. Its one-skeleton is the crossing graph of the associated partial cube, while higher faces retain strictly more cubical information.
