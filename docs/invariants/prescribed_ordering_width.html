Prescribed-Subset Ordering Width

Name: Prescribed-Subset Ordering Width

Symbol:
\[\sigma(C;I)\]
Definition:

For a nonempty subset $I\subseteq C$, minimize the ordinary ordering width over all orders of $C$ whose first $|I|$ concepts are exactly the members of $I$; their internal ordering is also optimized and its cost is included.

Status: Established

Codomain: Nonnegative integers

Parameters:

A finite nonempty binary class $C$ and nonempty prescribed initial subset $I\subseteq C$.

Normalization:

No order within $I$ is prescribed; $\sigma(C;C)=\sigma(C)$ and $\sigma(C;I)\geq\max\{\sigma(C),\sigma(I)\}$.

Category: Combinatorial

Isomorphism Invariant: Yes
