Coordinate Conditioning

Name: Coordinate Conditioning

Symbol:
\[H_x^\epsilon\]

Arity: 1

Definition:

For H ⊆ 2^X, x ∈ X, and ε ∈ {0,1}, fix the membership value of x and then remove x: H_x^ε = {h ∖ {x} : h ∈ H and 1_{x∈h}=ε} ⊆ 2^{X ∖ {x}}.

Category: Minor

Comments:

In the one-inclusion graph this is restriction to one coordinate semicube. Iterated nonempty conditionings give cube-face sections; arbitrary convex restrictions may fix several semicubes in succession.
