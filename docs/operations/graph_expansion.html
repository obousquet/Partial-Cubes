Graph Expansion

Name: Graph Expansion

Symbol:
\[\operatorname{Exp}\]

Arity: 3

Definition:

Let $G_1,G_2$ be isometric induced subgraphs covering a connected graph $G$, with no edge joining $G_1\setminus G_2$ to $G_2\setminus G_1$. Replace each vertex of $G_1\cap G_2$ by two adjacent copies, retain one copy of $G_1$ and one copy of $G_2$, and join corresponding overlap copies. The result is the expansion of $G$ with cover $(G_1,G_2)$.

Category: Expansion

Comments:

Convex, isometric, connected, and peripheral expansions impose additional conditions on the overlap or cover. These adjectives do not by themselves have interchangeable preservation theorems.
