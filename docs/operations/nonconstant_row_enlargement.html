Nonconstant-Row Enlargement

Name: Nonconstant-Row Enlargement

Symbol:
\[V(H,D)\]

Arity: 2

Definition:

For $H\subseteq Q_{F\cup\{z\}}$ with sections $B_0,B_1$ and contraction $C=B_0\cup B_1$, and for $D\supseteq C$ on $F$, replace $z$ by fresh coordinates $(e,f)$ and use the four rows $(B_0,D,C,B_1)$ indexed by $00,01,10,11$.

Category: Expansion

Comments:

The ordinary binary coordinate-block replacement is the case $D=C$. Marking and orienting $(e,f)$ makes $H$ and $D$ uniquely recoverable.
