Coordinate Fiber Product

Name: Coordinate Fiber Product

Symbol:
\[\times_{\{0,1\}^X}\]

Arity: 2

Definition:

For $C\subseteq\{0,1\}^{X\sqcup Y}$ and $D\subseteq\{0,1\}^{X\sqcup Z}$ with pairwise disjoint $X,Y,Z$, their coordinate fiber product is $C\times_XD=\{(x,y,z):(x,y)\in C,\ (x,z)\in D\}$. Equivalently, duplicate the shared coordinates $X$ in the two factors and retain exactly the pairs whose two copies agree.

Category: Product

Comments:

The output may be empty. For $|X|=1$ this is the one-coordinate product. For larger shared blocks, ampleness can fail even for maximum inputs; coordinate-tree projections and monotone path fibers provide positive special cases.
