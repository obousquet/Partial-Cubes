Common-Section Gluing

Name: Common-Section Gluing

Symbol:
\[\bigcup_D\]

Arity: 2

Definition:

Let $D\subseteq\{0,1\}^E$ be nonempty and let $F_1,F_2$ be disjoint private coordinate sets. Pieces $A_i\subseteq\{0,1\}^{E\sqcup F_i}$ satisfy $D\times\{0_{F_i}\}\subseteq A_i$ and $\pi_E(A_i)=D$. Embed each piece on $E\sqcup F_1\sqcup F_2$ by setting the other private block to zero, and take their union.

Category: Gluing

Comments:

The paper uses the simultaneous finite-arity version with pairwise disjoint private blocks. Distinct pieces meet exactly in the common coordinate section $D$; the projection requirement is essential for the invariant laws.
