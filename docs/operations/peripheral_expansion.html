Peripheral Expansion

Name: Peripheral Expansion

Symbol:
\[\operatorname{PerExp}\]

Arity: 2

Definition:

Given an isometric subgraph or subfamily $S\subseteq B$, form $P=(B\times\{0\})\cup(S\times\{1\})$. Equivalently, this is an expansion whose two cover members are $B$ and $S$, so one cover member is the whole base.

Category: Expansion

Comments:

The site $S$ and the base $B$ are both object inputs. The peripheral condition is stronger than being an arbitrary partial-cube expansion.
