Partial-Cube Minor

Name: Partial-Cube Minor

Symbol:
\[\preceq_{\rm pc}\]

Arity: 1

Definition:

A pc-minor of a partial cube G is obtained by a finite sequence of restrictions to nonempty convex subgraphs and contractions of Θ-classes, where contracting a Θ-class simultaneously identifies the endpoints of all its edges and takes the underlying simple quotient graph.

Category: Minor

Comments:

The elementary coordinate operations are restriction to a semicube and contraction of a Θ-class. This notion is different from an ordinary graph minor.

References:

[KnauerMarc20] (Section 2.1)

Coordinate Projection

Name: Coordinate Projection

Symbol:
\[\pi_A\]

Arity: 1

Definition:

For H ⊆ 2^X and A ⊆ X, the projection deleting A is π_A(H) = {h ∖ A : h ∈ H} ⊆ 2^{X ∖ A}. For A={x}, it is the union H_x^0 ∪ H_x^1 of the two x-sections.

Category: Minor

Comments:

On the one-inclusion graph of a partial cube, deleting one coordinate contracts its entire Θ-class. This is ordinary coordinate projection, distinct from strong projection.

Coordinate Conditioning

Name: Coordinate Conditioning

Symbol:
\[H_x^\epsilon\]

Arity: 1

Definition:

For H ⊆ 2^X, x ∈ X, and ε ∈ {0,1}, fix the membership value of x and then remove x: H_x^ε = {h ∖ {x} : h ∈ H and 1_{x∈h}=ε} ⊆ 2^{X ∖ {x}}.

Category: Minor

Comments:

In the one-inclusion graph this is restriction to one coordinate semicube. Iterated nonempty conditionings give cube-face sections; arbitrary convex restrictions may fix several semicubes in succession.

Source:

Cartesian Product

Name: Cartesian Product

Symbol:
\[\square\]

Arity: 2

Definition:

The Cartesian product $G\square H$ has vertex set $V(G)\times V(H)$, with $(g,h)$ adjacent to $(g',h')$ exactly when either $g=g'$ and $hh'\in E(H)$, or $h=h'$ and $gg'\in E(G)$.

Category: Product

Comments:

For binary set families on disjoint coordinate sets this is their direct product.

Majority Closure

Name: Majority Closure

Symbol:
\[\operatorname{med}\]

Arity: 1

Definition:

For a finite binary class $C$, repeatedly adjoin the coordinatewise majority $\mu(a,b,c)$ of every triple until stable. The result $\operatorname{med}(C)$ is the least majority-closed superclass of $C$ in the same cube.

Category: Expansion

Comments:

Repeated inputs are allowed. For connected classes the closure is an isometric median superclass.

Name: Rooted Daisy Completion

Symbol:
\[D_r\]

Arity: 1

Definition:

For $C\subseteq\{0,1\}^n$ and an ambient root $r$, set $D_r(C)=\bigcup_{c\in C}[r,c]$, the union of ambient cube intervals from $r$ to the concepts of $C$.

Common-Section Gluing

Name: Common-Section Gluing

Symbol:
\[\bigcup_D\]

Arity: 2

Definition:

Let $D\subseteq\{0,1\}^E$ be nonempty and let $F_1,F_2$ be disjoint private coordinate sets. Pieces $A_i\subseteq\{0,1\}^{E\sqcup F_i}$ satisfy $D\times\{0_{F_i}\}\subseteq A_i$ and $\pi_E(A_i)=D$. Embed each piece on $E\sqcup F_1\sqcup F_2$ by setting the other private block to zero, and take their union.

Category: Gluing

Comments:

The paper uses the simultaneous finite-arity version with pairwise disjoint private blocks. Distinct pieces meet exactly in the common coordinate section $D$; the projection requirement is essential for the invariant laws.

Peripheral Expansion

Name: Peripheral Expansion

Symbol:
\[\operatorname{PerExp}\]

Arity: 2

Definition:

Given an isometric subgraph or subfamily $S\subseteq B$, form $P=(B\times\{0\})\cup(S\times\{1\})$. Equivalently, this is an expansion whose two cover members are $B$ and $S$, so one cover member is the whole base.

Category: Expansion

Comments:

The site $S$ and the base $B$ are both object inputs. The peripheral condition is stronger than being an arbitrary partial-cube expansion.

Disjunctive Product

Name: Disjunctive Product

Symbol:
\[C\vee H\]

Arity: 2

Definition:
