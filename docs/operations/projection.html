Coordinate Projection

Name: Coordinate Projection

Symbol:
\[\pi_A\]

Arity: 1

Definition:

For H ⊆ 2^X and A ⊆ X, the projection deleting A is π_A(H) = {h ∖ A : h ∈ H} ⊆ 2^{X ∖ A}. For A={x}, it is the union H_x^0 ∪ H_x^1 of the two x-sections.

Category: Minor

Comments:

On the one-inclusion graph of a partial cube, deleting one coordinate contracts its entire Θ-class. This is ordinary coordinate projection, distinct from strong projection.
