Cartesian Product

Name: Cartesian Product

Symbol:
\[\square\]

Arity: 2

Definition:

The Cartesian product $G\square H$ has vertex set $V(G)\times V(H)$, with $(g,h)$ adjacent to $(g',h')$ exactly when either $g=g'$ and $hh'\in E(H)$, or $h=h'$ and $gg'\in E(G)$.

Category: Product

Comments:

For binary set families on disjoint coordinate sets this is their direct product.
