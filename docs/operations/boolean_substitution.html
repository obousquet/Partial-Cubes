Boolean Substitution

Name: Boolean Substitution

Symbol:
\[C\circ_i T\]

Arity: 2

Definition:

For a nonempty binary class $C$ on $X\mathbin{\dot\cup}\{i\}$ with sections $C_0,C_1$ on $X$ and a test $T$ on a disjoint coordinate set $Y$, define $C\circ_iT=(C_0\times T)\cup(C_1\times T^{\mathrm c})$, where the complement is taken in $\{0,1\}^Y$. Membership in $T$ selects the zero section.

Category: Other

Comments:

The operation includes a distinguished outer coordinate. For an empty or full test, a nonempty output is the corresponding outer section times a full cube.
