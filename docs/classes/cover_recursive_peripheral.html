Convex-Treelike Partial Cubes

Name: Convex-Treelike Partial Cubes

Symbol:
\[\mathcal R_{\mathrm{per}}\]
Summary:

Finite partial cubes obtained recursively by peripheral expansions whose contraction base and duplicated isometric site both satisfy the same rule.

Primary Characterization: Cover-Recursive Peripheral Construction

Aliases:

  • cover-recursively peripheral partial cubes
  • peripheral-recursive partial cubes
  • Rper

Category: Expansion and Boundary

Ambient Scope: Partial Cubes

Parameterized: Unspecified

Projection Closure: Closed

PC-Minor Closure: Closed

Comments:

This is Polat’s finite convex-treelike class. The recursion has two children at every peripheral expansion: both the contraction base and the duplicated site must recur. It is therefore smaller than the standard sequential tree-like class.
