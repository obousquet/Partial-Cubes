Connected Conditioning-Minimal Non-Ample Classes

Name: Connected Conditioning-Minimal Non-Ample Classes

Symbol:
\[\operatorname{Obs}_c^{\mathrm{conn}}(\mathsf{Amp})\]
Summary:

Conditioning-minimal non-ample classes whose one-inclusion graphs are connected; equivalently, tope graphs of non-free simple uniform oriented matroids.

Primary Characterization: Connected Minimal Failure under Conditioning

Aliases:

  • non-free uniform-oriented-matroid tope graphs
  • connected minimal non-ample classes

Category: Sign Vector and COM

Ambient Scope: Partial Cubes

Parameterized: Unspecified

Projection Closure: Not Closed

Conditioning Closure: Not Closed

PC-Minor Closure: Not Closed

Comments:

Connectedness removes exactly the two-concept parallel rank-one exception from the full conditioning-minimal non-ample class. Every member has at least three concepts and an irredundant isometric hypercube embedding. The class is neither p-closed nor c-closed, and hence is not pc-closed: sufficiently small projections are cubes, while every nonempty proper conditioning is an ample UOM fiber. These full UOM tope sets are the nontrivial parents whose proper topal fibers belong to UOMFib; the parents are non-ample and therefore are not themselves UOMFib members.
