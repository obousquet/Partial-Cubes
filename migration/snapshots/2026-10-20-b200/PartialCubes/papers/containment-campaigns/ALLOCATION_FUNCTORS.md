# Elementary allocation functors: minors, arithmetic and markings

2026-10-07. Coordinator foundation note for the structural reset.
Status: complete elementary author arguments below; independent review pending.
No containment is resolved and no literature-wide novelty is claimed.

The useful conclusions are diagnostic as well as constructive:

1. conditioning and reduction work over every commutative semiring;
2. coordinate projection works over additively cancellative semirings, but
   the same formula fails over the Boolean semiring;
3. the ring-valued functor collapses to affine space, by the old owner transform;
4. every finite tree with n vertices has the same unmarked cancellative
   allocation functor, the normalized n-coordinate simplex;
5. distinguished edge observables recover the tree.

Thus an unmarked functor is not a faithful reconstruction programme.
The proposed object is a functor together with geometric readouts and
operations, not another bare equation space.

## 1. Objects, coefficients and the allocation functor

Let X be a finite labelled coordinate set and C a nonempty subset of
the Boolean cube Q_X. For v in C and S contained in X, Q(v,S) is the
positioned cube obtained by flipping any subset of S at v. Put

\[
 I(C)=\{(v,S):v\in C,\ Q(v,S)\subseteq C\}.
\]

Let B(C) be the set of distinct actual positioned cubes, including vertices.
For B in B(C), dir(B) is its direction set. Define the integral matrix

\[
 M_C(B;(v,S))=\mathbf1\{v\in B,\ \operatorname{dir}(B)\subseteq S\}.
\]

A direction set S is shattered if the projection C|S contains every
binary pattern; it is strongly shattered if an actual S-cube occurs.
C is ample when these families coincide. We use the existing sandwich
equality and minor-closure dictionary, with proofs in
[ample_structure.md, Sections 1–3](../minor-atlas/research/ample_structure.md).

A commutative semiring has commutative addition and multiplication,
identities 0 and 1, distributivity, and absorbing zero, but subtraction
is not required. Morphisms preserve both operations and both identities.
R is additively cancellative if a+c=b+c implies a=b.
Rings, the nonnegative integers, and nonnegative rational or real numbers
are examples. The Boolean semiring has 1+1=1 and is not cancellative.
We allow the zero semiring; it contributes no separation information.

For every commutative semiring R define

\[
 \mathfrak A_C(R)=\{\lambda\in R^{I(C)}:M_C\lambda=\mathbf1\}.
\]

Every semiring morphism acts entrywise, so this is a covariant functor
from coefficient semirings to sets. All actual columns and rows are retained.
For the empty class use the singleton containing its empty array.

Over real numbers these are signed allocations; over nonnegative reals
they are full positive allocations; over the nonnegative integers the
vertex equations force one unit column at each root. For ample C, the
last objects correspond to ordinary representations by the existing
native/Yang dictionary. This last identification is imported, not
proved by changing coefficient systems.

Write U_C for the polynomial semiring on symbols z_(v,S), modulo the
semiring congruence generated by the equations M_C z=1. A congruence,
not a ring ideal requiring subtraction, is intended. Assigning its
generators gives the elementary natural bijection

\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_{\rm semiring}(U_C,R)=\mathfrak A_C(R).
\]

This representability statement is only the universal property of a
presentation; by itself it is not an existence theorem.

For a semiring U, define a~b if a+c=b+c for some c in U. This is a
semiring congruence: reflexivity and symmetry are immediate, transitivity
uses the sum of two witnesses, addition adds the same term, and multiplication
multiplies a witness. The quotient U^can is cancellative. Indeed equality
of [a]+[d] and [b]+[d] supplies a witness for a~b. Every map to a
cancellative semiring identifies equivalent elements, so it factors
uniquely through U^can. Consequently U_C^can represents the restriction
of the allocation functor to cancellative coefficient semirings.

## 2. Elementary geometric transports

For a coordinate i, delete it from the ambient set and write C_0,C_1
for the two sections, H=C_0 intersect C_1, and D=C_0 union C_1.
Absent source coefficients are zero. For an actual child column (u,S):

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \lambda_b(u,S)&=\lambda((u,b),S)+\lambda((u,b),S\cup\{i\}),\\
 \lambda_{\rm red}(u,S)&=\lambda((u,0),S\cup\{i\})
                       +\lambda((u,1),S\cup\{i\}),\\
 \lambda_{\rm proj}(u,S)&=\lambda((u,0),S)+\lambda((u,1),S).
\end{aligned}
\]

The first is conditioning, the second is reduction to H, the third is
projection to D. Projection does NOT intersect every support with the
receiving ground set: it keeps only supports omitting i. Conditioning
instead aggregates after deleting the fixed coordinate from supports.

**Proposition 1.** Conditioning and reduction preserve full allocation
equations over every commutative semiring. Their outputs are actual.
For ample C, projection does so over every cancellative semiring.
All three maps are natural in the coefficients and use matrices with
nonnegative integer entries.

**Proof.** A conditioned cube row is precisely its horizontal source
row: source supports with and without i are both counted. Its retained
subcubes are actual. A reduction row is precisely the source row of
the prism B times Q_{i}; a positive coefficient can occur only on an
actual such prism. These proofs use only addition.

For projection we use the ample whole-cube cover property. Every actual
cube B in D is wholly in at least one section. To prove it, condition C
on the outside horizontal bits of B. Its projection realizes every
pattern on dir(B); strong shattering in this ample conditioning supplies
a horizontal copy of B. If B is in only one section, columns from the
other section cannot contribute to its projected row: actuality of such
a column would give another whole copy. Nor can a contributing column
contain i. Its projected budget is therefore the single horizontal
budget, one.

If B is in both sections, its prism is actual. Let a be its projected
row sum and p the sum of contributions containing i in both shores.
Adding the two horizontal source equations gives
a+p=1+1. The prism equation gives p=1. Cancellation gives a=1.
All projected columns are actual because they come from actual horizontal
columns. Entrywise coefficient maps commute with these finite sums. QED.

The same formulas iterate for deleting several coordinates. Conditioning
on disjoint fixed coordinates commutes; reductions in distinct coordinates
commute. Projection order independence follows directly: the result sums
the original columns whose supports omit every deleted coordinate.

**Counterexample 2.** Projection is not this natural transformation over
all semirings. On the full one-edge class, over the Boolean semiring put
unit mass on the edge column at BOTH endpoints and zero on both empty
columns. Every source row has Boolean sum one. Projecting to the point
by the displayed formula gives zero, violating its vertex budget.
The cancellation step is therefore substantive, not notation.

## 3. An initial category and its limitations

Define a category with objects labelled ample classes (including the
empty class). Arrows are generated by signed coordinate isomorphisms,
conditioning, reduction and projection. Record the induced nonnegative
integer transport matrix T_f. Two composable descriptions with the same
source, target and matrix are identified; composition multiplies matrices.
Equality is stable under composition, so this is a category.

For every cancellative semiring R, the preceding proposition makes
C -> A_C(R) a covariant set-valued functor. Coefficient changes and
geometric arrows commute. Equivalently each arrow C -> D gives a
contravariant semiring map U_D^can -> U_C^can by substituting the
corresponding finite sums of source variables. Validity follows by
evaluating the arrow on the universal point of U_C^can.

This is an INITIAL minor category, not a claimed final category of
polarizations. Most minor arrows change the shattering complex.
Within a fixed-complex family it currently has mainly symmetry arrows.
Standardization, lifts and complementary completion are not inserted as
positive arrows without proving their transport. A fixed-complex
comparison can instead use a common-minor diagram C -> E <- D and its
natural relation

\[
 \mathfrak A_C(R)\mathbin{\times}_{\mathfrak A_E(R)}\mathfrak A_D(R).
\]

This relation need not project surjectively to either side or guarantee
a positive glued parent. Ordinary Yoneda does not prove those assertions.

## 4. Ring coefficients forget almost everything

The following is the existing signed owner transform, extended explicitly
to arbitrary commutative rings. It works for any finite cubical complex K,
not just an ample vertex-induced one. Define its actual rooted columns
and cube rows exactly as above, using its declared cells.

Put m=|I(K)| and r=|B(K)|. At a vertex v its incident support sets form
a downset. Introduce owner coordinates

\[
 w_{v,T}=\sum_{S\supseteq T}\lambda_{v,S},\qquad
 \lambda_{v,S}=\sum_{T\supseteq S}(-1)^{|T\setminus S|}w_{v,T}.
\]

The two transforms are inverse over every ring: intervals between two
incident supports are complete Boolean intervals, so their alternating
sums cancel. No division is used.

**Proposition 3.** Restricted to commutative rings, A_K is naturally
isomorphic to R -> R^(m-r). Its representing ring is a polynomial ring
over the integers on m-r variables. In particular signed integral
allocations exist on every finite cubical complex.

**Proof.** In owner coordinates each cube B has its separate equation
sum_(v in B) w_(v,dir B)=1. These variable blocks are disjoint and exhaust
all m owner coordinates. Choose one corner of each cube and eliminate
its owner as one minus the sum of the other owners. The remaining m-r
coordinates are free. Owner inversion reconstructs every allocation,
naturally in every ring. Taking free coordinates zero gives an integral
signed allocation. Empty complexes use a zero-variable polynomial ring.
QED.

Thus passing to a ring-valued affine scheme without its distinguished
positive/marked data cannot classify realizations: the abstract functor
remembers only this dimension. The geometric markings, not homological
exactness alone, are doing essential work.

For ample realizations this collapse has a stronger fixed-family form.
The existing fine cube-count law gives, for Sigma=Sh(C),
c_S=|C^S|=|{U in Sigma:S contained in U}|. Therefore

\[
 r=\sum_{U\in\Sigma}2^{|U|},\qquad
 m=\sum_{U\in\Sigma}3^{|U|},\qquad
 m-r=\sum_{U\in\Sigma}(3^{|U|}-2^{|U|}).
\]

Indeed r=sum_S c_S counts each cube once, whereas
m=sum_S 2^|S| c_S counts every corner-rooted cube. Interchange the finite
sums and use sum_(S contained in U) 1=2^|U| and
sum_(S contained in U) 2^|S|=3^|U|. The cube-count law itself is
[Theorem 3.1 of the structural source](../minor-atlas/research/ample_structure.md),
not a new result here. Consequently EVERY ample realization of a fixed
shattering complex has the SAME abstract ring-valued allocation functor.
Distinguished positive and geometric data are indispensable to this programme.

## 5. Trees: the positive functor also loses geometry

Let T be a nonempty distinct-edge tree, with vertex set V. Its actual
columns are empty supports and directed incident edges. For a directed
edge v -> w, let A_(v,w) be the component containing w after removing vw.
Write lambda_(v,vw) for the singleton-support coefficient in that edge's
labelled direction.

**Proposition 4.** Over every cancellative semiring R there is a natural
bijection

\[
 \mathfrak A_T(R)\simeq
 \Delta_V(R):=\{(p_v)\in R^V:\sum_v p_v=1\},
 \quad
 \lambda_{v,\varnothing}=p_v,\quad
 \lambda_{v,vw}=\sum_{q\in A_{v,w}}p_q.
\]

**Proof.** Given p, the two endpoint edge values sum to one because
the two components partition V. At v, its neighboring opposite
components together with v partition V, giving the vertex budget one.

Conversely sum all vertex equations of an allocation. Internal edge
equations give sum_v p_v+(|V|-1)1=|V|1; cancel the repeated unit to get
sum_v p_v=1. For A=A_(v,w), summing its vertex equations gives
sum_A p+lambda_(w,vw)+(|A|-1)1=|A|1.
Cancel the repeated unit, giving sum_A p+lambda_(w,vw)=1.
The edge equation also gives lambda_(v,vw)+lambda_(w,vw)=1.
Cancel its common term. This determines every column and proves uniqueness.
Finite sums make the bijection coefficient-natural. QED.

This is the old tree-root parametrization with cancellation replacing
subtraction; the categorical consequence below is an application.

The classes {000,100,010,001} and {000,100,110,111} have the same
shattering complex: empty set and three singletons. They have star and
path graphs. Their unmarked cancellative functors are both Delta_4,
despite not being geometrically isomorphic. In the initial minor category,
arrows cannot restore deleted coordinates, so isomorphisms on three
coordinates are signed coordinate isomorphisms; these preserve the
different degree sequences. The unmarked functor cannot be fully faithful.

## 6. Distinguished functions recover the tree

Keep the functor from Proposition 4 together with its paired edge
observables (lambda_(v,vw), lambda_(w,vw)), considered up to swapping
the two endpoints. The individual empty-support readouts may also be kept.

**Proposition 5.** This marked data recovers the abstract tree, and
recovers its edge directions if their names are retained.

**Proof.** The N-valued points are precisely the |V| unit vectors p,
so they supply the vertex set intrinsically. Evaluating an edge
observable at these points gives its component indicator, by Proposition 4.
Its paired observable gives the complementary component. Thus the marked
data supplies every edge split of V.

For distinct vertices a,b, count the marked splits separating them.
An edge separates a,b exactly when it lies on their unique tree path.
The count is therefore their tree distance. Pairs with count one are
exactly adjacent pairs, recovering the graph. A direction-labelled
split then recovers its corresponding edge label. QED.

Consequently geometry can survive as distinguished FUNCTIONS on a
positive arithmetic space even when the space itself is identical.
This is not a reconstruction theorem for all ample classes yet.

There is a direct fixed-shattering interpretation. Label every edge of
an arbitrary finite tree by a different coordinate, choose a root r,
and assign to v the set of labels on the path r to v. This gives an
injective Boolean embedding: the difference of two words is exactly the
label set of their connecting path. Thus its Hamming graph is the tree.
Each coordinate is strongly shattered by its edge. For two coordinates,
removing the corresponding edges gives three components, on each of
which the two bits are constant. At most three pair patterns occur,
so no pair or larger support is shattered. The embedded class is ample
with shattered complex consisting of the empty set and the edge singletons.
Changing the root is a coordinate bit flip.

Therefore this entire family with n labelled directions can be studied
as different cut-function markings on the SAME normalized (n+1)-coordinate
arithmetic simplex. The marks recover the labelled tree and hence the
embedded realization up to bit flips. This is a model for the family
viewpoint, not an assertion that every ample fixed-complex family has
one common arithmetic space.

## 7. Products and dependent points

For nonempty C,D on disjoint coordinate sets, define

\[
 (\lambda\boxtimes\mu)_{(v,w),S\cup T}
       =\lambda_{v,S}\mu_{w,T}.
\]

Every product cube is uniquely B times E. Distributivity factors its
budget as the product of the two unit budgets. This is a coefficient-natural,
associative and symmetric tensor construction over every semiring.
It gives a lax product structure, not an isomorphism between all point sets.
It commutes with factorwise elementary minor maps.

Conversely condition a product allocation at one fixed vertex of D
to obtain an allocation on C, and similarly obtain one on D. Therefore
for nonempty factors and every coefficient semiring,

\[
 \mathfrak A_{C\times D}(R)\ne\varnothing
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \mathfrak A_C(R),\mathfrak A_D(R)\ne\varnothing.
\]

Empty factors are excluded from the converse: their product is empty.

The tensor map is not surjective, even on integral points. On Q_2 use
bit masks 1,2 and give supports 0,1,3,2 at roots 0,1,2,3 respectively.
Every cube has one owner. Ownership of the direction-1 edge reverses
between the two direction-2 levels, so this cannot be a product of
two one-edge allocations. Dependence is already present at the elementary level.

## 8. Marks, exits and the algebraic order

The old face-exit record has a useful coefficient-independent form.
For a full face F with directions Y and (v,S) in that face, put

\[
 \nu_{v,S}=\sum_{T:T\cap Y=S}\lambda_{v,T},\qquad
 e_{v,S}=\sum_{T:T\cap Y=S,\ T\not\subseteq Y}\lambda_{v,T}.
\]

These are positive linear readouts, and nu_(v,S)=lambda_(v,S)+e_(v,S).
There is no need to define e by subtraction.

For complexes glued along exactly F with no additional cells, a MARKED
glue retains every outside coefficient and the prescribed common conditioned
face array nu (equivalently its labelled owners). Compatible records have
such a glue over a cancellative semiring exactly when each face coordinate
admits s with

\[
 s+e_1+e_2=\nu.
\]

This is the old real face-capacity criterion in the algebraic order
a <= b iff a+c=b for some c in the semiring. The slack s is unique
by cancellation. Place s at the face columns and retain all outside
columns. Conditioning the result to F gives s+e_1+e_2=nu, hence every
face cube has its unit nu budget. A cube outside F is in only one piece;
face columns and columns from the other piece cannot own it, since that
would place it in F. It retains its original unit budget. Conversely,
conditioning a marked glue to F directly gives the displayed equality.
If common labelled owners rather than the face coefficients are specified,
rootwise upper-zeta inversion is injective by downward induction and
additive cancellation, giving the same conclusion.

This is NOT a necessity claim for an unrestricted allocation on the union:
another completion may change the prescribed common face array.

An ambient union of vertex classes may create NEW cubes. That is not
this gluing theorem; they must be included and checked separately.
Global correlated response families cannot be replaced by their projections.

Retaining records at all labelled actual full faces determines each
allocation over a cancellative semiring: for any actual (v,S), choose
a face containing Q(v,S). The equality nu=lambda_(v,S)+e determines
that coefficient uniquely. This is injectivity on allocations with
known incidence data, not a fully faithful reconstruction of all objects.

## 9. What remains to develop

The subsequent [polarization-chart receiving packet](POLARIZATION_CHARTS.md)
adds a proposed geometric base of marked partial quotients and known shore
charts. It does not change this note's initial minor category or define its
allocation functor on nonreduced partial quotients. Geometric standardization
paths are already known; their positive/integral reverse lifting is not.

The initial category has reliable minor arrows and meaningful arithmetic
points. But fixed-shattering horizontal arrows, complement transfer and
marked maximum completion still require new constructions. They are not
formal consequences of representability or of Yoneda.

The next structural questions are:

- which smaller systems of marked observables determine useful geometry,
  generalizing the tree split reconstruction;
- which natural transformations respect these markings and geometric minors;
- how to introduce comparisons between polarizations without presuming
  positive witness transport;
- which duality or extension laws preserve positive and integral points;
- how higher-cell response data distinguish dependent product points and
  control genuinely global compatibility.

These questions concern the common framework; they do not select another
local containment subfamily or assert that the framework already proves existence.

analytic arguments or any containment. No negative ample separator is claimed.
